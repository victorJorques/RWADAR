WHAT HAPPENS IF BACKED ASSETS (JE) IS UNABLE TO PAY OUT?

The product is collateralized through the purchase of the Underlyings. When an investor buys the product, the Issuer uses the proceeds, minus the costs, to
finance the purchase of Underlyings. The value of the collateral at any point in time must correspond to the value of the product at that time, calculated
accordingly by the Issuer. The investors have a primary claim to the collateral allocated to the specific product.
In addition, the investors have a subordinated claim to the assets of the Issuer after all primary claims have been settled. The product is neither insured nor
guaranteed by any government or agency.
