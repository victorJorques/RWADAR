08​       Characteristics of the crypto-asset

​         LCXG is classified as a Utility Token under Article 3(1)(9) of Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 (MiCAR). ​
​         LCXG tokens solely provide digital access rights to unlock and retrieve a specific Tiamonds Gold NFT
​         previously deposited into the LCX Tokenization Bridge smart contract.

​         Each Tiamonds Gold NFT independently represents a direct ownership right to exactly one (1) ounce
​         of investment-grade physical gold. This physical gold is securely stored in insured, high-security vaults
​         in Liechtenstein and fully insured by Lloyd’s of London. It is important to clearly understand that LCXG
​         tokens themselves are NOT backed by gold, do NOT grant ownership rights to physical gold, and do
​         NOT confer a direct claim or redemption right to physical gold.

​         Instead, the LCXG token exclusively functions as a digital key or access mechanism. Holding LCXG
​         tokens enables holders to retrieve the Tiamonds Gold NFTs stored within the LCX Tokenization ​
​         Bridge. Once the NFT is successfully redeemed from the smart contract, the holder independently
​         holds the ownership right to the underlying gold represented by the NFT itself. All rights related to ​
​         redemption of the physical gold are thus exclusively derived from and managed under the separate
​         terms applicable to the Tiamonds Gold NFTs issued by Tiamonds AG.

​         Therefore, LCXG tokens neither stabilize their value against gold nor any other asset and should not
​         be viewed as a stablecoin or asset-referenced token. Any economic value or market pricing of LCXG
​         tokens is purely market-driven based on demand and supply dynamics, and may indirectly reflect ​
​         market perceptions about the NFTs they grant access to.

10​       Key information about the offer to the public or admission to trading

​         LCXG is a utility token as defined in Article 3(1)(9) of MiCAR. It provides access to a service operated
​         by LCX AG – namely, the ability to redeem a specific NFT (Tiamonds Gold NFT), which serves as a
​         digital representation of ownership in physical gold.

​         The NFT is redeemable through a third-party provider (Tiamonds AG). LCXG does not directly ​
​         reference or stabilize its value against any underlying assets, including gold, and is therefore not an
​         asset-referenced token (ART) under MiCAR.​

          H.​        PART H – INFORMATION ON THE UNDERLYING TECHNOLOGY
H.1​      Distributed ledger technology

​         LCXG is an ERC-20 token issued on the Ethereum blockchain, a public, permissionless distributed
​         ledger that enables secure, transparent, and immutable token issuance and transfer. Ethereum’s ​
​         smart contract infrastructure is used to manage the minting and redemption of LCXG tokens via the
​         LCX Tokenization Bridge, a proprietary smart contract system developed by LCX.

​         The smart contract architecture ensures that each LCXG token issued corresponds precisely to the
​         retrieval right of exactly one Tiamonds Gold NFT, each of which independently represents exactly one
​         (1) ounce of investment-grade physical gold held separately in custody.

​         All LCXG token movements — including issuance, redemption, and transfers — are recorded on the
​         Ethereum ledger, ensuring traceability, transparency, and compliance with MiCA’s technical integrity
​         and market integrity principles.

H.3​      Technology Used

​         LCXG operates as a smart contract-driven token on the Ethereum Layer 1 blockchain. The issuance
​         mechanism is implemented via the LCX Tokenization Bridge, which links the on-chain token with ​
​         off-chain physical gold through Tiamonds Gold NFTs.

​         Ethereum ensures transaction finality, global accessibility, and immutability of token operations. Users
​         can store LCXG in any ERC-20-compatible wallet.

​         While Ethereum Layer 1 is used for mainnet operations, LCX may explore Layer 2 scalability solutions
​         (e.g., Arbitrum, Optimism) for gas optimization and faster transaction throughput in future iterations.



H.4​      Consensus Mechanism
          Ethereum currently uses a Proof-of-Stake (PoS) consensus mechanism. Validators are selected to
​         confirm transactions and secure the network based on staked ETH.
          PoS significantly reduces energy consumption compared to Proof-of-Work and contributes to ​
​         Ethereum’s environmental efficiency. LCXG does not rely on a proprietary consensus mechanism but
​         benefits from Ethereum’s robust validator model.
          In accordance with MiCA, LCX provides transparency on the network’s consensus and ​ ​
​         ensures that any risks associated with transaction finality and validator behavior are mitigated by ​
​         smart contract architecture and custodial safeguards.

    I.​   PART I – INFORMATION ON RISKS
I.1​      Offer-Related Risks

​         LCXG tokens are not part of a fixed fundraising round, but are offered on a continuous basis through
​         LCX Exchange. While the token is fully backed by physical gold, market price fluctuations may lead
​         to temporary deviations from the underlying asset value, especially on secondary markets.

​         Although redemption is always available through the LCX Tokenization Bridge, liquidity constraints or
​         ​operational issues at the exchange level may cause temporary delays in trading or settlement. In the
​         event of rapid market demand or withdrawal surges, LCXG liquidity may be temporarily limited, ​
​         impacting pricing or order execution.



I.2​      Issuer-Related Risks
​         LCX AG is a regulated entity under the Liechtenstein Blockchain Act and has applied for MiCA ​
​         authorization. However, risks may still arise from:

​         ​•​        Operational dependencies on third-party custodians (e.g., Tiamonds AG for gold storage),

​         •​         Cybersecurity threats to internal systems or the trading platform,

​         •​      Regulatory changes affecting token classification, redemption rights, or cross-border ​
​         marketing.

​         Furthermore, past security events (e.g., the 2022 incident disclosed in Part A) highlight potential IT
​         infrastructure vulnerabilities, although corrective actions were taken and reserves were maintained.




I.3​      Crypto-Assets-Related Risks

​         While LCXG is fully collateralized, token price volatility may still occur on secondary markets due to
​         trading activity, investor sentiment, or macroeconomic conditions.

​         There is a risk that the perceived value of the token may temporarily deviate from the spot price of
​         gold due to arbitrage lag, demand fluctuations, or limited on-exchange liquidity.

​         Additionally, token holders must ensure secure custody of their private keys. If users lose access to
​         their wallets or credentials, LCX cannot restore access to LCXG tokens held in self-custody.

I.4​      Project Implementation-Related Risks

​         The LCX Tokenization Bridge smart contract and NFT infrastructure are critical components of the
​         LCXG issuance model. Any technical failure, upgrade delay, or exploit within the bridge or the NFT
​         verification process could affect minting, redemption, or on-chain auditability.

​         Delays in physical gold redemption or bottlenecks at Tiamonds AG’s redemption process could ​
​         temporarily impair user experience or undermine redemption confidence. Furthermore, scalability ​
​         challenges on Ethereum (e.g., high gas fees) may impact transaction efficiency for users interacting
​         with LCXG on-chain.

I.5​      Technology-Related Risks

​         LCXG operates on Ethereum, which is a robust and widely used blockchain, but still susceptible to
​         network congestion, smart contract bugs, or DLT-level disruptions.

​         The risk of previously unknown vulnerabilities cannot be ​fully eliminated. Malicious attacks, exploits,
​         or bugs in the LCX Tokenization Bridge could compromise issuance logic or token integrity if ​
​         unpatched.

​         Additionally, Ethereum gas fees may become prohibitively high during times of network congestion,
​         temporarily reducing user accessibility to redemption or transfers.



I.6​      Mitigation Measures

​         LCX employs multiple risk mitigation strategies including:

​         ​•​        Independent smart contract audits and internal code reviews for the LCX Tokenization Bridge;

​         •​         Redundant security infrastructure for LCX Exchange and user wallets;

​         •​         Full 1:1 collateralization model with physical gold held in insured, regulated vaults;

​         •​         Continuous on-chain auditability of token supply and reserves;

​         •​         AML/CFT compliance through full KYC onboarding for all LCX Exchange users;

​         •​         Segregation of customer funds in line with MiCA and TVTG requirements;

​         •​         Clear disclosure of fees, redemption procedures, and rights in this MiCA white paper;

​         •​         Ongoing monitoring of Ethereum Layer 2 solutions to address gas cost efficiency.
