Deploi Launches Direct Issuance Infrastructure for Private Credit on Polygon with Nasdaq CSD ISIN Allocations
Assetera and Deploi Partner to Bring Regulated Private Credit to European Investors
Partnerships
13 May 2026
Vienna, 13 May
Deploi, the institutional infrastructure layer for digital private credit, today announced the launch of its direct issuance framework on Polygon after receiving ISIN allocations from Nasdaq CSD for its inaugural UK Consumer Credit notes.
The first issuance, Series 2026/CON/001, enables regulated digital debt issuance for consumer credit assets with individual notes of up to EUR 5 million. Approximately EUR 100 million in additional issuance volume through Assetera is already lined up over the next six months.
Deploi’s infrastructure is designed to modernize the operational backbone of the private credit market by replacing manual fund structures with programmable issuance, settlement, and risk management infrastructure.“The legacy private credit model is operationally outdated, investors and lending partners want faster execution, better transparency, and more flexible structures. Deploi replaces fragmented intermediaries with scalable digital infrastructure purpose-built for modern private credit markets.”
Oskars Jepsis, Founder of Deploi 
The notes are issued and settled through Assetera, the EU-regulated DLT trading platform licensed under the MiFID II framework, enabling compliant access for European investors."Private credit has long been inaccessible to most European investors due to structural barriers — high minimums, opacity, and illiquidity. By providing the regulated infrastructure for Deploi's instruments, Assetera removes those barriers while maintaining full MiFID II compliance. This partnership demonstrates precisely what regulated DLT infrastructure makes possible: institutional-grade yield products, compliantly distributed at scale across Europe." 
Thomas Labenbacher, CEO of Assetera
Deploi’s issuance and registry infrastructure is anchored on EVM-compatible chains, with Polygon serving as the initial settlement layer ahead of planned expansion to Canton Network infrastructure.“Private credit is a massive market, but it still runs on outdated infrastructure. What Deploi is doing with direct issuance and bringing this onchain with Polygon is a clear step toward making these markets more efficient, transparent, and easier to access for institutional participants.” 
Marc Boiron, CEO at Polygon Labs
Early Market Traction
Deploi enters the market with growing institutional demand and active issuance momentum:
Initial live product focused on UK consumer credit
Notes issued in tranches up to EUR 5 million

Target net yields are expected to range between 6–18%, depending on underlying asset structures and market conditions

Strategic Expansion: Canton Network
Looking ahead, Deploi is extending its infrastructure to bridge the gap between decentralized finance and institutional capital.
Deploi is working with the Canton Foundation, the independent governing body of the Canton Network, to launch issuance and secondary market capabilities on Canton within the next 60 days. Canton is rapidly becoming a leading blockchain infrastructure for regulated financial markets, combining institutional-grade privacy with public blockchain interoperability.

About the Companies
About the Deploi
Deploi is a Nordics-based financial infrastructure provider enabling private credit originators to issue institutional-grade digital debt securities directly to global investors through regulated blockchain infrastructure.

About Assetera
​​Assetera is the first EU-regulated DLT trading platform for securities and real-world assets. Licensed by the Austrian FMA under the MiFID II framework, Assetera enables compliant distribution of tokenized financial products across Europe.
‍
About the Canton Foundation
The Canton Foundation is the independent governing body of the Canton Network, a privacy-enabled blockchain network built for regulated financial markets. The Foundation oversees network governance, ecosystem development, and participant onboarding.

About Polygon Labs
Polygon Labs develops blockchain infrastructure for scalable payments and financial markets. Its technology supports institutions, fintechs, and enterprises building onchain financial applications at global scale.
