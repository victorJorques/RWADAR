How do I buy tokenized stocks? 
To buy tokenized stocks:
From the Crypto.com App home screen, tap Buy or Market > Tokenized stocks to find a tokenized stock
From the tokenized stock's Detail page, tap Trade token > Buy
Select an order type, payment method, and enter an amount
Review the confirmation screen and tap Confirm to place the order
How do I sell tokenized stocks?
To sell tokenized stocks:
From the Crypto.com App home screen, tap Accounts and find the tokenized stock you want to sell under My assets
From the tokenized stock's Detail page, tap Trade token > Sell
Select a destination, enter an amount, and tap Confirm
What order types are supported for tokenized stocks?
One-Time order: Buy or sell at the current quoted price immediately.
Limit order: Buy or sell only when the market reaches your specified target price.
Recurring Buy order: Schedule repeat purchases on a set frequency.
Trading hours for tokenized stocks
Depending on liquidity conditions, it is possible that One-Time orders for some tokenized stocks are available outside of U.S. regular trading hours. In that case, you can only place a limit order.
U.S. regular trading hours are:
Monday through Friday, except U.S. market holidays
9:30 am. to 4:00 pm. ET
How to set up Recurring Buy for tokenized stocks
To set up Recurring Buy order:
From the Crypto.com App home screen, tap Buy or Market > Tokenized stocks to find a tokenized stock
From the tokenized stock's Detail page, tap Trade token > Buy
Select Recurring buy, then choose a start date and frequency
Review the details and tap Confirm
Scheduling rules:
Only U.S. trading days are available. Weekends and U.S. market holidays are not selectable in the calendar.
If a scheduled run falls on a U.S. trading holiday, that purchase automatically moves to the next U.S. trading day.
You cannot pick a past date, the current day after the regular U.S. session has ended, or a date more than two months ahead.
For monthly schedules, you can pick any day from 1 to 31. If that day does not exist in a given month (e.g., 31 in April), the purchase will run on the last U.S. trading day of that month.
Execution timing:
If you start today before the U.S. market opens, your first purchase will execute at around 10:00 am. ET. If you start after 10:00 am. ET and before the market closes, it will execute immediately. Otherwise, choose the next available trading day.
All subsequent purchases execute at around 10:00 am. ET on each scheduled U.S. trading day. Holiday deferral still applies.
Subject to jurisdictional availability. Availability depends on your country of residence, applicable law, account type and completion of all required verification checks.
