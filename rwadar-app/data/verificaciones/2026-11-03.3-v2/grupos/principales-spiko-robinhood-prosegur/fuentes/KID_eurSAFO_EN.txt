What is this product?
Type: This Product is an undertaking for collective investment in transferable securities (UCITS) governed by French law.
Term: The Product has a term of 99 years.
Objectives:
AMF classification: Not applicable.
The management objective of SPIKO AMUNDI OVERNIGHT SWAP FUND is to achieve, over a minimum investment horizon of 3 months, an annualised
net performance at least equal to the benchmark €STR + 0.50%. In order to achieve its management objective, the fund:
●    invests up to 120% of its assets in bonds, equities and money market instruments. This investment complies with the applicable UCITS rules and
     aims to diversify assets in order to limit issuer concentration risk;
●    enters into so-called "Total Return Swap" (TRS) contracts with one or more counterparties. Under these contracts, the performance of the bonds,
     equities and money market instruments held by the fund (including the dividends and coupons attached to them) is returned to the TRS
     counterparty (or the TRS counterparties), which pay(s) the fund a return equal to the benchmark plus a margin predetermined at the time the
     TRS is entered into. This margin allows the annualised performance to exceed the benchmark, in order to meet the fund’s management objective.
The Fund enters into these Total Return Swaps on its entire securities portfolio, with the possible exception of the portion invested in money market
UCITS. As a result, the volatility of the investment portfolio (including the TRS) is in line with the volatility of overnight rates. No unhedged exposure
to instruments with a maturity of more than 1 month is permitted. Given this strategy, the sub-fund aims to neutralise both the exposure to portfolio
securities and the currency risk between these assets and the currency of each share class, through an overall hedge using Total Return Swap
contracts. In the event of a counterparty default, the Fund could be exposed to the risk of the portfolio securities and/or to residual currency risk.
Please note that the currency risk between the share class currency and any other currency is not hedged.
The €STR benchmark (Euro Short Term Rate) expresses the overnight rate of the euro money market. It is calculated by the European Central Bank.
The index is used ex post as a performance comparison indicator. The management strategy is discretionary and is not constrained by the index.
Income allocation: Accumulation.
Target investors: The Product is aimed at any type of investor wishing to invest short-term cash. Capital is not guaranteed for investors, who must
be able to bear losses equal to the amount of their investment in the Product. The Product is not marketed to U.S. investors falling within the definition
of "U.S. Persons" as summarised in the Product’s prospectus.
Subscription and redemption operations: Subscription requests, expressed as an amount, and redemption requests, expressed in number of shares
or as an amount, are centralised by Spiko Finance by 3:00 p.m. (Paris time) at the latest. These requests are executed on the basis of the net asset
value established on the day of their centralisation. Subscriptions and redemptions relate to shares to be registered in the holder’s name on the DLT
registers offered. Centralisation of orders received after 3:00 p.m. (Paris time) is postponed to the following business day. The net asset value is
calculated on each day on which at least one of the two financial centres, Paris or New York, is open for a full trading session. At the investor’s option,
settlement of subscriptions and redemptions may be made by bank transfer or by transfer of electronic money tokens denominated in the currency
of the relevant share class. Investors wishing to use this settlement method should refer to the Product’s prospectus, which describes the
arrangements and risks involved.

Material risk(s) for the Product not taken into account in this indicator:
Counterparty risk: The fund uses temporary purchase and sale operations of securities and/or OTC derivatives, including Total Return Swap contracts.
These operations, concluded with a counterparty, expose the fund to the risk of default and/or non-performance of the swap contract by the
counterparty, which may have a material impact on the fund’s net asset value. This risk may not, where applicable, be offset by the financial collateral
received.
Additional risks: In the event of default and/or non-performance of the Total Return Swap contract by a derivative counterparty, market liquidity risk
may amplify variations in the product’s performance.
Risks related to the settlement of subscriptions and redemptions in electronic money tokens and to the holding of electronic money tokens by the
Product: investors are invited to refer to the risk section of the prospectus, which sets out the risks related to electronic money tokens.

protection against market fluctuations, you could lose all or part of your investment.

What happens if TWENTY FIRST CAPITAL is unable to pay out?
The Product is a co-ownership of financial instruments and deposits separate from the portfolio management company. In the event of the latter’s
default, the Product’s assets held by the depositary will not be affected. In the event of the depositary’s default, the risk of financial loss for the Product
is mitigated by the legal segregation of the depositary’s assets from those of the Product.
