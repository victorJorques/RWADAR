 Investment Mandate
 Spiko T-Bills Euro ​ 
 The Fund targets a net return aligned with the Euro Short-Term Rate (€STR). To achieve this objective, the Fund invests exclusively in Treasury Bills with maturities of less than 6 months issued by high credit rating ("investment grade") Eurozone member states, such as Germany and France. Besides, the average portfolio maturity cannot exceed 2 months. The Fund maintains a residual cash balance (up to 10% of assets) in EUR to facilitate deposit and withdrawal processing.
 BENCHMARK INFORMATION
 The Euro Short-Term Rate (€STR):
 is calculated and published by the European Central Bank 
 updates daily on business days
 can be referenced on the ECB's official website
