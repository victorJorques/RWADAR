 Onboarding Restrictions
 For regulatory reasons, we can onboard clients from all countries except the following ones:
 Africa :
 North Africa: Algeria, Libya
 West Africa: Burkina Faso, Côte d'Ivoire, Mali, Nigeria
 East Africa: Kenya, Mozambique, Uganda, Somalia, Sudan, South Sudan, Tanzania, Zimbabwe
 Central Africa: Angola, Cameroon, Central African Republic, Democratic Republic of the Congo
 Southern Africa: Namibia, South Africa
 Americas :
 North America: United States
 Caribbean: Anguilla, Antigua and Barbuda, British Virgin Islands, Cuba, Haiti, Turks and Caicos Islands, Trinidad and Tobago, U.S. Virgin Islands
 Central America: Belize, Nicaragua, Panama
 South America: Bolivia, Venezuela
 Asia :
 East Asia: North Korea
 Middle East: Iran, Iraq, Lebanon, Palestine, Syria, Yemen
 South Asia: Afghanistan, Nepal
 Southeast Asia: Myanmar, Vietnam, Lao PDR
 Europe :
 Belarus, Bulgaria, Monaco, Russia, Ukraine
 Oceania :
 Guam, Palau, American Samoa, Vanuatu
