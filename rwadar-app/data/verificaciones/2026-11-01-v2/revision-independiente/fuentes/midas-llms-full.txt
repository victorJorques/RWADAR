# About Midas

Midas is a platform for composable onchain investment products. It enables strategy managers to turn institutional strategies into tokens that offer investors full transparency, instant redemptions, and native composability across DeFi.

#### Midas Products

Midas products (mTokens) are financial instruments that are issued with clearly defined investor rights, onchain transparency, and robust operational controls. They are not stablecoins or DeFi vaults. Each mToken tracks an underlying reference portfolio managed by appointed strategy managers, such as BlackRock, Hyperithm and Edge Capital, with ongoing oversight of risk parameters and performance. Its value, expressed as a Net Asset Value (NAV),  fluctuates in line with the performance of this reference portfolio, which can increase or decrease in value.&#x20;

Midas products are designed around three principles:

Learn more about the [Open Liqudity Architecture](/liquidity-and-composability/open-liquidity-architecture) and [MSL](/liquidity-and-composability/midas-staked-liquidity-msl).

# Open Liquidity Architecture

Midas provides investors with access to highly liquid and composable onchain instment products. Bridging the gap between continuous onchain environments and legacy financial systems often exposes inherent settlement delays and capital drag.

To solve this, we have created an Open Liquidity Architecture to enhance liquidity where traditional inefficiencies emerge. This architecture ensures instantaneous, on-demand access, bridging these settlement gaps while maintaining maximum capital efficiency and seamless composability for your investments.

**Midas Open Liquidity Architecture**

<figure><img src="https://3475141875-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FMndxFHqGeA4nzBBeKDTV%2Fuploads%2FPsQBgqwKlskmDgKbPag7%2Fdocs-Atomic%20redemption%20via%20MSL%20(liquidity%20bridge).png?alt=media&amp;token=fbc0b16f-72e7-483f-b040-59e5889062b4" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### Choosing Your Redemption Method

When redeeming, you can choose between standard and instant redemption, corresponding to the two functions embedded in the smart contract:

* **Standard Redemption:** This option is fee-free and follows the natural settlement timeline of the underlying assets. Redemption requests accumulate and are  processed after the next price update, i.e. the next time the Net Asset Value (NAV) of the portfolio is determined in line with our standard processes (click [here](/transparency/pricing-of-the-mtoken) for more information). The scheduling of the next price update depends on the specific strategy and can span several days or weeks. For some strategies, the redemption request needs to be submitted within a specific window prior to the next price update in order to be processed in the next redemption cycle.
* **Instant Redemption:** This option provides on-demand liquidity, allowing you t skip the standard waiting period and settle your withdrawal immediately into a stablecoin like USDC. Because this feature draws on a dedicated liquidity pool, it is subject to available capacity and incurs a fixed instant redemption fee. The smart contract executes this in a single, immediate transaction: it checks the current NAV, burns your mTokens, and returns the stablecoins directly to your wallet.

> :warning: <sub>In order to protect tokenholders, Midas has standard emergency processes. We may pause deposit and redemption facilities in the event of, but not limited to, external security incidents (e.g., hacks in the broader market), external depeg events, external bad debt events, technical failures, internal security incidents, or extreme market volatility, that create contagion risks. We may take this action if current market conditions introduce any level of uncertainty regarding the validity of the latest mToken price. This precautionary measure can be enacted even when Midas has no direct exposure to the incident, acting proactively to ensure equitable treatment and protect the value of all tokenholders. We expect these situations to be limited to a few hours or days, and we will gradually re-establish deposit and redemption processing as soon as the broader market uncertainty clears.</sub>

#### Internal Liquidity Sleeve

For certain products, Midas employs an Internal Liquidity Sleeve as a first layer to process instant redemptions. In this structure, a small portion of the invested capital (typically up to 10% of the total value locked) is allocated to a highly liquid investment buffer, which can include, for example, direct holdings in USDC or mTBILL. Because the mTBILL token itself features atomic redemption, other tokens can leverage this to meet instant redemption requests. However, instant redemption capacity for mTBILL is also subject to availability, meaning it can constrain the instant redemption capacity of other mTokens if fully utilised.&#x20;

The assets held within the internal sleeve remain a component of the overall portfolio and are included in the Net Asset Value (NAV) assessment of the token and hence the APY calculation. As the yield generated by this liquid portion is generally lower than the main strategy's returns, it introduces a cash drag on the overall portfolio. &#x20;

Please note that this internal sleeve is tailored to specific strategies, and not all Midas tokens feature this allocation.

# Midas Staked Liquidity (MSL)

{% hint style="info" %}
To view how much instant liquidity each mToken has, please click [here](https://midas.app/liquidity#liquidity-profile).
{% endhint %}

To process instant daily redemptions and bridge the gap with traditional settlement times, portfolio managers have historically relied on an "internal liquidity sleeve" which holds a percentage of the portfolio in unproductive, idle cash. While this provides a basic liquidity buffer, it introduces a significant "cash drag" that dilutes the overall yield of the portfolio, penalising long-term investors by leaving capital uninvested.

#### MSL: Atomic Settlement Without Cash Drag

[Midas Staked Liquidity (MSL) operates as a dedicated liquidity infrastructure to facilitate an atomic settlement experience](#user-content-fn-1)[^1]. When a user requests an instant redemption, the portfolio manager automatically pulls USDC from the MSL pool to pay the user, net of a fixed instant redemption fee, while burning the redeemed Midas tokens.

This transfer of funds creates a liability towards MSL that is securely registered on-chain. Once the portfolio manager liquidates the underlying traditional assets, the portfolio repays its debt to MSL via a smart contract. This architecture ensures total auditability and transparency for all interacting parties without relying on complex secondary market trading.

**MSL Redemption Flow**

<figure><img src="https://3475141875-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FMndxFHqGeA4nzBBeKDTV%2Fuploads%2Fzt2uoA3O68XxRbhS6F8J%2FMSL_redemption_flow.png?alt=media&amp;token=c331b3e7-0666-4a27-8092-673cef68d7b2" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

#### MSL token: A Risk-Isolated Yield Opportunity for LPs

The MSL compartment functions as a pure liquidity provider and assumes no underlying investment risk regarding the portfolio's assets. [The loan provided via MSL has seniority over any other investor in the mToken](#user-content-fn-2)[^2]. Instead of bearing market exposure, MSL simply facilitates the immediate transfer of value during the brief settlement window, earning a predictable yield generated entirely from the fixed instant redemption fees.

As the demand for composable onchain investment products scales, the need for risk-isolated, high-velocity liquidity provision grows alongside it. Institutions or capital allocators interested in providing pure liquidity to capture these transactional fees without taking on market exposure are encouraged to contact our team to discuss participating in the MSL ecosystem.

[^1]: Subject to availability and capped at 10% of TVL

[^2]: The structural design of mSL provides deep principal protection against market volatility. Because the short-term loan extended by mSL is strictly senior to the equity of mToken holders, and the maximum liquidity facility is capped at 10% of the portfolio's Total Value Locked (TVL), mSL liquidity providers would only incur losses in the extreme event that the underlying portfolio's total asset value declines by more than 90% before the short-term loan is repaid.


# Composability

Composability is a core proposition of mTokens, allowing them to function seamlessly as high-quality collateral within the broader DeFi ecosystem. By bridging traditional assets with decentralized infrastructure, Midas enables native integration across various onchain strategies. This deep composability is fundamentally supported by two key pillars: frictionless transferability and instant liquidity.

## Permissionless Transferability&#x20;

Once minted, an mToken is fully permissionless. This guarantees the transferability required for DeFi composability, unlocking several key functionalities:

* **Seamless Movement:** They can be freely transferred between wallets without friction.
* **Market Trading:** They can be traded on both decentralised and centralised exchanges.
* **DeFi Integration:** They are natively compatible with smart contracts and can be seamlessly deposited as collateral in third-party protocols.

> :warning: <sub>Certain products operate on a whitelist basis, which restricts who can subscribe or redeem directly through Midas. This does not affect the transferability of the token itself.</sub>

## Instant Liquidity via MSL

For decentralised lending protocols, immediate settlement is a critical technical enabler of composability. Lending markets rely on automated liquidation bots to instantly buy and liquidate undercollateralised positions, which is necessary to maintain protocol solvency.

By supporting an on-demand liquidity pool, Midas Staked Liquidity (MSL) ensures that Midas tokens can be instantly converted to stablecoins in a capital-efficient structure. This liquidity is what ultimately makes mTokens eligible and highly effective as premium collateral across the ecosystem.

# Pricing of the mToken

The NAV (Net Asset Value) per mToken represents the current value of one mToken expressed in its base currency, which can include USD, BTC, ETH, or any other currency as stated in the pricing supplement of the specific product.&#x20;

At any point in time, the formula to price the mToken is as follows:

<p align="center"><span class="math">\text{mToken Price (NAV)} = \Large \frac{\text{Total Value of the Underlying Portfolio}}{\text{Number of mTokens outstanding}}</span></p>

<p align="center"></p>

> :warning: <sub>At launch, the mToken is typically initiated at a NAV of 1.0 unit of its base currency. However, this does not imply any form of pegging or flooring mechanism relative to that base currency. The mToken is an investment product that can increase or decrease in value, depending on the underlying performance of the reference portfolio.</sub>

#### The NAV Update Process

The NAV update process involves a detailed assessment and pricing of all assets held within the strategy. This process follows a regular cadence, which can range from daily to weekly or longer, depending on the specific product.&#x20;

Midas maintains a rigorous oversight structure by separating portfolio management from final pricing validation. While strategy managers provide the primary valuation data, Midas performs an internal review to cross-reference all figures against independent data sources and valuation approaches. Our team deducts fees accrued since the last price update, and applies rigorous sanity checks to ensure price integrity, for instance, verifying that the current APY aligns with historical and projected performance ranges.&#x20;

This multi-layered process mitigates conflicts of interest and ensures that portfolio pricing reflects objective market reality.

#### Onchain Verification

Once this independent assessment is complete, the [Oracle Architecture](/transparency/oracle-architecture) further enforces a tolerance check to prevent large deviations and protect investors. Each price update is initiated from a secured backend with additional safety checks, and reviewed by at least two trained co-signers from the Midas team. Finally, to provide maximum transparency, the [Midas Attestation Engine](/transparency/the-midas-attestation-engine) enables users to independently audit and verify NAV valuations at any time.

> :warning:<sub>The Midas Attestation Engine is currently being rolled out and may not be available for all products yet.</sub>&#x20;

# Oracle Architecture

The oracle system is the backbone of accurate, tamper-resistant pricing across all mToken operations. It ensures that the NAV used in any deposit or redemption reflects a verified, up-to-date valuation of the underlying portfolio.

The oracle architecture connects the offchain NAV calculation process to the onchain smart contracts through a secure, three-step pipeline:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}

#### NAV Verification & Price Update

Once the NAV is provided by the strategy manager, Midas verifies and validates it internally. This includes deducting fees accrued since the last price update and applying sanity checks to ensure price integrity. The update is initiated from a secured backend and reviewed by at least 2 trained co-signers from the Midas team. After this verification step, Midas pushes the corresponding price update onchain to the Oracle Feed contract.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}

#### The Oracle Feed&#x20;

The Oracle Feed receives the price update and stores it onchain, alongside its exact cryptographic timestamp for transparent record-keeping.&#x20;

As part of this process, the Oracle Feed performs a deviation check: it compares the newly submitted price against the previously recorded price, enforcing deviation thresholds to prevent erroneous data entry or anomalous price volatility. If a proposed update exceeds the min/max bounds, the price update fails. To ensure price integrity, deposits and redemptions may then be manually paused until the next valid price update.&#x20;

High-deviation updates can still be pushed onchain, but require a different process with a higher quorum for approval than routine updates.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}

#### The Datafeed Contract

The Datafeed serves as the authoritative smart contract consumed by the minter and redeemer smart contracts. It retrieves the raw price from the Oracle Feed and applies a final layer of protection by validating the "heartbeat" (data freshness) and enforcing price boundaries. Only once these secondary security checks are satisfied is the NAV authorised for use in deposit and redemption transactions.

{% tabs %}
{% tab title="Freshness" %}
The contract verifies the exact time the data was generated to ensure it is recent. Rejecting stale data prevents users or the protocol from executing transactions at outdated valuations.
{% endtab %}

{% tab title="Boundaries" %}
The datafeed confirms that the price falls within hardcoded minimum and maximum limits. This acts as a failsafe against human or technical errors (for example, preventing a price from being set to zero).
{% endtab %}
{% endtabs %}
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
**Atomic Execution**

If a price update fails one of the above checks, the smart contract reverts the entire transaction. The update is temporarily discarded, ensuring the valuation remains secure and user funds are protected.
{% endhint %}

## Third-Party Oracles for Payment Tokens

While the mToken NAV is updated via the Midas Oracle Pipeline, the payment tokens, i.e. the tokens used for deposits and redemptions, such as wETH, WBTC, or stablecoins, require continuous, real-time market pricing. Midas handles this through specific integrations and security bands:

* **Third-Party Oracles:** Midas integrates with established, decentralized oracle networks to provide real-time pricing for all payment tokens, including notably Chainlink and Pyth.
* **Asset Price Validation & Depeg Protection:** When users transact using stablecoins (such as USDC, USDT, or non-USD denominated stables), the system typically maintains a fixed 1:1 conversion ratio to its underlying peg. To ensure safety, Midas applies a Universal Tolerance Band to all payment tokens. For stablecoins, this is a strict corridor of 0.997 to 1.003. This narrow range accommodates routine market liquidity fluctuations while acting as a critical circuit breaker. If the market price of a token provided by our oracles breaks outside these pre-defined boundaries, the system detects the anomaly and halts transactions. This prevents the strategy and its investors from absorbing assets during a severe depeg or price manipulation event.

# The Midas Attestation Engine

{% hint style="info" %}
You can independently audit the Midas Attestation Engine and verify the backing of any mToken.
{% endhint %}

The **Midas Attestation Engine** is the trust layer powering Midas onchain investment products.

It transforms structured information into verifiable onchain checkpoints. These checkpoints can relate to financial exposure, technical state, operational processes, or any other information that requires public verification.

The engine was built in close collaboration with Chainlink, LlamaRisk, vLayer, and Canary.

#### A General-Purpose Attestation Layer <a href="#a-general-purpose-attestation-layer" id="a-general-purpose-attestation-layer"></a>

Anything can be attested.

The Attestation Engine is not limited to NAV or proof of reserves. It can formalize and verify:

* Asset backing
* Net Asset Value calculations
* Collateralization levels
* Smart contract parameters
* Governance decisions
* Operational workflows
* Risk committee approvals
* Offchain portfolio statements
* Legal confirmations
* Service provider confirmations
* Monitoring alerts
* Any structured claim requiring verifiable anchoring

If information matters to investors, it can be transformed into a claim and bundled into an attestation.

#### Why It Matters <a href="#why-it-matters" id="why-it-matters"></a>

Midas aims to provide the highest possible level of real-time reporting across its products (release anticipated in April 2026). Real-time reporting delivers continuous visibility. However, live systems can occasionally experience temporary inconsistencies, latency, or data synchronization issues.

The Midas Attestation Engine addresses these limitations by providing **high-quality, independently verified checkpoints**.

Each attestation represents a formal and structured event that:

* Is anchored onchain
* Is independently verified onchain
* Can be programmatically consumed by investors, integrators, curators, and risk frameworks

Attestations are designed to serve as authoritative checkpoints. They do not replace real-time transparency dashboard and KPIs. Instead, they complement them.

In practice:

* Real-time reporting provides continuous information.
* Attestations provide verified reference states.

Together, they establish a transparency model that combines visibility with independently verifiable trust anchors.

#### Core Concepts <a href="#core-concepts" id="core-concepts"></a>

**Claim**

A **claim** is a structured assertion about a specific fact.

A claim may originate from:

* A service provider
* A fund administrator
* A risk manager
* A blockchain endpoint
* An API
* A notarized email
* An internal monitoring system

Claims can, when required, be stored directly in the Midas Attestation Engine smart contracts.

**Attestation**

An **attestation** is a structured bundle of one or more claims, which:

* Defines a reporting scope
* Links related claims
* Includes timestamps and metadata
* Follows predefined validation logic
* Is emitted onchain
* Is stored on IPFS
* Is permanently recorded in the Attestation Engine smart contracts

When possible, Midas leverages Chainlink CRE to publish attestations in a standardized and decentralized manner.

An attestation becomes a machine-readable checkpoint that downstream systems can consume.

#### Verification Layer <a href="#verification-layer" id="verification-layer"></a>

After publication, attestations are independently reviewed by verifiers.

A onchain verification confirms that:

* The expected operational workflow was followed
* The claims originate from legitimate sources
* The validation process was executed independently

Verifications are recorded onchain (and tied with their associated attestation), and deliver the credibility layer of the system.

#### **Built With Best-in-Class Partners** <a href="#built-with-best-in-class-partners" id="built-with-best-in-class-partners"></a>

We did not build this alone. The Attestation Engine is the result of close collaboration with [Chainlink](https://chain.link/), [LlamaRisk](https://www.llamarisk.com/), [vlayer](https://www.vlayer.xyz/), and [Canary](https://www.canaryprotocol.com/).

LlamaRisk co-developed the SAVE verification framework and the core attestation architecture, and also serves as an independent onchain verifier. Chainlink Runtime Environment (CRE) provides the decentralized attestation building and publication infrastructure that ensures attestations are broadcast through a predefined, transparent onchain workflow. vlayer powers the cryptographic notarization of source documents (like email disclosures) preserving their content and timestamp so they can be publicly inspected. And Canary provides independent verification capacity, reinforcing the credibility layer of every attestation cycle.

#### Smart contract and Public Codebase <a href="#smart-contract-and-public-codebase" id="smart-contract-and-public-codebase"></a>

The Attestation Engine is available at [0x2D6e9F608807436DE5D9603B00Abe3FEd1Bc809d](https://etherscan.io/address/0x2d6e9f608807436de5d9603b00abe3fed1bc809d) on Ethereum.

The workflow and verification scripts are made public at Midas's [GitHub repo](https://github.com/midas-apps).

#### Feed Types <a href="#feed-types" id="feed-types"></a>

**POR Feed**

The Midas Proof of Reserve attestation cryptographically anchors the risk manager’s NAV disclosure. The NAV statement is notarized using vLayer’s technology, which converts the original email into a verifiable onchain input while preserving its integrity and timestamp.

The notarized email is stored on IPFS and is publicly accessible for review. This allows anyone to inspect the exact content that entered the attestation workflow.

The workflow used to construct and publish the attestation is executed through Chainlink CRE. This ensures that the publication process follows a predefined and transparent onchain logic.

Independent verifiers, including LlamaRisk and Canary, review the process and confirm onchain that the publication workflow has been respected. Their verification confirms that the data submitted by the risk manager was not altered by Midas during the attestation process.

Before the NAV is used to update token pricing, Midas performs internal consistency checks and reconciliation procedures. These controls ensure that the disclosed NAV is coherent within the broader allocation and valuation framework.

> :warning: <sub>*Independent onchain verification is currently being rolled out progressively and does not yet apply to all mTokens. It operates as a complementary transparency layer and does not currently condition the onchain price update.*</sub>


# Strategy Rules

Products are managed and monitored by appointed strategy managers. We enforce a strict separation of powers between asset safeguarding and portfolio management. While appointed strategy managers are responsible for ongoing portfolio composition, capital deployment, and performance, they operate within clear boundaries to which Midas, ultimately, holds the keys.

This structure ensures that strategy managers can efficiently execute their specific strategies without ever possessing unilateral control or discretionary withdrawal rights over the assets.

The operational parameters of every strategy manager are strictly defined by Strategy Management Agreements which legally bind them to specific strategy rules. The strategy rules define the permitted investment universe and risk parameters, including liquidity thresholds, leverage limits, asset and counterparty concentration limits, protocol and exchange exposure caps, blacklist restrictions, and required screening and risk management standards.

#### Strategy Manager Selection Process

Before an Strategy Manager is granted authority to manage a Portfolio, they must undergo a rigorous vetting process to ensure they meet the following standards:

* **Legal & Regulatory Standing:** Every manager must represent and warrant that they hold all necessary authorizations, licenses, and registrations required under Applicable Law to perform strategy management activities.
* **"Skin in the Game":** To ensure alignment of interests, Strategy Managers may be required to establish and maintain a Minimum Investment in the strategy.
* **Compliance & AML:** Managers must maintain anti-money laundering (AML) and anti-terrorism policies according to the standards required by the applicable AML regime.
* **Operational Integrity:** Managers must provide evidence of professional indemnity insurance and prove that they maintain robust internal operational safeguards and risk management procedures.

#### Approval Policies & Whitelisting

Midas employs technical and operational "guardrails" that prevent any single Party from acting unilaterally:

* **Workspace:** All strategy execution occurs within a designated MPC Workspace. This Workspace is configured to require authorization for transactions.
* **Approver Quorum:** Transactions are only satisfied when the Approver Quorum is met. It is a combination of Strategy Manager Signers who initiate the transaction and Midas Signers who must approve it. Policies and whitelists can only be modified with Approver Quorum.
* **Whitelisting:** Strategy Managers are restricted to executing transactions only included in a pre-approved Whitelist.
* **Strict Control over Destinations:** Midas maintains exclusive control over the Whitelist. If a manager requires a new destination address, it must be submitted for review and may only be used after formal approval.

#### Dual Risk Management

Risk management is a shared responsibility where discretionary strategy meets institutional oversight:

* **Discretion vs. Safekeeping:** While the Strategy Manager has discretionary authority to manage the Portfolio, they never acquire title to or custody of any assets. Assets always remain the property of Midas and are held in designated accounts or wallets.
* **Continuous Monitoring:** Managers must deliver comprehensive reports. These reports include asset allocation breakdowns, crystallised yields, and confirmations of compliance with Strategy Rules.
* **Emergency Intervention:** Midas retains the right to issue "Emergency Instructions" in the event of a security incident, protocol or oracle failure. Upon receipt of such instructions, the Strategy Manager must immediately halt transactions, unwind positions, or move assets to stablecoins to prioritize loss containment.
* **Protocol Tiering:** Risk is further mitigated by strict exposure caps based on protocol maturity.


# Risk Monitoring

### Risk Management & Monitoring

The Midas risk management framework utilises automated monitoring, data processing, and scenario analysis to ensure portfolio operations adhere to defined investment mandates.

#### Core Infrastructure & Data Integration

Midas uses [1Token](https://1token.tech/), a Portfolio Management System (PMS), to monitor underlying positions across all venues. Data is aggregated from three primary sources:

* **DeFi (On-Chain):** Data is sourced via RPC endpoints and platforms like DeBank to capture EVM and non-EVM wallet balances.
* **CeFi (CEX/DEX):** API integrations capture live positions, transactions, and P\&L from external exchanges.
* **Synthetic/OTC:** Off-chain agreements are integrated into the system to reflect total portfolio exposure.

Raw data fetched from the PMS is processed through internal scripts to resolve nested DeFi positions, such as liquid staking receipts and wrapped tokens. These are mapped to their ultimate underlying asset (Delta Currency) to calculate exact exposure.

#### Risk Classification

Positions are categorized into three distinct risk profiles:

1. **Market Risk:** Exposure to the price volatility of standard assets (e.g., BTC, ETH) and the base currency of the fund.
2. **Depeg Risk:** Exposure to the price of a stablecoin relative to its fiat peg, which is measured as equivalent to the credit risk of the stablecoin issuer.
3. **Protocol & Counterparty Risk:**&#x20;

* **General Protocol Risk:** Potential loss from smart contract vulnerabilities, hacks, or operational errors across wallets and DeFi protocols.
* **Explicit Credit Risk:** Contractual risk of loan non-repayment or collateral impairment in borrowing/lending pools.
* **Venue/Custodian Risk:** Exposure to the centralized exchanges, custodians, or brokers holding the assets.

#### Automated Surveillance & Limit Framework

Automated snapshots track portfolio composition, valuations, and greeks against predefined limits. The system monitors for:

* **Issuer & Counterparty Limits:** Maximum allowable allocations to specific fiat currencies, stablecoins, networks, tokens, self-custody wallets, custodians, and exchanges.
* **Leverage Limits:** Constraints on Initial Margin and Maintenance Margin (IM/MM) ratios, net/gross delta relative to equity, and individual account leverage.
* **Market Neutrality:** Verification that delta exposures by asset class remain within fund-specific bounds.

Deviations from these thresholds trigger automated alerts to the Risk and Operations teams. This process identifies pricing anomalies, asset mapping errors, or limit breaches prior to the formal Net Asset Value (NAV) publication cycle.

#### Scenario Analysis

Stress tests are calculated on a fund-by-fund and firm-wide basis to model potential losses under specific conditions:

* **Credit Loss Scenarios:** Simulating predetermined haircuts applied to all borrowing/lending protocols and yield-bearing assets that carry explicit contractual risk.
* **Depeg Scenarios:** Calculating the P\&L impact of applied haircuts on specific stablecoin holdings.
* **Systemic Default Risk:** A combined scenario where all credit protocols and stablecoins are shocked simultaneously. Projected losses are capped by the limited liability structure of the levered accounts.

The portfolio composition and exposures are periodically published in our [transparency section](https://midas.app/transparency).


# Midas Native Bridge

Midas Bridge moves mTOKENS natively between chains. Tokens are burnt on the source chain and minted 1:1 on the destination chain. There are no wrapped assets, liquidity pools, or bridged variants. You hold the same canonical mTOKEN on every supported chain.

Routes run on [LayerZero](https://layerzero.network/). Midas owns and configures the token contracts and pools on every chain, so mint and burn rights are never delegated to a third-party bridge. \
Support for [Chainlink CCIP](https://chain.link/cross-chain) is in development and will be enabled per route once live. The order summary and tracking link show the protocol used for your transfer.

### How it works

1. **Initiate a transfer.** The Midas contract burns the amount on the source chain. It then emits a cross-chain message.
2. **Verify the message.** LayerZero uses independent DVNs. Chainlink CCIP uses its oracle network and Risk Management Network.
3. **Mint on the destination.** The destination contract mints the same amount to the recipient address.

### Eligibility

Most mTOKENS are permissionless. Any address can hold and transfer them, and bridging behaves like any other transfer.

Some mTOKENS are permissioned, meaning the token enforces a greenlist, a minimum balance, or both. Where those checks apply:

* The sending address must not be blacklisted on the source chain.
* The receiving address must be eligible on the destination chain.

If the recipient is not eligible when the message arrives, the mint cannot complete and the transfer stays pending until it can.

The bridge interface has the same geographic restrictions as the Midas app.

### Fees and timing

Midas charges no fee on the amount bridged. You receive exactly what you send.

You pay two costs in the source chain's native token:

* Gas for the source-chain transaction.
* A messaging fee for destination verification and execution.

They are quoted in the order summary before you confirm.

![](https://3475141875-files.gitbook.io/~/files/v0/b/gitbook-x-prod.appspot.com/o/spaces%2FMndxFHqGeA4nzBBeKDTV%2Fuploads%2FZrgxDfaJC928JV5d52sp%2FScreenshot%202026-09-16%20at%2012.03.32.png?alt=media\&token=a1a484e3-4c8d-4128-bb9e-958d833bc573)<br>

Delivery usually takes a few minutes. Transfers from Ethereum can take up to around 20 minutes. Source-chain finality drives delivery time. Network congestion can extend it.

### If a transfer does not complete

#### The message was not executed

The inbound rate limit was usually reached on arrival. The message remains valid. Find the source transaction in [LayerZero Scan](https://layerzeroscan.com) or the [CCIP Explorer](https://ccip.chain.link). Connect your wallet and retry execution manually.

Waiting for the rate limiter to refill is normally sufficient.

#### The mint to your address failed

This usually means the recipient is not eligible on the destination chain. Trying will keep failing until they are. Complete onboarding for the destination address on the destination chain, then retry the message.

If the original recipient is blacklisted, tokens remain in escrow. Midas handles recovery directly.&#x20;

#### Nothing was minted

This is rare. It can happen when the token is paused on arrival. Midas monitors and registers these messages. If a transfer has not completed after 24 hours, [contact us](https://discord.com/invite/midasrwa). Include the source transaction hash.

In every case, assets remain fully backed. Recovery changes where tokens sit, never how many exist.

### FAQ

#### Can I send to a different address than I bridge from?

Yes, if that address is eligible on the destination chain.

#### Do I receive a wrapped token?

No. You always receive the native mTOKEN.

#### Can I cancel after sending?

No. Once the source transaction confirms, the tokens are burnt and the message is in flight.

#### Can Midas reverse a transfer sent to the wrong address?

No, as with any on-chain transfer, provided the destination was valid and eligible.

# Smart Contract Security

To ensure the strict integrity of all token and vault operations, Midas enforces a comprehensive suite of on-chain security parameters. These mechanisms are hardcoded into the protocol to eliminate single points of failure and prevent unauthord access.

#### Multisig/MPC Controls&#x20;

No single individual or compromised key can unilaterally update prices, access funds, or modify critical vault parameters. All high-level protocol operations require approval from a multi-signature wallet and or MPC, typically requiring a minimum of three independent approvers. This strict consensus mechanism applies to Oracle price updates, modifications to fee recipients, and any adjustments to the underlying risk parameters of the vaults.

#### Midas Controller

**Overview**\
The Midas Controller ([address](https://etherscan.io/address/0x0312a9d1ff2372ddedcbb21e4b6389afc919ac4b)) is the core access control layer governing platform-level operations. It is designed with a multi-layered security architecture to ensure robust protection against single points of failure.

**Quorum Structure**\
The Midas Controller operates under a 1-of-3 quorum model:

* 1 Safe (multisig)
* 2 MPC-based signers:
  * 1 Fordefi
  * 1 Fireblocks

Each of these three entities represents one quorum participant.

\
**Underlying Security (Per Signer)**\
Each quorum participant is backed by an independent threshold signing mechanism:

* Safe (multisig): requires a quorum of at least 4 signers backed by cold wallet
* Fordefi MPC: requires at least 4 MPC participants
* Fireblocks MPC: requires at least 4 MPC participants

\
**Security Design Principles**

* Multi-provider setup: Reduces reliance on a single custody or signing provider
* Layered quorum: Combines wallet-level and MPC-level thresholds
* Fault tolerance: System remains operational even in case of partial signer failure
* Operational separation: Different entities and systems participate in signing flows

#### Role-Based Access Control&#x20;

Midas enforces a strict, cryptographic separation of powers across all smart contract functions. By isolating specific capabilities to dedicated contracts, the system ensures that a vulnerability in one area cannot be exploited to compromise the broader protocol.

* **Minter Role:** The Deposit Vault and cross-chain OFT bridge contracts hold the Minter Role. These contracts can strictly mint new mTokens upon successful deposit confirmations but have no ability to burn them.
* **Burner Role:** The Redemption Vault and OFT bridge contracts hold the Burner Role. They are authorised only to destroy mTokens when processing user withdrawals and have no minting capabilities.
* **Pauser Role:** This role holds dedicated emergency pause authority. It is separated from daily operational roles, is activated manually, and requires multi-sig authorisation to execute.
* **Token Management Role:** This specialised role handles manual minting or burning required for specific OTC transactions. System roles are tightly governed and can only be granted or revoked by the highest-level authorised administrative addresses.

#### Granular Pause Mechanisms & Smart Contract Controls

In the event of extreme market volatility or a security incident, Midas supports manual pause functionality. There are no automatic triggers for these specific actions. All manual pauses require multi-sig authorisation to activate, preventing malicious griefing attacks.

* **Vault-Level Pauses:** Operators can apply targeted restrictions directly to the vaults. They can choose to pause only deposits, only instant redemptions, only standard redemptions, or all vault operations simultaneously. This granularity allows the protocol to halt a specific liquidity flow without freezing the entire system.
* **Token-Level Pauses:** At the overarching token layer, a pause freezes all ERC-20 transfers, meaning mTokens cannot be moved between wallets or smart contracts. However, critical read-only functions, such as balance queries and block explorer viewing, remain fully operational.
* **Timelocked Upgrades:** As a broader protocol safeguard, all smart contracts are gated by a timelock controller that enforces a mandatory 48-hour delay for any contract upgrades, ensuring transparency and preventing abrupt structural changes.

During any pause event, background price updates and Oracle operations continue uninterrupted to ensure the NAV remains perfectly synced with the underlying assets once operations resume.

#### Transaction Atomicity&#x20;

All operations on Midas smart contracts are fully atomic, meaning they execute sequentially in a single block. A transaction must either execute completely from start to finish, or it will revert entirely.&#x20;

If any single step in a deposit or redemption flow fails, including a rejection by the Oracle's automated validation checks, the entire transaction is instantly reverted. No funds are transferred, no tokens are minted, and users are never left in an ambiguous, partially processed state.

# Standard Incident Response Protocol

#### **Objective**

The objective of Midas’ Standard Incident Response Protocol is to protect tokenholder interests, ensure market integrity and mitigate risk during times of market disruption. This protocol establishes a structured framework for responding to potential security threats, third-party exploits, or market anomalies that could impact mToken price stability.

#### **Activation Triggers**

The protocol is activated upon the detection of a security incident by internal monitoring systems or authorised third-party security providers. Activation may occur under the following conditions:

1. Identification of vulnerabilities in third-party protocols.
2. Detection of unusual onchain activity suggesting a potential exploit.
3. Indications of potential contagion effects that could threaten assets underlying mTokens.

#### **Preventative Safeguarding Measures**

Once triggered, the protocol implements a temporary suspension of specific platform functionalities to prevent further exposure. This phase is characterised by:

* **Subscription and Redemption Pause:** Investing in and redeeming of affected mTokens is temporarily disabled.
* **Precautionary Stance:** A suspension does not necessarily indicate a compromise of Midas’ infrastructure or exposure of a product, but rather an intentional move to verify safety before continuing operations.
* **Public Disclosures:** Status updates are prominently displayed on the Midas website (<https://midas.app/products>).

#### **Investigation and Resolution**

During an active investigation, the team and strategy managers execute a coordinated response plan. An immediate investigation is launched to determine the scope of the alert and the degree of exposure, while assessing integrity of mToken pricing to ensure no deviation from underlying value has occurred. Efforts are prioritised to minimise the duration of the temporary measures.

#### **Criteria for Service Restoration**

Functionality is restored progressively only after the successful verification of the following benchmarks:

1. **Asset Security:** Confirmation that the underlying assets are fully secured.
2. **Oracle Reliability:** Verification that price oracles are providing accurate and reliable data feeds.
3. **Smart Contract Integrity:** Technical validation that all platform smart contracts are functioning according to specifications.

#### **Information Channels**

Real-time updates and notifications regarding the resumption of normal operations are provided through the following channels:

* **Official Twitter Feed:** The primary source for incident timestamps and resolution announcements. <https://x.com/MidasRWA>&#x20;

Our Luxembourg securitisation vehicle is also structured as an orphan vehicle, owned through a charitable trust and without shareholder links to other Midas entities. #### German Issuer Setup&#x20;

The products issued by Midas Software GmbH give investors a contractual exposure to the performance of the underlying strategy as set out in the applicable offering documentation. This structure does not use statutory compartmentalisation, and investors should not assume that assets are ring-fenced at issuer level in the same way as under the Luxembourg securitisation framework. For certain products, the applicable offering documentation may provide for collateral and security arrangements granted in favour of tokenholders.

# Methodology & Definitions

<details>

<summary><strong>Fees</strong></summary>

{% hint style="info" %}
When reviewing the fees associated with Midas mTokens, we highly recommend starting by verifying the specific product documentation. In particular, always check the Pricing Supplement for the individual product you are interested in, as the exact fee structures, percentages, and configurations are defined there.
{% endhint %}

<sup>*Here is a breakdown of the fees that we may charge at the product level.:*</sup>

* <sup>***Management Fees:***</sup><sup>*&#x20;*</sup><sup>*The product may charge a management fee, which represents a specific percentage of the strategy's net asset value over a given period. If applicable, management fees accrue with each NAV pricing update.*</sup>
* <sup>***Performance Fees:***</sup><sup>*&#x20;*</sup><sup>*The product may charge a performance fee based on a percentage of its positive returns. This is calculated using a "high-water mark" system. Simply put, a high-water mark is the highest historical peak in value the strategy has reached over a certain time period. The performance fee is only charged on profits made above this peak, ensuring you do not pay a fee twice for recovering past losses. Please note that how often this high-water mark is evaluated or reset (its periodicity) depends on the specific underlying strategy.*</sup>
* <sup>***Instant Redemption Fees:***</sup><sup>*&#x20;*</sup><sup>*Standard redemptions are always free of charge. However, you have the option to use an instant redemption feature for accelerated settlement. If you voluntarily choose this option, an instant redemption fee (typically ranging from 20 to 100 basis points) may be charged to compensate liquidity providers for the costs of immediate settlement.*</sup>
* <sup>***Minting (Issuance) Fees:***</sup><sup>*&#x20;*</sup><sup>*While minting is usually free, Midas may occasionally apply a small fee if you choose to subscribe using less common payment tokens. Whenever this happens, we will always offer a free alternative payment method. Any potential fees are fully transparent and will be clearly shown on the subscription page before you finalise your transaction.*</sup>

<sup>*Additional fees, transaction costs, and other deductions may also accrue within the underlying assets themselves, which can indirectly impact the overall returns of the strategy.*</sup>

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Annualised Percentage Yield (APY)  &#x26; Rewards</strong></summary>

<sup>*When evaluating the performance of your mTokens, it is important to understand the distinction between the core Annual Percentage Yield (APY) and the additional ecosystem rewards that may accrue.*</sup>

<sup>**Understanding APY**</sup>&#x20;

<sup>*The core APY reflects the annualised historical growth of the token’s Net Asset Value. For example, our Trailing 7-Day APY is calculated by determining the percentage change in the NAV over the past seven calendar days. This 7-day return is then annualised by compounding it over a full year, estimating what the annual percentage yield would be if that recent performance continued for a full 12 months.*</sup>

<sup>*Please note that this APY is a strictly historical metric. Past performance is not a reliable indicator of future performance. Because mTokens are based on actively managed strategies, returns will fluctuate based on the risk manager's discretionary decisions and market conditions. The displayed APY does not constitute a simulation, projection, or forecast of future yields you might expect.*</sup>

<sup>**How Rewards Work**</sup>&#x20;

<sup>*mTokens issued by Midas allow token holders to earn additional ecosystem rewards through the underlying strategy's allocations. Because mToken strategies are deployed across various DeFi protocols and ecosystems, they often qualify for incentive programs like points, token emissions, or multipliers.*</sup>

<sup>*These rewards are not included in the reported APY. Any reference to ecosystem rewards does not represent a guaranteed entitlement or financial return; they are contingent benefits that vary based on third-party protocol conditions and the risk manager’s strategic allocations.*</sup>

<sup>**When Rewards Are Realised and Distributed**</sup>&#x20;

<sup>*Rewards are not factored into the token’s ongoing price updates until they are "crystallised", meaning they have been successfully received, unlocked, and (if necessary) converted into stablecoins by the risk manager.*</sup>

<sup>*Once crystallised, these stablecoins are used to mint additional units of the respective mToken. These newly minted tokens then become claimable via the Midas frontend. Crucially, your share of these rewards is distributed on a pro-rata basis depending on your holding period.*</sup>

<sup>*Depending on their size and frequency, crystallised rewards may be:*</sup>

* <sup>***Incorporated into the APY calculation:***</sup><sup>*&#x20;*</sup><sup>*If the rewards are marginal or recurring.*</sup>
* <sup>***Distributed separately:***</sup><sup>*&#x20;*</sup><sup>*If the rewards are significant or one-off, they are distributed retroactively as claimable mTokens on the respective ecosystem chain.*</sup>

<sup>*Note: Crystallisation and reward distribution are subject to execution, operational, and market risks.*</sup>

<sup>**How Rewards Are Displayed**</sup>&#x20;

<sup>*Each mToken’s product page dynamically displays its known reward exposure based on current strategy allocations. This transparent view includes the protocols where the strategy is active and the corresponding points multipliers or reward categories.  As strategies shift (entering or exiting positions) this display is updated accordingly.*</sup>

<sup>*Only verifiable, active reward exposures are shown. Midas does not speculate on the value of uncrystallised rewards or publish estimates of future potential earnings.*</sup>

<sup>**About Merkl**</sup>&#x20;

<sup>*To make reward distribution seamless and highly efficient, Midas utilises Merkl, a platform designed to distribute rewards based on on-chain snapshots and off-chain calculations.*</sup>

<sup>*Traditional reward systems often require users to lock or stake their tokens in specific smart contracts, which costs gas fees and limits how you can use your assets. Merkl changes this by tracking token balances and DeFi activities automatically. This means you can simply hold your mTokens in your wallet or deploy them across supported DeFi protocols, and Merkl will track your eligibility in the background.*</sup>

<sup>**Special Rewards**</sup>&#x20;

<sup>*In certain instances, mTokens may become eligible for Special Rewards. To ensure clarity, we group all non-standard incentives under this single category (which includes what might occasionally be referred to as "bonus" or "deposit-to-earn" rewards).*</sup>

<sup>*Special Rewards are those not part of standard public points programs. They may stem from custom agreements, early-stage ecosystem contributions, or specific campaign participation (e.g., depositing an mToken into a specific summoning campaign to earn bonus ecosystem tokens). These Special Rewards follow the exact same crystallisation and distribution process:*</sup>

* <sup>*Minor Special Rewards may be incorporated into regular price updates once crystallised.*</sup>
* <sup>*Major Special Rewards are converted to the respective mTokens and distributed retroactively via Merkl after crystallisation.*</sup>

</details>

<summary><strong>Total Value Locked (TVL)</strong></summary>

<sup>*TVL (Total Value Locked) describes the outstanding supply of all mTokens and Midas-powered Vault Tokens valued in USD.*</sup>

</details>

<details>

<summary><strong>Net Asset Value (NAV)</strong></summary>

<sup>*The NAV (Net Asset Value) per mToken represents the current value of one mToken expressed in its base currency. It is defined as the total value of the reference portfolio that the mToken tracks, divided by the number of mTokens outstanding.*</sup> &#x20;

</details>

# Financial Crime Policy

This Global Financial Crime Policy (the "Policy") establishes the framework for preventing Anti-Money Laundering (AML), Counter-Terrorism Financing (CTF), Sanctions violations, and Market Abuse across the Midas Group. This Policy is formally reviewed at least once per calendar year. It was last updated on 27 January 2026.

As a result of the Policy, we may from time to time ask for additional information, reject transactions, freeze assets, or refuse to provide services. We may set stricter requirements than our legal obligations, and we may choose not to support certain customer relationships or business activity even if they are legally permitted.

#### Introduction

Midas Software GmbH ("Midas" or the "Issuer") is an issuer of blockchain-based certificates (the "Products" or "mTokens") with the mission to bring real-world assets (RWA) on-chain, making them accessible for investment through transparent and efficient digital infrastructure. The mTokens are tradable and designed to track the performance of underlying assets. Midas offers its products to a range of investors, including institutional and professional investors.

Midas is committed to the highest standards of integrity and has zero tolerance for the use of its platform or products for illicit purposes. Specifically, our objectives are to:

* **Prevent Financial Crime:** Ensure that products are not used as vehicles for the financing of terrorism or other criminal activities.
* **Combat Money Laundering:** Implement robust controls to verify that all investments in mTokens originate from legitimate, non-criminal sources.
* **Enforce Sanctions:** Prevent any direct or indirect investment from individuals, entities, or jurisdictions subject to international sanctions (including OFAC, EU, and UN lists).
* **Protect Market Integrity:** Prohibit the unlawful purchase of tokens and prevent any activity that could constitute market abuse or manipulation.

Midas adopts rigorous internal processes and engages industry-leading service providers and law firms to mitigate the risks arising from these challenges.

#### 1. Governance

**1.1 Oversight**

This Policy is formally reviewed and approved by Midas’ Senior Management to ensure its continued alignment with Midas's mission and risk profile.

**1.2 Organisational Awareness and Training**

Midas ensures that all personnel are aware of this Policy and fully equipped to recognize and mitigate financial crime risks through a mandatory training program. This process begins during onboarding and is reinforced through formal training sessions conducted at least once per calendar year. The company may, at its discretion, engage specialized external training providers to deliver this content or conduct the training internally. Midas maintains a log of all training activities, including attendee lists, dates, and the specific modules covered. To maintain a high level of technical proficiency, the curriculum is regularly updated with the latest intelligence regarding emerging trends in the digital asset ecosystem.

**1.3 Internal Reporting**

Midas operates an internal reporting framework requiring all employees to escalate "red flags" or suspicious activities through designated channels immediately. This internal reporting must occur before any other action or third party communication to prevent the risk of tipping off. Employees are strictly prohibited from disclosing to a client or third party that a report has been filed or that a transaction is under scrutiny. Targeted training ensures staff recognize that tipping off is a criminal offense and are equipped to maintain professional client interactions without compromising the confidentiality of an investigation.

**1.4 External Reporting**

Midas is committed to maintaining the integrity of its platform by cooperating with relevant authorities where a legal obligation exists. Midas shall provide prompt reporting of identified financial crime risks to the relevant authorities as required by the laws of the applicable jurisdiction, including the filing of necessary reports with Financial Intelligence Units (FIUs) regarding suspicious activity and notifying Sanctions Authorities in the event of a detected nexus with a sanctioned individual or entity.

**1.5 Financial Crime Committee (FCC) Meetings**

To ensure the Policy remains effective and operationally integrated, Midas shall hold a Financial Crime Committee (FCC) Meeting at least once per quarter. Attendees shall include representatives from Compliance, Operations, and management. Agenda Items shall include:

1. Review of any suspicious activity reports (SARs) or flagged wallets.
2. Evaluation of continued appropriateness of the technical infrastructure.
3. Performance review of third-party KYC/KYT service providers.
4. Discussion of new regulatory developments or emerging on-chain threats.

**1.6 Review Cycle**

The Policy is reviewed at least once per calendar year. In addition to this annual review, immediate updates and approvals are conducted on an ad-hoc basis following significant business changes such as the launch of new products, or the regulatory changes.

#### 2. Prohibited Counterparties

**2.1 Prohibited Jurisdictions**

Midas does not offer services to citizens of, or persons located in, jurisdictions in which Midas identified an unacceptable legal or regulatory risk, including:

1. **Sanctioned Territories:** Any country or region subject to comprehensive or targeted international sanctions as recognized by major global regimes (including the UN, EU, US, UK, and Switzerland).
2. **The United States of America:** Midas tokens are not offered to citizens of, or residents in, the United States of America.
3. **Discretionary Risk Jurisdictions:** Any country or region that Midas, at its sole discretion, deems to pose a risk to its operations, reputation, or compliance profile.

The consolidated list of restricted countries and the specific international sanctions lists utilized for enforcement are maintained in the "Technical Compliance Operations Manual."

**2.2 Prohibited Persons and Entities**

A "Prohibited Person" refers to any individual or entity that Midas deems ineligible to access its services for legal, regulatory, or risk-management reasons, including:

* **Sanctioned individuals:** Any individual identified as being subject to financial sanctions or travel bans by the UN, EU, OFAC (US), HM Treasury (UK), or SECO (Switzerland).
* **Status-Based Exclusions:** Any individual known to Midas to be a citizen or resident of a Prohibited Jurisdiction, regardless of their physical location or technical access point.
* **Indirectly Controlled or Associated:** Any entity identified as being significantly owned or controlled by a Prohibited Person, or any party identified as appearing to act on behalf of, or as a proxy for, a Prohibited Person, including "Proxies" or "Close Associates" of sanctioned individuals.
* **Discretionary Exclusions:** Any party that Midas, at its sole discretion, designates as a ineligible based on identified red flags, which may include suspicious on-chain behavior, high-risk scoring from blockchain analytics providers, a known association with illicit activities such as money laundering, terrorism financing, or cybercrime, or any other proprietary risk indicators identified during the due diligence process.

The specific service providers and screening criteria used to identify these parties are detailed in the "Technical Compliance Operations Manual."

#### 3. Technical Measures and Controls

Midas utilizes a multi-layered technical stack to enable the prevention, detection, and restriction of financial crime risks. These "Technical Gates" are designed to enforce compliance at the protocol and application levels. They represent the most critical layer of the Midas security and compliance framework.

**3.1 Jurisdictional Access Control Measures**

Midas employs geographic restrictions to manage access to its digital touchpoints, such as the Midas web application and platform interface. These measures prevent individuals in restricted regions from browsing or initiating workflows and are implemented via:

* **DNS-Level Filtering:** Utilizing reputable infrastructure providers to block access at the DNS level before a connection is established.
* **IP Intelligence and Geoblocking:** Employment of high-fidelity geolocation data services to prevent platform interaction from restricted regions.
* **VPN and Proxy Detection:** Midas may employ technical measures to detect and obfuscate access from Virtual Private Networks (VPNs) or other anonymizing proxies when deemed relevant to prevent the circumvention of perimeter controls.

**3.2 Wallet Screening and AML Risk Assessment**

While geoblocking manages interface access, Midas integrates blockchain analytics directly into its operational workflows to govern direct protocol interactions, specifically the subscription (issuance) and redemption of Midas tokens. This layer ensures that even if a user bypasses geographic filters, the actual movement of assets is subject to rigorous technical scrutiny. The measures and controls for these on-chain gates include:

* **Sanctions Verification:** Real-time cross-referencing of every interacting wallet address against the global sanctions lists defined in the "Technical Compliance Operations Manual".
* **Taint and Exposure Analysis:** Automated evaluation of a wallet’s transaction history based on their proximity to high-risk activities, such as:
  * Recent interactions with illicit "mixers" or obfuscation protocols.
  * Association with wallets linked to known hacks, exploits, or fraudulent schemes.
  * Complex transaction "hops" that indicate attempts to hide the origin of funds.
* **Transaction Blocking:** Where appropriate, Midas utilizes smart contract-based gates or API-driven triggers to prevent interactions from wallets that exceed defined risk thresholds. This "Technical Gate" prevents the commingling of protocol assets with high-risk or sanctioned liquidity.
* **Address Whitelisting and Permissions:** For specific strategies or product tiers, Midas may utilize "Allowlists" where only pre-approved wallet addresses are permitted to interact with the underlying smart contracts.

**3.3 Risk-Based Approach (RBA) to Controls**

Midas applies a Risk-Based Approach (RBA) to the implementation of its technical and procedural controls. This means that the intensity and depth of scrutiny are not uniform but are instead proportional to the identified risk profile of the counterparty, jurisdiction, and transaction type.

* **Dynamic Scrutiny:** The application of the technical control measures described above is determined by an internal risk-scoring framework. For example, transactions involving higher monetary values, or those originating from jurisdictions with a higher inherent risk profile, will trigger enhanced levels of scrutiny and may require additional manual review or supplemental documentation.
* **Efficiency and Proportionality:** By utilizing RBA, Midas ensures that its compliance resources are focused where the risk of financial crime is highest, allowing for a seamless user experience for low-risk interactions while maintaining robust safeguards against high-risk activity.
* **Periodic Calibration:** Midas regularly reviews and calibrates its risk-scoring parameters to adapt to the evolving threat landscape in the decentralized finance sector and changes in global regulatory expectations.

**3.4 Standards for Third-Party Providers**

To implement the technical controls described in this Section and to guarantee rigorous, real-time updates across all automated gates, Midas relies primarily on specialized third-party databases and screening tools. These external providers are essential to fulfilling the Group’s compliance requirements in the rapidly evolving digital asset landscape.

* **Independence and Integrity:** Midas ensures that all data and screening tools are sourced from reputable, highly experienced providers who operate independently, with no conflicts of interest or business relations that would compromise the integrity of their data or risk-scoring.
* **Automated Data Processing:** All screening results are processed through these providers' proprietary algorithms. This ensures high-fidelity matching against global sanctions lists and minimizes the risk of false negatives through sophisticated fuzzy logic and entity resolution.
* **Real-Time Integration:** By utilizing API-driven connections to these global databases, Midas ensures that its technical gates are updated instantaneously as global sanctions regimes or high-risk wallet clusters change.

The specific list of service providers and data partners currently engaged by Midas is maintained and regularly reviewed in the "Technical Compliance Operations Manual."

#### 4. Customer Due Diligence (KYC)

**4.1 Scope of Identity Verification**

While the previously described technical measures focus on location and wallet-level history, Know Your Customer (KYC) procedures are designed to verify the legal identity of the Tokenholder. The objective is to ensure that the investor is not a Prohibited Person as defined in this Policy, moving beyond pseudonymity to establish a clear link between a wallet and a verified individual or entity.

**4.2 Tiered Due Diligence**

Midas utilizes a Risk-Based Approach (RBA) to determine whether identity verification is required. Users that are categorized as having a moderate or high risk profile may be required to provide the following documentation:

* **Tier-1 Due Diligence:** Applied to moderate risk profiles. This may involve basic identity verification, such as a government-issued photo ID and a biometric liveness check.
* **Tier-2 Due Diligence (EDD):** Triggered by high-value transactions, complex corporate structures, or users associated with higher-risk jurisdictions. This may require deeper scrutiny, including, for example, Source of Wealth (SoW) and Source of Funds (SoF) declarations.

**4.3 Critical Touchpoints: Subscription and Redemption**

Midas identifies Subscription (entry) and Redemption (exit) as the two critical touchpoints for KYC enforcement. The point of issuance serves as the primary filter for capital entering the ecosystem; for specific tokens, Midas enforces Systematic KYC, requiring a wallet to be fully identified and whitelisted before any tokens are minted. For other entries, Midas may instead rely on Risk-Triggered Screening, where automated tools monitor the origin of funds and only "step up" to full identification if a user’s profile or on-chain history triggers specific risk thresholds.

Because tokens are transferable, the party seeking to exit the ecosystem may not be the same entity that originally subscribed. To prevent the protocol from interacting with Prohibited Persons or sanctioned assets, Midas applies a similarly rigorous screening process to Redemptions. This process evaluates both the redeeming wallet and the token’s secondary transaction history. Even if a redeeming wallet appears "clean," Midas may trigger risk-based identification if the tokens have passed through intermediary wallets flagged for illicit activity or other technical red flags. This holistic view ensures that the redemption process serves as a robust safeguard regardless of how the tokens were acquired.

#### 5. Monitoring & Surveillance

**5.1 Voluntary Monitoring of Unusual Transactions**

Midas endeavours to perform discretionary monitoring of both primary (subscription / redemption) and secondary market transactions on a best-effort basis. This monitoring is intended to detect transactions that deviate from standard economic patterns, which may include, for example:

* **Unusual patterns in volume & velocity:**
  * **Trading volumes:** unexplained spikes in mToken transfer activity
  * **High-Frequency / Short-Term Trading:** Identifying wallets that engage in rapid "in-and-out" movements of mTokens, which may be inconsistent with the investment profile of a long-term certificate holder.
* **Unusual Wallet Behavior:** Flagging wallets that exhibit erratic patterns, such as a dormant wallet suddenly moving large volumes, or wallets that act as "pass-through" nodes—receiving and immediately dispersing tokens to multiple unknown addresses.
* **Concentration Risks:** Monitoring for the sudden accumulation of significant percentages of the total supply in a single unverified wallet.
* **Geographic Anomalies:** Detecting patterns that suggest tokens are being moved systematically toward high-risk or sanctioned jurisdictions.
* **Structuring (Smurfing):** Monitoring for the fragmentation of large holdings into smaller amounts across numerous wallets to bypass detection thresholds or obfuscate the true size of a position.

**5.2 Market Abuse Prevention**

Midas employs surveillance techniques designed to identify patterns suggestive of market manipulation. These efforts focus on:

* **Wash Trading:** Detecting circular or coordinated trades between related addresses intended to generate artificial liquidity or volume.
* **Price Distortion:** Flagging activity aimed at causing the secondary market price of mTokens to deviate materially from their underlying Net Asset Value (NAV).
* **Informed Trading:** Reviewing unusual secondary market activity that occurs in close proximity to portfolio rebalancing or significant corporate actions.
* **Manager & Related Party Surveillance:** Monitoring the on-chain activity of addresses known to be associated with Strategy Managers, their affiliates, or other parties with non-public information. This is intended to ensure that those responsible for managing the Notional Reference Portfolios are not engaging in trading activity, such as "front-running" portfolio rebalances or arbitrating expected NAV adjustments.

**5.3 Intelligence Integration & Risk Enhancement**

The data gathered through the monitoring of unusual transactions and market abuse is used to enhance Midas’s internal risk-based assessments. This "Proprietary Intelligence" loop allows Midas to:

* **Augment Third-Party Data:** Supplementing standard screening lists from third parties with Midas-specific "red flags" and internally identified high-risk clusters.
* **Proactive Gating:** Updating internal blacklists and "Technical Gates" based on observed behavior before those wallets are officially flagged by global regulators.
* **Dynamic Risk Scoring:** Adjusting the risk profile of users or strategies based on real-time secondary market interactions.

#### 6. Legal & Contractual Measures

**6.1 User Accountability, Warranties, and Transparent Communication**

Midas utilizes a framework of transparent disclosure and contractual warranties to ensure that all protocol participants operate within legal and regulatory boundaries.

* **Preventative Transparency:** Midas is committed to providing clear and comprehensive information regarding the technical and legal framework under which the mTokens are issued. This transparency is specifically designed to incentivize prospective users to conduct their own independent legal and regulatory assessment prior to interaction, thereby serving as a primary preventative measure against the unintended or unlawful acquisition of mTokens in restricted jurisdictions.
* **Affirmative Responsibility:** The onus of compliance remains with the user. Interaction with the protocol is predicated on the user's confirmation that they have performed the necessary due diligence and have determined that their participation is fully compliant with local laws. Users are solely responsible for verifying the legality of their interaction with the protocol under the laws of the jurisdiction from which they access the Services.
* **Prohibited Persons Warranty:** Users must warrant that they are not "Prohibited Persons" in the meaning of this Policy.
* **Market Integrity Commitment:** All users contractually agree to refrain from any activity that could be construed as market abuse, including wash trading or attempts to artificially distort the secondary market price of mTokens.
* **Cooperation and Supplemental Information:** To support its ongoing risk-based monitoring, Midas reserves the right to require additional information to perform its KYC obligations. This right is exercised particularly when a wallet's activity triggers an internal risk alert, ensuring that the Company can verify the legitimacy of the capital within the ecosystem.

**6.2 Strategy Manager Obligations**

Strategy Managers (“Managers”) play a pivotal role in maintaining the integrity of the mTokens. To ensure their conduct aligns with the interests of investors and the stability of the ecosystem, Managers are subject to the following legal and contractual requirements:

* **Duty of Market Integrity:** Managers warrant that they will manage the Notional Reference Portfolio with the sole focus on market integrity and the best interests of the mToken holders.
* **Warranties Against Market Abuse & Manipulation:** Strategy Managers explicitly warrant and undertake that they shall not engage in, facilitate, or encourage any conduct that constitutes or is conducive to market abuse. This comprehensive warranty includes, but is not limited to:
  * **Prohibition on Information Leaks (Anti-Tipping):** A strict prohibition on the unauthorized disclosure or "tipping" of material non-public information (MNPI) regarding portfolio compositions, upcoming rebalancings, or corporate actions to any third party.
  * **Market Manipulation:** A ban on any activity intended to create a false or misleading appearance regarding the price, liquidity, or market demand for mTokens, including wash trading, layering, or spoofing.
  * **Price Interference:** A commitment to refrain from any attempt to artificially influence or peg the secondary market price of mTokens in a manner that causes them to deviate from their underlying Net Asset Value (NAV).
  * **Prohibition on Self-Dealing:** An obligation to ensure that no trading activity is conducted, either directly or through associated parties, that places the Manager’s interests above the interests of the mToken holders.
  * **Mandatory Wallet Disclosure:** To facilitate Midas’s monitoring and surveillance efforts, Strategy Managers and their associated parties (including key personnel, affiliates, and controlled entities) are required to disclose all wallet addresses through which they intend to hold, subscribe to, or trade mTokens. Midas maintains a registry of these addresses to ensure that any manager-linked activity is transparent and compliant with internal policies.
  * **Prohibition of Speculative Trading:** Strategy Managers and their associated parties are prohibited from engaging in short-term speculative trading of the mTokens they manage. While investment in their own strategy is permitted (and encouraged as a signal of confidence), any such investment must be conducted through declared wallets and is subject to a minimum holding period as agreed between Midas and the Manager. This restriction is designed to prevent "pump and dump" schemes, front-running of portfolio rebalances, or any trading activity that leverages non-public information.

**6.3 Employee Conduct**

Midas maintains strict internal standards for employees and contractors to prevent the misuse of non-public information and ensure internal market integrity:

* **Prohibition of Insider Activity:** All employees are strictly prohibited from trading mTokens based on non-public information (e.g., upcoming strategy changes or rebalancings).
* **Mandatory Holding Periods:** employees are encouraged to be long-term holders of mTokens to align their interests with the protocol's success. However, any employee trades are subject to a mandatory minimum holding period to discourage short-term speculation.
* **Conflict of Interest & Interest Disclosure:** Employees must disclose their transactions and holdings in Midas products upon request.
