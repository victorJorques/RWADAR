ARTEX GM enables investors to trade shares of iconic masterpieces on a fully regulated venue.

MULTILATERAL TRADING FACTILITY
Trade Shares In Iconic Works of Art

Buy and sell shares in extraordinary art on the world’s first Multilateral Trading Facility (MTF) regulated for fine art by the Financial Market Authority (FMA) in Liechtenstein and compliant with the Markets in Financial Instruments Directive (MiFID II). Listed masterpieces will be displayed in museums around the world for all to appreciate.

Our regulated MTF will offer:
LIQUIDITY

Streaming liquidity via established market makers

TRADITIONAL ACCESS

Traditional order management (using Bloomberg and NYFix)

STRONG DUE DILIGENCE

The quality, provenance, and authenticity of each artwork is diligently checked by ARTEX using world-renowned experts

COMPLIANT REPORTING

Prospectuses following MiFID II rules are created for each artwork

HIGH TECHNICAL PERFORMANCE

An independently tested and high-performance matching engine.

INVEST IN ART
A Virtuous Ecosystem

Art is a tangible, physical asset. Investing in art can be viewed as investing in gold but with less volatility, and more social impact.
