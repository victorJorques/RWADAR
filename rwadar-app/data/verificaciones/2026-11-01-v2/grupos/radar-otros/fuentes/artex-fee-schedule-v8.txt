4. BILLING PROCESS

    1.     Unless agreed otherwise, the invoices must be paid within 30 days of the issue on the terms set out in the relevant
           invoice.
    2.     For the partial execution of an order within one day, the trading fee is charged only once.
    3.     Interest in arrears of 10% per annum may be charged on payments received late.

Currency and FX Conversion

    1.       All invoices will be issued and payable in EUR.

    2.       For any transactions executed in currencies other than EUR, amounts will be converted to EUR at the official
             European Central Bank (ECB) month end rate.



Reimbursement of fees

    1.       Any claim for the reimbursement of fees must be lodged within six months of the invoice date. Entitlement to
             reimbursement will lapse at the end of this period.
    2.       The claim for reimbursement must be supported by confirmation from the member’s auditors.

For any further queries, please email billing@artexgm.com
