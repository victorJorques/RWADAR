ARTEX GLOBAL MARKETS BENEFITS
Why Trade Art?
REGULATED

ARTEX is regulated by the Financial Market Authority (FMA) in Liechtenstein and compliant with the Markets in Financial Instruments Directive (MiFID II).

LIQUIDITY

ARTEX will bring much needed liquidity to the art market, making it quick and easy for investors to buy and sell shares in iconic masterpieces.

IMPACT

Listed masterpieces will be displayed in museums around the world for all to appreciate.

FRACTION

Through fractionalisation, ARTEX will enable with those of modest means to invest in world-renowned art works for the first time. Experienced investors will be able to gain exposure to a wider array of artists’ works with much smaller investment outlays.

ACCESS

ARTEX enables access to renowned artworks, empowering modest investors to participate, and providing experienced investors a wider selection. Explore diverse artist portfolios with low-barrier investment requirements.

SPREADS

Spreads (the difference between buying and selling prices) on ARTEX will be kept low, making art transaction costs highly competitive compared to galleries and auction houses.

SECURITY

ARTEX will provide similar investor protections as other regulated Multilateral Trading Facilities (MTF).
