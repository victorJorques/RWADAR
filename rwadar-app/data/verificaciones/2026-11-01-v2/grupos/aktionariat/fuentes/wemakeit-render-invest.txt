About wemakeit
Crowdfunding•Founded in 2012, Zurich, Switzerland
wemakeit was founded in Switzerland in 2012 and has quickly become the no. 1 platform in Switzerland and one of the largest crowdfunding platforms in Europe. Launched as a cultural initiative, wemakeit is now open to crowdfunding in all sectors and has established itself as an important financing and marketing tool for courageous creators of all kinds – for individuals, collectives, organisations and start-ups. In addition, since July 2022, wemakeit has been the first crowdfunding platform owned by the community: The more we are, the more wemakeit! Together with nearly 5,300 owners, wemakeit is even more successful, more innovative and more impactful.
