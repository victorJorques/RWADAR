4 Secondary Trading
At its sole discretion, the Company may enable and disable the possibility to trade Share
Tokens or Wrapped Tokens ("Traded Tokens"), using blockchain-based smart
contracts to settle trades between buyers and sellers without counterparty risk ("Trade Contracts"). The Trade Contracts are provided ‘as is’ and ‘as available’ in the spirit of a public
utility for shareholders of the Company and the User acknowledges and agrees that the Company
has only very limited control over the Trade Contracts. The Investor Page merely visualizes
the information available on the blockchain and proposes transactions which the User then can
independently communicate to the blockchain via its own wallet software. The Company can only
suspend trading and cancel stale trades. The applicable fees are shown on the Investor Page.
At no point has the Company any power of disposal over the Traded Tokens or the settlement
currency. It also cannot override the price limits electronically signed by the Users. Neither

an ongoing business relationship between the User and the Company. If the Company requires
identity verification for Secondary Trading, the User must complete it before placing orders.
Secondary Trades are settled in supported cryptocurrencies only. Bank transfers are not
available for Secondary Trading.
4.1 Order Matching and Price Determination
When entering an order, the User electronically signs structured data that includes an amount,
a limit price, and an expiration date ("Signed Constraints"). Unexpired and
funded orders are partially or fully executed as soon as there is a matching order on the
other side that results in the bid-ask-spread being crossed after taking fees into account (if
any) ("Secondary Trade"). Applicable fees are borne by the seller. If the
cross exceeds what is necessary to cover the fees, the settlement price is chosen for the
benefit of the more recent order. In case of multiple concurrent orders, the outcome of
submitting an order can deviate from what was indicated on the Investor Page. Matching Signed
Constraints are automatically routed to Trade Contracts for execution. The Trade Contracts are
programmed such that they cannot allow a violation of the Signed Constraints.
The Users of the Trade Contracts are free to conclude purchase and sale agreements bilaterally
outside of the Investor Page relating to the terms of the Secondary Trade, such as
representations and warranties relating to the Traded Tokens. Unless expressly stated
otherwise by the trading parties, the seller excludes all representations or warranties in
relation to the Traded Tokens.
4.2 Settlement and Delivery
Secondary Trades are settled in one atomic transaction, ensuring that the Traded Tokens cannot
be delivered without a payment and vice versa, eliminating the counterparty risk. The Traded
Tokens and the settlement currency are withdrawn from one party and delivered to the other
party within the same atomic transaction, to the respective address from which the order was
signed. It is the responsibility of the User to ensure that they are using a wallet that
supports the Traded Tokens as well as the settlement currency.
4.3 Fees
To cover its costs relating to the operation of the Investor Page and the setup and
maintenance of the Trade Contracts, the Company may charge a fee on Secondary Trades. The fees
are displayed on the Investor Page and directly subtracted from the settlement currency. For
purposes of accounting, the fees are deemed to be paid by the seller.
