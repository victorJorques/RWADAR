What is STX?
STX is a regulated digital Stock Exchange that connects companies and investors directly, quickly and securely using blockchain.
It supports native issuance, trading, settlement and custody of shares and bonds—security tokens—on blockchain, with full legal standing in Spain and the European Union.
Authorised as a Securities Agency by the CNMV and as a trading and settlement system (SNL) by ESMA.
