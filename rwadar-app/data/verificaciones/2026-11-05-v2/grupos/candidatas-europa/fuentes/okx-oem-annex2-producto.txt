6. Deposit and Withdrawal

6.1 Scaled Quantity Accounting

Tokenised Equities are represented on-chain in Token Quantity units. The OKX Platform displays and
processes UTS in Scaled Quantity (share-equivalent) units to provide a standard equity trading experience,
regardless of which Tokenised Equities is held by the User. The conversion is:




3
Scaled Quantity = Token Quantity × Multiplier (M)

On deposit: the applicable Underlying Token Quantity received by OEM as custodian for the User is
converted into Scaled Quantity using the then-current Multiplier, and the User’s UTS balance is credited
with that Scaled Quantity.

On withdrawal: the requested UTS Scaled Quantity is converted back into the Token Quantity of a
supported Underlying Token using the applicable Multiplier, and the resulting Underlying Token Quantity is
sent on-chain to the User’s withdrawal address, subject to the applicable issuer, network, compliance and
inventory requirements.

6.2 Multiplier Updates

The Multiplier may change over time as Corporate Actions occur (e.g., dividend payouts step up M; stock
splits adjust M). OEM will update M in its systems following validation against the Tokenised Equities
Issuer’s API and on-chain data. The Multiplier applicable at the time of deposit credit or withdrawal dispatch
governs the conversion for that transaction.

6.3 Edge Cases

    -   Deposits received during a Corporate Action processing window will be held in a queue and
        credited after the CA completes, using the post-CA M (M_new).
    -   Withdrawals submitted before a CA event and not cancelled by the User will be processed at the
        pre-CA M (M_old), as the Token Quantity is fixed at the time of submission.
    -   Withdrawals that are cancelled after a CA event will be returned to the user at the post-CA M
        (M_new).
    -   Deposits of Tokenised Equities to OEM where OEM does not support that particular Tokenised
        Equities will be credited but the User will be restricted to withdrawal only and may not trade the
        balance.


6.4 Standard Checks

All standard on-chain controls applicable to digital asset deposits and withdrawals on the OKX Platform
apply to UTS deposits and withdrawals, including on-chain analytics screening, travel rule compliance, and
applicable risk and compliance checks. No special exemptions apply to UTS.

6.5 Reconciliation

A reconciliation check is performed on every deposit credit and withdrawal dispatch. If a discrepancy is
detected between the Underlying Token holdings maintained by the relevant OEM and the aggregate UTS
balances recorded as contractual entitlements on the OKX Platform, the relevant credit or withdrawal will
be held until the discrepancy is resolved.

9. Corporate Actions

9.1 Dividend Corporate Actions

When a Tokenised Equities Issuer processes a dividend on an Underlying Asset, the Multiplier is adjusted
upward to reflect the economic value of the dividend being reinvested into the token (M steps up). OKX will:

- pause deposit crediting, withdrawal processing, and internal transfers for the affected Unified Tokenized
Stock during the CA processing window; trading is not halted for dividend CAs; although trading may be
paused when processing large dividends;

- compute a per-user share adjustment: new_share = floor8(old_share × M_new / M_old), and credit the
delta to each User’s UTS balance as an account adjustment;

- apply the adjustment to all UTS User accounts without exclusion (including accounts subject to compliance
holds or KYC holds — the adjustment reflects economic entitlement and does not alter any hold or freeze
status);

- resume paused services once reconciliation is complete.

You acknowledge that dividend Corporate Actions do not involve a cash distribution — economic value is
reflected through the Multiplier adjustment and corresponding share quantity increase.

Management and Investor Fee: Each Tokenised Equities is subject to an annual Management Fee as
specified in the applicable Final Terms. The Management Fee is calculated and deducted daily at 12:00
pm UTC by reducing the Multiplier. The Management Fee is an Issuer-side fee, not an OKX Platform fee,
and is embedded in the price of each Underlying Token. Separate issuance and redemption fees ("Investor
Fees") may apply to primary market transactions (subscriptions and redemptions directly with the Issuer
via OKX as authorised participant for the Tokenised Equities). Investor Fees are Issuer-side fees and are
embedded in the quoted prices for primary market transactions.
