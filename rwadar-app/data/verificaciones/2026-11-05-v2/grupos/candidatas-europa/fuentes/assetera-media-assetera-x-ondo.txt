Assetera Lists 450+ Ondo Tokenized Stocks and ETFs for Eligible Investors Across 30 EEA countries
Eligible European investors can now gain exposure to US equities via tokens issued by Ondo Global Markets (BVI) Limited and made available via Assetera's Austrian MiFID II license. Each token is backed by the stocks or ETFs it tracks, held with US-regulated custodians, and reviewed daily by an independent verification agent.
Announcement
07 Oct 2026
Vienna, Austria, 7 October
Assetera has listed 100+ Ondo’s tokenized US stocks and ETFs issued by Ondo Global Markets (BVI) Limited, expanding access to eligible investors in all 30 EEA countries. This list will widen to the full catalogue of 450+ assets through Q4 of 2026. Assetera, licensed by the Austrian Financial Market Authority (FMA), makes the tokens available to its clients through its MiFID II-regulated platform.
Collateral, Custody, and Redemption
Each token is backed by corresponding underlying stocks or ETFs. Ondo’s BVI issuer holds those underlying stocks and ETFs with a US-registered broker-dealer, an independent verification agent reviews the collateral daily, and a security agent holds a first-priority security interest in the underlying for the benefit of token holders. Eligible, Ondo-onboarded investors mint and redeem the tokens directly against that collateral rather than depending on secondary market liquidity, 24/5 across the catalogue and 24/7 for the most heavily traded assets.
Since launching in September 2025, Ondo Stocks has surpassed $1 billion in total value locked and processed more than $27 billion in cumulative trading volume. Its catalogue now includes more than 450 tokenized stocks and ETFs.
Issuance on One Side, Regulated Distribution on the Other
In partnership, different responsibilities are clearly divided between the two companies. Ondo issues the tokens, uses a U.S. registered broker-dealer to custody the underlying securities, and operates minting and redemption. Assetera handles distribution to its own clients through its regulated platform, and provides access for the B2B partners that use its infrastructure to offer tokenized securities under their own brand.
“European investors want exposure to US equities, and until now the regulated routes to it have been narrow. Ondo built the catalogue and the collateral structure behind it. We bring the license that lets retail and professional clients across 30 countries access it under supervision they already recognize.”
‍
Thomas Labenbacher
, Chief Executive Officer, Assetera
“Expanding access to Ondo Stocks across Europe is an important step in bringing global capital markets onchain. Assetera’s regulated infrastructure gives eligible investors and institutions across the EEA a compliant way to access a broad range of tokenized U.S. stocks and ETFs. Together, we’re making institutional-grade, onchain market access available through the platforms and infrastructure European investors already use.”
‍
Oya Celiktemur
, Sales Director, EMEA, Ondo Finance
Tokenized equities carry risks, including price volatility, the potential loss of capital, and risks specific to digital assets and blockchain infrastructure. Availability and eligibility vary by jurisdiction, and investors should consider these factors before investing.
About Assetera
Assetera
 is a regulated platform for the distribution of tokenized securities and real-world assets. Licensed by the Austrian Financial Market Authority (FMA) as a MiFID II investment firm, Assetera distributes tokenized financial instruments directly to investors on its own platform and offers the same regulated infrastructure to partners, enabling them to distribute across up to 30 EEA countries under Assetera's license. In addition, Assetera provides regulated access to secondary-market liquidity for public and private market tokens and offers this capability to partners as well.
