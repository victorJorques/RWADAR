1. WHAT IS THIS PRODUCT?
Type:                 Open-ended tracker certificate in the form of Ledger-Based Securities (Registerwertrechte) according to Art.
                      973d et seqq. Swiss Code of Obligations (“CO”) under Swiss law.
Term                  The Product (as defined below) does not have a fixed maturity date.
Objectives:            STXon (hereinafter the “Product”) is a tracker certificate issued on the Ethereum Mainnet and other
                       blockchain networks which the Issuer may choose to issue on in its sole discretion, a Securities Ledger
                       supported by the Product. The Product is engineered to mirror the price movements of the ordinary shares of
                       SEAGATE TECHNOLOGY HOLDINGS PUBLIC LIMITED COMPANY (“Underlying”). The fundamental
                       investment goal of the Product is to replicate the return performance of the Underlying, after the relevant fees
                       and expenses. The Product offers several key advantages, such as faster transaction speeds compared to
                       traditional securities, and it is specifically developed for offering the Investors exposure to the performance of
                       the Underlying while simultaneously leveraging the advantages of the blockchain technology such as

3. WHAT HAPPENS IF THE ISSUER IS UNABLE TO PAY OUT?
The focus of the Issuer’s business activities is the issuance of Product directly or indirectly linked to the Underlying. The Investors have
recourse only to the collateralised relevant Underlying in respect of the Product, and not to any other assets of the Issuer or any service
provider of the Product.
The Product is collateralized through the purchase of the Underlying. When the Investor buys the Product, the Issuer uses the
proceeds, minus the costs, to finance the purchase of the Underlying. The value of the Collateral at any point in time must correspond at
least to the value of the Product at that time calculated accordingly as specified within the Base Prospectus. The Investors have a
primary claim to the Collateral allocated to the Product. In the event the Collateral is insufficient, then the Investors have a claim to any
Additional Collateral as detailed within the Base Prospectus
The Product is neither insured nor guaranteed by any government, regulator or agency. Accordingly, the Investor has no residual claim

5. HOW LONG SHOULD I HOLD IT, AND CAN I TAKE MONEY OUT EARLY?
The Product does not have a fixed maturity date. The recommended holding period is 5 years.
You may place a redemption order for the Product with the Issuer, or any party acting on behalf of the Issuer, at any time. The redemption
can be settled in cash, and/or at the sole discretion of the Issuer, optionally also in other fiat currencies or cryptocurrencies. You may also
sell the Product in the secondary market to third parties (“OTC”) subject to the transfer restrictions set forth in the Base Prospectus and
the Final Terms.
The Product at the moment is neither listed nor admitted to trading at any stock exchange, authorized multilateral trading facility or
organized trading facility (inside or outside of the EU/Switzerland). Should the Issuer resolve to proceed with a listing or an admission to
trading on any stock exchange, authorised multilateral trading facility (“MTF”), or organised trading facility (“OTF”), it shall notify this on
its website: https://ondo.finance/global-markets
In unusual market situations, or in the event of technical faults/disruptions, a purchase and/or sale of the Product can be
temporarily hindered or may not be possible at all.
