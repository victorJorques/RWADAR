1.11 Daily Compression
1.11.1
 Nature of the exercise:
 Where You hold more than one open transaction in the same financial instrument, We may combine those positions into a single transaction by terminating them and creating a replacement transaction in their place (a “
Compression
”). A Compression reduces the number of contracts outstanding between You and Us and the counterparty credit and operational risk associated with them. It does not change the size of Your overall exposure and it does not change Your profit or loss at the time it is carried out.
1.11.2
 Standing Authority: 
You authorise Us to carry out a Compression on each Business Day, in respect of all of Your eligible open positions. We may carry out a Compression without further instruction from You, and no separate proposal, agreement or documentation is required in respect of any individual Compression.
1.11.3
 Eligible Positions: 
Only positions in the same financial instrument and having identical material contract specifications, including the same underlying, expiry date, settlement currency, contract multiplier, settlement method and price quotation, may be included in the same Compression. Positions that do not satisfy all of those criteria will not be combined. Long and short positions in the same instrument may be combined, with quantities taken as net.
1.11.4
 Price of the Replacement Position: 
The price of the replacement transaction is the quantity-weighted average price of the positions it replaces. Long quantities are treated as positive and short quantities as negative, and the price is calculated as the sum of the price multiplied by the signed quantity of each position, divided by the net signed quantity, rounded to the tick size of the relevant instrument. If the net signed quantity is zero, the original positions are terminated without creating a replacement transaction and any fixed profit or loss arising from their termination is credited or debited to Your account, as applicable. Any economic difference resulting from rounding is credited or debited to Your account as a cash adjustment. From the time of the Compression, the calculated price applies to the replacement transaction for all purposes, including margin, valuation, settlement and the calculation of Your profit or loss.
1.11.5
 No Change to Your Economic Position: 
The financial instruments to which this clause applies have a payoff which varies in direct proportion to the price of the underlying. Subject to the settlement and cash adjustments expressly described in clause 1.11.4, the replacement transaction accordingly produces the same aggregate economic result as the positions it replaces and a Compression does not transfer economic value between You and Us. Any amount credited or debited under clause 1.11.4, and any amounts already accrued, will be identified separately in Your account statement.
1.11.6
 Effect on Your Account: 
Following a Compression: (a) the replacement transaction carries a new transaction identifier; (b) any subsequent partial closure is applied against the replacement transaction and not against the original positions; (c) financing and other periodic charges are calculated by reference to the replacement position; (d) any stop-loss, take-profit or other conditional order attached to an original transaction will be re-attached to the replacement transaction on equivalent terms or, where that is not possible, cancelled and notified to You; and (e) margin requirements are recalculated on the replacement position.
1.11.7
 Your Trade History: 
The execution price, quantity and time of each original transaction remain available to You in Your trade history and on request, and We will retain those records in accordance with Our record-keeping obligations. Confirmations previously issued in respect of the original transactions are not affected by a Compression.
1.11.8

Daily Compression Statement
