Frequently Asked Questions
What do I invest in, and how are prices determined?
1X offers RWA security tokens backed by assets such as public equities, money market funds, gold funds, debt instruments and real estate, as well as private equity listings. Prices on 1X’s MAS-regulated secondary market are determined by supply and demand, with trades executed when buy and sell orders match. Underlying asset values, such as stock prices or fund NAVs, may serve as reference points.

Who can invest on 1X?
Investors in Singapore must qualify as Accredited Investors or Institutional Investors. Overseas investors may also be eligible, subject to local regulations and 1X’s onboarding requirements.
What payment options are available on 1X?
You can fund your account via bank transfer in SGD or USD, or through on-chain transfers of USDT or USDC to your 1X wallet address. Deposited funds can be used to buy and trade tokens on the platform.
What are the trading hours?
Trading on 1X is available 24/7, with orders executed when matching buy and sell orders are available. Fiat deposits and withdrawals are processed on Singapore business days and may take longer outside banking hours.
