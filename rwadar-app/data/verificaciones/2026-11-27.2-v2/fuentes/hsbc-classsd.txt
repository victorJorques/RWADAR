HSBC RedCoin expands into wealth with tokenised fund access
Minimum subscription of HKD 100 (funded with RCHKD 100)1 via HSBC HK Mobile Banking App, with potentially faster settlement2

Hong Kong, 19 November 2026 — HSBC RedCoin (Ticker: RCHKD), HSBC’s Hong Kong dollar-denominated stablecoin, is now available on the HSBC HK Mobile Banking App (the App).

Eligible customers will now be able to activate their stablecoin wallet in the App and use RCHKD to access a new tokenised unit class (Class SD)3 of the HSBC Global Money Funds – Hong Kong Dollar (the Fund)4. By embedding tokenised funds into the App which customers are already familiar with, HSBC RedCoin is now extended from day-to-day payments into wealth management.

[nombre retirado], Head of Wealth and Premier Solutions, Hong Kong, HSBC, said: “Tokenised funds reduce traditional market friction and enable faster settlement. By bringing HSBC RedCoin together with tokenised investments, we are creating new ways for our wealth customers to access innovative investment opportunities with a lower entry threshold and a more efficient experience. This is an important step in broadening our digital wealth proposition, and we will continue to expand the range of tokenised investment products available to our customers.”

Launched in 1991, the Fund is managed by HSBC Asset Management. Its dividend distributions are separate from HSBC RedCoin, which pays no interest.

[nombre retirado], CEO, HSBC Asset Management, Hong Kong, said: “We are delighted to launch a tokenised unit class of our long-standing Hong Kong dollar money market fund, offering investors a new digital gateway to an established fund. This launch marks an exciting step forward, and we look forward to continuing our collaboration with all stakeholders to support the development of the digital asset investment ecosystem and delivering a more diverse range of solutions to our customers as the market evolves.”

The launch responds to clear customer interest in practical applications for digital assets. HSBC’s Hong Kong stablecoin study found that digital asset trading and tokenised investments were the most widely recognised use case, cited by 57 per cent of respondents.

HSBC’s Securities Services tokenises the new unit class, while its Digital Assets Custody capability provides safekeeping services for HSBC RedCoin and the tokenised unit class (Class SD) of the Fund.

For more information, please visit: https://www.hsbc.com.hk/investments/products/mutual-funds/guide-to-tokenised-funds/
