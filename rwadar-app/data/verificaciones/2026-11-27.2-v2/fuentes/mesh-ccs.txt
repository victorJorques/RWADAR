The Investment Case

South African SMEs face a persistent funding gap.

Many businesses need productive assets to grow but don’t meet traditional bank lending criteria. That creates a structural opportunity for specialist lenders like CCS.

CCS finances secured, income-generating assets and manages those transactions through disciplined underwriting and active portfolio management.

For investors, this creates access to a private credit strategy where returns are driven by operational cash flow rather than market movements.
