## How do I get started, and who can trade?

Trading is supported using third party self-custody wallets only. To be enabled for trading you are required to set up a Securitize account (and go through the KYC process) and once approved, connect your third party wallet. Once this process is completed, Securitize will whitelist your wallet address, allowing this wallet to interact with the platform. Securities can only move between whitelisted wallets on the platform.

## What fees does the platform charge?

The platform charges a fee of .10% of trade amount in USDC. The platform fee covers any gas charges associated with the execution.
