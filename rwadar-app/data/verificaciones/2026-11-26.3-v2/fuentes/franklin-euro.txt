Franklin Templeton Launches Tokenised Euro Money Market Fund

New Franklin OnChain Euro Liquidity Fund extends established Benji suite into euro-denominated money market investments

Luxembourg – 23 November 2026 – Franklin Templeton1, a global investment leader and pioneer in digital assets technology, today announced the launch of the Franklin OnChain Euro Liquidity Fund2, a Luxembourg-registered UCITS SICAV under its Franklin Templeton OnChain Funds (FTOCF) umbrella. The new Fund will be available to retail investors in Austria, France, Germany, Luxembourg, the Netherlands, Portugal and Spain, and to institutional investors in Italy and Switzerland.

The Franklin OnChain Euro Liquidity Fund is a euro-denominated, short-term Low Volatility Net Asset Value (LVNAV) money market fund, designed for investors seeking capital preservation, liquidity and returns in line with euro money market rates.

The new Fund’s objective is to maintain capital value while seeking to produce a return to the investor in line with Euro money market rates and to maintain a low volatility Net Asset Value of EUR 1 per Share. It will aim to achieve this by investing in a diversified portfolio of high-quality eligible money market instruments denominated in euro or fully hedged into euro. These include short-term debt securities, commercial paper and certificates of deposit alongside eligible deposits and reverse repurchase agreements.

The launch marks a further step in Franklin Templeton's expansion of its digital assets capabilities in Europe as investors increasingly explore how tokenisation can support liquidity management, asset transfers and other institutional financial workflows. The new Fund complements the Luxembourg-domiciled Franklin OnChain U.S. Government Liquidity Fund which launched in February 2025.

Matthew Harrison, Head of EMEA and Americas (ex-US), Franklin Templeton commented: “European investors are looking for more choice in how they access and manage their liquidity. The launch of the Franklin OnChain Euro Liquidity Fund extends our Benji offering into euros, bringing our money market expertise to investors in their local currency. By combining the familiarity and established investment disciplines of a regulated euro money market fund with tokenised ownership, we are making this technology relevant to the way investors manage their liquidity today and supporting the evolving needs of our clients across Europe.”

Combining traditional liquidity management with blockchain technology

Through Franklin Templeton's proprietary Benji technology platform, the Fund uses blockchain-integrated transfer agency infrastructure to record share ownership and transactions enabling eligible investors to transfer fund shares directly between approved digital wallets.

Unlike traditional fund structures, where income is often distributed periodically, the Fund will distribute and reinvest income daily, 365 days a year. Its leading capabilities include 24/7 peer-to-peer (P2P) transfers of shares between approved wallets and Intraday Yield, which allocates income accrued by the second according to how long investors hold shares during a NAV cycle, including when shares are transferred.

These capabilities are designed for investors seeking short-term euro money market exposure alongside blockchain-enabled ownership and transfer capabilities. The Fund can support corporate treasury management, institutional funding arrangements and intercompany transfers. Subject to regulatory and operational requirements, potential applications also include collateral and settlement workflows.

Sandy Kaul, Head of Innovation, Franklin Templeton commented: “Tokenisation is about much more than putting traditional investment products on a blockchain. It has the potential to fundamentally change how financial assets are held, transferred and used across the financial ecosystem.

“With the Franklin OnChain Euro Liquidity Fund we're bringing this technology into euro liquidity management, enabling investors to transfer fund shares between approved wallets while income is allocated according to how long those shares are held. This is a practical example of how tokenisation can make financial assets more flexible and open up new possibilities for institutional liquidity management.”

Building on Franklin Templeton's Benji platform

The Fund is powered by the Benji Technology Platform, Franklin Templeton’s proprietary blockchain-integrated stack, designed to facilitate trading, management, and administration of token-based investments. Using this platform, Franklin Templeton launched the world’s first U.S. registered mutual fund in 2021 using blockchain-integrated technology to process transactions and record share ownership. The platform is available to clients via several tokenized money market funds launched by Franklin Templeton for a variety of global institutional and retail use cases - fully on-chain, without parallel recordkeeping.

Franklin Templeton is a pioneer in digital asset investing and blockchain innovation, combining tokenomics research, data science, and technical expertise to deliver cutting-edge solutions since 2018.
