Access. Transact. Move capital. Powered by tokenization.

The BlackRock HKD Digital Liquidity Fund offers a simple, modern way to manage cash. It combines money market investing with new technology, allowing convenient access through traditional and digital channels.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION:

i. BlackRock HKD Digital Liquidity Fund seeks to achieve its investment objective by investing in short-term, high-quality money market instruments issued or guaranteed by a government, its public or local authorities or other multilateral agencies, as well as overnight or term deposits. Deposits will be placed with high-quality financial institutions with an investment grade or equivalent rating. The Investment Manager has discretion to select the Fund’s investments and is not constrained by any benchmark. The Fund is a “Constant Net Asset Value” Money Market Fund as defined by the Securities and Futures Commission (SFC).

Three key benefits of the BlackRock HKD Digital Liquidity Fund
Income potential with stability at the core
Put cash to work in high-quality, short-term HKD money market instruments, with a strategy that seeks income while keeping liquidity and stability as its core objective.
Greater choice in access and transactions
Use traditional or digital channels, with conventional HKD cash or eligible digital cash, which may include Hong Kong regulated stablecoins.
Streamlined processes with tokenization
For tokenized units, blockchain-based records can simplify ownership tracking and help reduce manual processing, supporting greater transparency and operational efficiency.

What is the BlackRock HKD Digital Liquidity Fund?

The BlackRock HKD Digital Liquidity Fund is a Hong Kong dollar-denominated money market strategy designed to provide investors with access to short-term liquidity management through a digitally enabled fund structure. The fund combines traditional money market investing with digital asset infrastructure to support institutional use cases in evolving financial markets.
