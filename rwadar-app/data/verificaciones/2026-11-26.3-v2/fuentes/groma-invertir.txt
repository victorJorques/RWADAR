TECHNOLOGY

Meet Grobot

Grobot is our purpose-built technology platform for small-format multifamily real estate. Grobot helps us buy smart, upgrade efficiently, and helps drive sustained yield growth through optimized operations.

Acquire
With 1000s of properties to pick from, but finite capital and time, Grobot ensures we focus on the “right” ones efficiently at scale.

‍Grobot uses first-and third-party data to filter out the ~95% of properties that don’t meet our criteria.
Standardize
Grobot uses our operating history and optimized material set to upgrade each unit to Groma Grade.
‍
Consistency of assets ensures Grobot, and our human team, can access, diagnose, and fix efficiently.
Operate
Grobot is a multi-lingual, talented property manager. It has all of the context that it needs to address most problems.
‍
Human feedback (from our residents and our technicians) helps it learn what works and what doesn’t so it’s always improving.
