The Offering

RNCRrev is a tokenized real estate investment that allows investors to participate in the projected profits of the Renacer Condominio development.

The name Renacer reflects the broader transformation taking place in Soyapango. Once known primarily for its challenges, the district is now undergoing a visible process of renewal, with improving conditions in security, infrastructure, commerce, and urban mobility. This shift is driving new confidence, investment, and long-term development across the area.

For thousands of working families, Soyapango has always been a strategic place to live — close to jobs, connected to the economic center of San Salvador, and anchored in daily economic activity. Today, that same foundation is being reshaped by a new cycle of urban recovery.

This demand supports the sale of units and the generation of returns for investors:

sustained population density and urban growth
a structural shortage of accessible housing
proximity to San Salvador’s economic center
increasing investment and development activity in the area

Renacer Condominio is positioned within this context: a modern residential development designed for middle-income families seeking accessible, well-located housing in a high-demand corridor.

How this investment works

Each token represents a proportional economic right to distributions tied to the financial outcome of the Renacer Condominio development, as defined in the issuance documentation. Investors participate through the TOHKN app by purchasing tokens.

Capital is used to support the construction and development of the project. Returns are generated from the sale of residential units. Investors receive a proportional economic right to distributions derived from the project's performance, as defined in the issuance documentation.

Based on projected unit sales, the project targets an estimated internal rate of return (IRR) of 15-20%.

The investment is directly tied to the execution of the development, the pace of unit sales, and the overall financial outcome of the project.

RNCRrev does not represent ownership of the property or individual apartments. Instead, it provides structured exposure to the results of the development.

Returns are distributed once project obligations are met and units are commercialized. This depends on successful execution. There are no fixed payments, and returns are not guaranteed.

Investors should carefully review the Relevant Information Document (DIR) and official offering documents before investing.
