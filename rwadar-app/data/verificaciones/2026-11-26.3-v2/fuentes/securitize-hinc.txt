Overview

For Investors Seeking High Current Income Through Institutionally Managed Credit

The Neuberger Securitize High Current Income Tokenized Fund provides exposure to U.S. high yield bonds and high-yielding fixed income instruments including CLOs and bank loans, with global asset manager, Neuberger Berman, fully responsible for sourcing, underwriting, and selecting all portfolio investments, leveraging its deep credit expertise and emphasis on fundamental credit analysis across primary and secondary markets.

High Current Income Strategy



Access institutional fixed-income markets through a professionally managed portfolio.

Tokenized Access & Liquidity



Daily subscription and redemption requests are accepted (subject to a 2:30pm ET Cut-Off and a 24-hour Lock-Up), and redemptions aim to support a T+1 settlement target.

Active Credit Selection



Neuberger Berman’s fundamental credit analysis targets high income with potential for capital appreciation.

Diversified High-Income Strategy



Combining high yield bonds with CLOs and bank loans diversifies sources of credit exposure and income within a single, professionally managed portfolio.
