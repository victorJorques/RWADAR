The Offering

MMLTI1 is the first tranche of MultiMoney's MMY issuance programme (the “Issuance Programme”), designed for investors seeking access to a fixed-rate debt investment tied to a regulated financial institution in El Salvador.

MultiMoney is a savings and credit institution incorporated in 2013, supervised by the Superintendency of the Financial System (SSF) and regulated by the Central Reserve Bank of El Salvador. It offers financial products and services including deposit-taking, lending, and the administration of financial operations.

Its business model is oriented toward fast, accessible, technology-enabled financial solutions, with an emphasis on serving individuals and segments that need efficient financing alternatives.

MMLTI1, as the First Tranche of the MMY Issuance Programme, is part of MultiMoney's strategy to strengthen and expand its credit portfolio, allowing investors to participate through a regulated digital debt instrument available on TOHKN.

Through MMLTI1, investors acquire debt tokens representing a credit claim against MultiMoney. This entitles holders to receive principal and interest payments from MultiMoney under the terms and conditions of the Issuance Programme.

The MMLTI1 first tranche (the “First Tranche”) carries a 6.20% fixed annual interest rate, monthly interest payments, and a 24-month term. Principal is due at maturity, except in the early redemption cases defined in the Relevant Information Document (DIR) and the issuance programme agreements, which may be exercised at the Issuer's option only.

Proceeds will be used to strengthen, expand, and develop MultiMoney's ordinary financial intermediation operations, including the origination, granting, administration, and expansion of its credit portfolio.

Investing involves risk. Always review the DIR and the official offering documents before investing.

How MMLTI1 works

Each MMLTI1 token has a reference value of $100. When you invest through the TOHKN app, you acquire MMLTI1 debt tokens representing a credit claim against MultiMoney.

The tokens do not represent equity in MultiMoney or in its assets, and grant no voting rights, corporate rights, or direct ownership rights over the Issuer's assets.

Transfers and secondary market activity, if enabled, are subject to eligibility verification, KYC, KYB, KYT, and AML controls, and to the restrictions described in the official documents, including a 180-day trading restriction period from token acquisition. Liquidity is not guaranteed.

MMLTI1 pays interest monthly. Interest accrues from the effective token issuance date and is paid in arrears, successively, under the official terms of the issuance programme.

For the First Tranche, interest is payable on the last day of each month.

Principal is paid at the end of the 24-month term, unless an early redemption process applies at the Issuer's option under the DIR.

Payments are made in USDT or USDC through the TOHKN app.
