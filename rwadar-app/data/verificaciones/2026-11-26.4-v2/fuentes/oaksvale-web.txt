FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Answers about the Oaksvale DAO Platform.
How do I purchase and co-own a property on the Oaksvale platform?

Visit the Oaksvale DAO Platform and sign up using your email address. After verifying your account, you can browse available properties. Before purchase, you'll complete a brief KYC process for legal ownership allocation. Once verified, fund your wallet and invest in a property to receive property-backed digital ownership units.

What currency can I use to buy into a property?

All property purchases on Oaksvale are settled in USDC stablecoin. You fund your Oaksvale wallet with USDC and use that balance to acquire property units in any active property sale on the platform.

What happens after I join a property DAO?

Once you buy into a property, you'll receive property-backed digital ownership units representing your share in the underlying property. You gain rights to your share of rental income distributions and governance participation, allowing you to vote on key property-related decisions.

How do I track my property earnings?

You can track your earnings from your Oaksvale dashboard. It gives you real-time visibility into the properties you co-own, including rental income, operating expenses, net returns, booking activity, and your available USDC balance, all in one place.

Can I invest in a property alone through Oaksvale?

Oaksvale is built for fractional co-ownership. Each property is divided into ownership units that are acquired by multiple verified investors. However, if you wish to acquire all available units in a Property DAO yourself, you are free to do so.

Can I sell or exit my property units?

Yes. Today, investors exit through a collective sale: any DAO member can table a proposal to sell the property, and if the vote passes, the property is sold and the proceeds are distributed among all investors. Individual unit-to-unit trading through the Oaksvale Marketplace is coming soon and will be enabled as the legal framework matures.

Who manages the day-to-day operation of the property?

Oaksvale operates the tokenized assets directly. In every jurisdiction where a property is located, Oaksvale sets up a dedicated property management subsidiary that handles day-to-day operations, maintenance, leasing, vendor coordination, and reporting. This local Oaksvale entity is the operational manager of the asset and is responsible for executing the collective decisions of the DAO governance, ensuring smooth, accountable, and compliant running of the property.

What is the legal structure behind property DAOs?

Each property DAO is anchored to a dedicated Special Purpose Vehicle (SPV) that legally owns the underlying property. Investor participation is structured through legally binding agreements tied to the SPV, while property units recorded on-chain represent economic interests, governance rights, and ownership records. Operationally, the property is run by an Oaksvale property management subsidiary established in the asset's jurisdiction, which executes DAO resolutions on behalf of unit holders. This ensures every digital unit is connected to enforceable real-world ownership and a clear operational manager.
