FAQ
Frequently asked questions
What is Cradle?

Cradle is a regulated, on-chain marketplace for African real-world assets — equities, bonds, local-currency stablecoins, and tokenized fund offerings. It bundles three apps (Issuer, Marketplace, Launchpad) onto a shared compliance and settlement rail.

How is Cradle different from offshore tokenization platforms?

Cradle is built around African listings and settles in local-currency stablecoins (NGN, KES, GHS). No offshore detour, no FX leakage, no foreign jurisdictional friction layered on top of local trading.

What token standard do you use?

ERC-3643 (T-REX) — permissioned tokens with on-chain identity and compliance hooks. Trades only settle between verified, jurisdictionally-eligible parties, without sacrificing the composability of an ERC-20-like surface.

Who can issue an asset?

Issuers complete a 7-step onboarding wizard covering entity verification, beneficial ownership, KYB documents, and compliance attestations. Once approved, you can declare assets, confirm custody, and request token mints — all from the Issuer console.

Where do trading fees go?

Issuers receive 70% of every trade fee on the assets they originate. Splits happen at the smart-contract level on every fill — accrued fees are claimable directly from the Issuer console with no invoicing or reconciliation.

When do you launch?

The Marketplace, Issuer console, and Launchpad are in active development. Join the waitlist below to be notified when we open up new jurisdictions and asset listings.
