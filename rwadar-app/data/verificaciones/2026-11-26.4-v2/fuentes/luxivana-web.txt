DIGITAL TWIN TECHNOLOGY

YOUR ASSET. YOUR TOKEN.
YOUR DIGITAL TWIN.

When you invest through Luxivana, a digital twin of your legal ownership is created on the blockchain. It is not a separate asset — it is an immutable, real-time digital mirror of your interest in the underlying asset trust, always in sync with the real world.

ONE TOKEN = ONE LEGAL INTEREST

EVERY TOKEN ISSUED BY LUXIVANA IS BACKED 1:1 BY A REAL-WORLD ASSET HELD WITHIN A REGULATED AUSTRALIAN MANAGED INVESTMENT SCHEME.

ALWAYS IN SYNC WITH THE REAL WORLD

AS THE REAL-WORLD ASSET GENERATES INCOME, REACHES MILESTONES, OR CHANGES IN VALUE, THE ON-CHAIN RECORD UPDATES AUTOMATICALLY. YOUR DIGITAL TWIN IS ALWAYS AN ACCURATE REFLECTION OF WHAT YOU OWN.

TRANSPARENT AND VERIFIABLE

ANY INVESTOR, AUDITOR, OR REGULATOR CAN VERIFY THE EXISTENCE AND STATUS OF THE DIGITAL TWIN ON-CHAIN AT ANY TIME.
