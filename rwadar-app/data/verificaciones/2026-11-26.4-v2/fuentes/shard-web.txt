What is Shard?

Shard is a real-world asset investment platform that makes it easier for you to invest in carefully selected opportunities across real estate, agriculture, and fixed income. You can own a share of valuable assets without having to purchase them outright.

How does Shard work?

Simply create an account, verify your identity, and explore available investment opportunities. Choose an asset, review its details, and invest an amount that works for you. Once your investment is confirmed, you can track your holdings and any earnings directly from your dashboard.

What exactly am I investing in?

Shard offers access to real-world investment opportunities, including real estate, agriculture, and fixed income. Each opportunity comes with information about the underlying asset, expected returns, risks, and investment terms, so you know what you’re putting your money into.

How do I make money from my investment?

Your returns depend on the investment you choose. You may earn rental income from properties, interest from fixed-income investments, or returns from agricultural projects. Some assets may also increase in value over time. Returns are not guaranteed, and every opportunity has its own risks.

How does Shard verify the assets on its platform?

We take asset verification seriously. Before an investment is made available, it goes through due diligence, including relevant ownership checks, legal reviews, and financial assessments. Where applicable, we also work with government agencies and independent professionals to verify asset records and documentation.

How do I know my investment is secure?

Shard is built around three layers of trust: asset verification, legal documentation, and blockchain-backed records. Together, these help establish what you’re investing in, document your rights, and maintain a transparent record of your holdings. Like every investment, however, your capital remains subject to risk.

Can I withdraw my investment at any time?

Every investment has its own duration and exit terms. Some require you to stay invested for a set period, so you may not be able to withdraw early. You’ll see these details before investing, allowing you to choose opportunities that fit your financial plans.

Can Nigerians living abroad invest on Shard?

Shard is designed to make Nigerian investment opportunities accessible to investors at home and abroad. Eligibility, identity verification, payment options, and any country-specific restrictions will depend on the investment and applicable regulations.
