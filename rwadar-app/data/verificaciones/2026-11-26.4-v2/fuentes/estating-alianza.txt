Estating and Reental sign strategic partnership connecting institutional infrastructure and tokenised investment rails
October 26, 2026

From real asset to token holder

Estating and Reental sign strategic partnership connecting institutional infrastructure and tokenised investment rails

The partnership formalises six transactions already executed together, and builds on six years in which each firm has developed its own rails. Estating becomes Reental’s exclusive product partner for institutional-grade real estate, and Reental becomes Estating’s exclusive platform partner for tokenised retail investment in Latin America and Southern Europe. Institutional-grade real estate, sold until now through wealth managers, becomes the underlying of tokens for retail investors. Estating remains responsible for the securities and works only business to business. Reental issues and offers its tokenized securities to its community of investors

Luxembourg and Madrid, 26th October 2026 — Estating S.A., the Luxembourg-domiciled infrastructure platform for real-world assets, and Reental, the Spanish-US platform for tokenised real estate investment, today announced a strategic partnership. It connects their rails into a single chain that runs from the real estate asset to the token holder. Estating becomes Reental’s exclusive product partner for institutional-grade real estate investments. Reental becomes Estating’s exclusive platform partner for their tokenised retail business across Latin America and Southern Europe. The partnership builds on six transactions the two firms have already executed together.

One chain, from asset to token holder

The chain starts with established owner-operators and sponsors, who normally transact only with institutional counterparties. Estating curates and structures each investment and issues it as a bankable, ISIN-bearing security through its Luxembourg securitisation vehicle. Reental subscribes that security as a professional counterparty and uses it as the underlying asset of its own token, which it issues and places with its investors. On Reental’s platform, token holders can then take up the liquidity options and leverage that DeFi (decentralized finance) offers. Estating remains accountable for the underlying security over its whole life, covering servicing, reporting and valuation and, where required, restructuring, refinancing or an orderly exit.

Each firm is responsible for its own part of the chain, and each has done this work at scale for six years.

Two established rails

Estating’s institutional rails. On the asset side are owner-operators and sponsors with multi-billion portfolios. At the centre is an owned Luxembourg securitisation vehicle with Swiss paying agency. On the distribution side are some thirty regulated wealth management partners, together with private banks and family offices, serving their clients business to business.

Reental’s retail rails. Reental has a direct client base of approximately 50,000 users in some 100 countries and a tokenisation platform built around them. Its features include accessible investment tickets and integration with decentralised tools that give investors new ways to manage their assets, unlock liquidity, compound returns and leverage their holdings through RNT Lend, a leading lending protocol for tokenised real estate.

Proven together

The two firms have worked together for two years. In that time they have executed six transactions with four sponsors across four asset types: multifamily, mixed-use development and value-add hospitality in the United States, and land-bank lending in the Dominican Republic. One of those issues cleared within minutes of being presented to Reental’s investors. The partnership now gives that cooperation a framework to scale.

Two client groups, two channels that add to each other

Wealth management clients invest through regulated partners. They hold the security in their existing custody, advised, priced and reported alongside their equities and bonds, and transferable as securities in the securities market.

Reental’s investors buy Reental’s tokens, accessible from smaller amounts and on a digital platform, and look for flexibility in how they enter, hold and exit a position.

The same underlying security supports both channels, with the same issuer, structure and governance. Estating works with professional counterparties, including private banks, wealth managers and family offices. Through its global community and tokenisation platform, Reental serves individual investors, entrepreneurs, companies seeking to invest their cash reserves, and investors active in real estate, digital assets and decentralised finance.

The framework

Under the partnership, the institutional-grade real estate securities that underlie Reental’s tokens in this segment are originated and structured by Estating. Reental can also call on Estating to structure its own products, through Estating as a Service. Hospitality is the first vertical, with an active forward pipeline. A joint Partnership Committee governs the arrangement.

“Real-world assets reach investors only as well as the rails beneath them,” said Martin Halblaub, Founder, Chairman and Group CEO of Estating. “We have spent six years building institutional rails: access to owner-operators, a securitisation vehicle, and regulated wealth management partners who reach clients where they already invest. Reental has spent the same six years building retail rails we would never build ourselves. Connected, they form one chain from the asset to the token holder, and each of us answers for our own part. We provide the security and remain accountable for it. Reental builds its token on it and serves its investors. We are part of the system, not the final product. Six transactions proved it works. Now we build on it.”

“This partnership builds on Reental’s track record, combining our expertise in tokenised real estate with Estating’s institutional-grade structuring and governance,” said Eric Sánchez, Founder and CEO of Reental. “Together, we bring that institutional standard onchain, expanding what investors can do with their assets through composability with decentralised protocols, new liquidity options and the ability to use their holdings as collateral through RNT Lend. For our community, this means institutional-quality investments with greater flexibility to manage their positions, compound returns and put their capital to work.”

About Estating

Estating S.A. is a Luxembourg-domiciled infrastructure platform for real-world assets. Founded in 2020, the company originates and curates, structures and securitises, and distributes real assets as securities through a regulated European framework. It serves private banks, wealth managers, family offices and institutional investors, and works business to business rather than directly with end investors. Estating is domiciled and governed in Luxembourg.

About Reental

Reental Holding Company is a global real estate investment and tokenisation platform founded in 2020. It serves a community spanning around 100 countries, including individual investors, entrepreneurs and companies seeking to invest their cash reserves. Reental offers its own tokenised securities, combining real estate expertise with accessible investment opportunities and onchain infrastructure. Through integration with decentralised finance, the platform expands investors’ options for managing their assets, accessing liquidity, compounding returns and using eligible holdings as collateral
