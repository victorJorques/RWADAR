Savvy Group of Companies a brainchild of Jaxay Shah, Sameer Sinha and Jigish Shah, was established in 1996. Over the past 25 years, the group has developed over 10 million sq. ft. of real estate across various segments like residential, commercial, retail, golf, SEZ, and hospitality projects.

With its proven track record and expertise in the real estate industry, Savvy Group continues to develop innovative and high-quality projects that enhance the lives of people and communities.

Collated is reimagining real estate to create a platform for a shared economy focusing on the concept of live, work and play. It is led by Shaan Zaveri, Gaurav Gadhecha and Aniruddh Jhaveri and powered by a dynamic team with a solid track record in the field of real estate development and property management.

Collated aims to design and develop purpose-built real estate projects that cater to the evolving needs of its users. The varied project concepts comprise of community living, senior living, thematic retail, serviced apartments, flexible workspaces, and urban communities.
