Why Real Estate Remains Locked Out of Wealth Portfolios
High demand. Sub-Optimal structure.
The Access Gap
THE PROBLEM

High-quality institutional deals require $5M+ minimums, direct GP relationships, and proprietary deal flow, systematically excluding private wealth channels.

THE IMPACT

HNW clients are locked out of best-in-class opportunities. Advisors lose to direct platforms. Alpha stays institutional.

The Operational Burden
THE PROBLEM

Each deal requires bespoke legal structuring, tax planning, subscription execution, custody onboarding, and lifecycle reporting. All manual, expensive, and time-consuming.

THE IMPACT

Advisors become operators instead of advisors. Scalability breaks. Efficiency collapse.

Portfolio Exclusion
THE PROBLEM

Traditional real estate investments lock capital for 7-10 years with no secondary market access, and no custodial integration.

THE IMPACT

Advisors can't integrate into dynamic portfolios. Clients can't capture alpha. Wealth platforms can't offer curated real estate opportunities.

The Compliance Maze
THE PROBLEM

Local structures, inconsistent documentation, jurisdiction-specific tax treatment, and custody incompatibility create operational friction at every level.

THE IMPACT

Most opportunities fall outside regulated advisor workflows, forcing professionals to choose between client needs and compliance standards.

Estating was built to solve all four, so you can focus on what matters: building a more complete portfolio.
