How TangibleX Works

TangibleX provides digital infrastructure designed to support the tokenisation and digital presentation of real-world asset investments within a secure and compliance-aligned environment. By integrating licensed fund managers, blockchain-based smart contract infrastructure, trusted custodial providers such as Fireblocks, and stablecoin settlement through partners such as AUDD, TangibleX enables investors to access fractionalised real-world asset opportunities within a structured digital environment. Each opportunity is structured through a unit trust linked to an underlying real-world asset, with tokens providing a digital representation of those interests.

1
Asset Origination via Fund Managers

All investment opportunities are originated and structured by licensed AFSL fund managers through unit trust or managed investment scheme structures.

2
Smart Contract Tokenisation

Units issued by the fund manager are digitally represented by tokens, with blockchain-based smart contracts supporting their controlled issuance and transfer.

3
Asset Listing on TangibleX

Tokenised investment opportunities are presented through the TangibleX digital infrastructure, where verified investors can discover and participate in offerings originated by licensed fund managers.

4
Investor Onboarding & KYC

Investors complete identity verification through the TangibleX infrastructure and directly with the relevant fund manager prior to participating in any investment opportunity

5
Wallet Creation via Fireblocks

Secure digital wallets are established for investors and asset owners within the fund manager's custodial framework, supported by Fireblocks institutional-grade infrastructure.

6
Stablecoin Funding via AUDD

Investors obtain stablecoin through external digital asset service providers and transfer those funds into wallets established within the fund manager's custodial framework to participate in available investment opportunities

7
Blockchain-Based Settlement & Registry Alignment

Blockchain infrastructure supports the recording of digital transfers, with the fund manager responsible for updating the official unit registry to reflect the underlying change in ownership.
