The future of securities trading is here. Starting at noon ET today, eligible U.S. customers can trade available assets around the clock on OKXICE’s Tokenized Securities Venue (TSV), found in the TSV tab of the OKX app.

The venue has confirmed 113 assets, including stocks, ETFs and ETPs, which will become available on a rolling basis beginning today. Confirmed issuers and asset managers include Better.com, BitGo, Circle, Coinbase, Direxion, eToro, Fidelity Investments, Robinhood, Securitize, Strategy, Strive and Vida Global.

A market that never closes

Company developments, economic data and global events can unfold outside traditional market hours. OKXICE TSV brings securities trading onchain and makes it available around the clock to eligible participants, giving them more flexibility to choose when they trade.

Behind the venue is OKXICE, a joint venture between OKX and Intercontinental Exchange (ICE), parent company of the New York Stock Exchange. It brings together OKX’s onchain technology and ICE’s market infrastructure expertise.

How the TSV works

Each digital asset is a security entitlement backed one-to-one by an underlying share, with applicable shareholder rights and economic benefits, including dividends, voting rights and corporate actions.

Trading takes place through automated market maker (AMM) liquidity pools on Uniswap v4, built on X Layer, OKX’s Ethereum Layer 2 network. Smart contracts restrict trading and liquidity provision to eligible wallets. Securitize provides the infrastructure for bringing the securities onchain.

Customers hold their securities in self-custodial wallets; OKXICE TSV does not hold customer assets or private keys.

Getting started

The venue is available only to eligible U.S.-based OKX Inc. customers who complete identity verification, activate a CeDeFi Wallet and complete OKX Inc. onboarding. Access is subject to geographic and other eligibility requirements.

Eligible customers can find the venue in the TSV tab of the OKX app starting at noon ET.

Securities trading involves risk, including loss of principal. Tokenized securities may trade at prices different from the underlying share, especially outside market hours. Self-custodied assets are not SIPC- or FDIC-protected, and lost keys cannot be recovered.

OKXICE TSV is offered by OKXICE LLC under a temporary SEC exemption; the NYSE does not operate or oversee this venue. It is not a registered exchange or broker-dealer and is not approved by the SEC.
