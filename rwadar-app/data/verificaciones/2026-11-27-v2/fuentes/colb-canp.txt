Pre-IPO opportunities

CANP is a tokenized structured certificate giving investors onchain exposure to the value of Anthropic pre-IPO shares, without, private banking barriers, custody constraints or intermediaries. CANP opens an access to a premium private market opportunity, enabling subscribers to hold a secure, transparent, and fully digital asset.

The Colb Anthropic Token (CANP) is a Swiss-structured, tokenized certificate that tracks the value evolution of Anthropic pre-IPO shares, offering qualified investors an exclusive opportunity to participate in the growth of one of the world’s most visionary private companies.

CANP’s primary objective is to deliver medium- to long-term capital growth through exposure to Anthropic pre-IPO shares. Based on recent indications, the expected valuation range is USD 442 - 612 per share, corresponding to an implied valuation of approximately USD 650 - 900 billion.

The underlying asset of CANP consists of a 100% allocation to Anthropic shares, held through a dedicated trust.

The investment follows a Net Asset Value (NAV)-based model, where pricing evolves dynamically to reflect the latest available information on the underlying asset, ensuring ongoing alignment between token value and portfolio  exposure.

ANTHROPIC

Anthropic is a U.S.-based AI safety company (founded 2021, San Francisco) best known for Claude, its family of language models: Opus (advanced reasoning), Sonnet (balanced), and Haiku (fast, cost-efficient), available across web, mobile, API, and major clouds.

It also offers tools like Claude Code and Claude Cowork, plus integrations with Microsoft Office. Its API supports features such as extended reasoning, tool use, web search, and multi-agent workflows.

Anthropic focuses on AI safety research, including Constitutional AI and interpretability, and serves 300,000+ businesses worldwide, generating revenue through API access, subscriptions, and enterprise deals.
