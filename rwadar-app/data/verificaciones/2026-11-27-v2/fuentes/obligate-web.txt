Frequently asked questions
What is Obligate?
Obligate is a digital securities infrastructure provider that enables the issuance and distribution of regulated on-chain financial instruments. We operate a proprietary platform for tokenizing bonds natively on-chain, fund trackers and structured products under the Swiss DLT Act, connecting issuers with global investors through blockchain technology.
What does Obligate offer?
Obligate offers yield products via bonds or yield strategies for investors seeking access to regulated on-chain private credit, and asset-backed securities from investment-grade to high yield options.

For issuers and asset managers targeting to launch digital securities, Obligate offers tokenization solutions through its automated platform along the lifecycle for creation and management of these securities , reducing issuance costs by 80% and time from weeks to hours.
Who are Obligate's solutions tailored to?
Our yield products are designed for institutional investors, qualified investors, and professional clients seeking regulated exposure to private credit markets.

Our tokenization solutions serve companies, financial institutions, and issuers looking to modernize their capital raising through blockchain infrastructure—from established corporates to innovative fintech companies across trade finance, real estate, and structured debt.
How much does it cost?
Our pricing varies based on the solution and your specific needs. For detailed information about fees for investors and issuers, please visit our fee schedule or contact our team for a customized quote.
How quickly can I onboard with Obligate?
For investors, onboarding typically takes 1-3 business days after completing KYC/AML verification. You can begin investing immediately once approved.

For issuers, the initial setup and structuring process takes 1-2 weeks, after which subsequent issuances can be executed in as little as one hour. Our team will guide you through each step to ensure a smooth and compliant onboarding.
