Colb Asset SA is a registered financial intermediary under Swiss law, conducting the distribution of securities in Switzerland in full compliance with FinSA regulation, and, therefore, a member of ARIF.

ARIF is a self-regulatory organization approved by the Swiss Financial Market Supervisory Authority (FINMA) for the oversight of financial intermediaries referred to in Article 2, paragraph 3 of the Swiss Anti-Money Laundering Act (AMLA), which covers the prevention of money laundering and terrorist financing in the financial sector. Every financial instrument offered to clients follows FinSA's rules of conduct, client segmentation, and documentation requirements, ensuring investors receive the protection and transparency standards established by Swiss law.
