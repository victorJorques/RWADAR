FundBridge and Libeara Launch Regulated Tokenised Treasury Fund on Avalanche

Singapore – 28 August 2025 – FundBridge Capital and Libeara announced today the launch of infrastructure support for a fully tokenised U.S. Treasury strategy (“ULTRA”) on the Avalanche blockchain, marking a significant milestone in the institutional adoption of tokenised U.S. Treasuries. 

 

ULTRA is managed by FundBridge Capital and sub-managed by Wellington Management. FundBridge Capital is one of Singapore’s leading independent fund managers, and Wellington Management is one of the world’s largest asset managers, with over USD 1.2 trillion in AUM. Designed to preserve capital and maintain liquidity, ULTRA offers accredited and institutional investors secure, short-duration U.S. Treasury exposure in an on-chain format, subject to institutional-grade governance standards. 

 

At the heart of ULTRA’s tokenisation technology infrastructure is Libeara, a next-generation tokenisation platform incubated by SC Ventures by Standard Chartered. Libeara powers the technology lifecycle of tokenised fund operations – from investor onboarding and subscription to redemption and transfer – supported by FundBridge’s partners. 

 

The fund enabling the ULTRA strategy has been established under the Delta Master Trust, an umbrella unit trust constituted under Singapore law. The fund is accessible through fiat or USDC channels, supported by regulated on-/off-ramp partners. The solution offers operational efficiency, compliance, and transparency through a single institutional-grade platform. 

 

The integration with Avalanche provides the performance layer, leveraging deterministic finality, sub-second settlement, and configurable blockchain environments tailored to governance and risk control needs. This deployment marks a significant step forward in the institutional adoption of real-world assets (RWAs), demonstrating that tokenised funds can be built and operated on public infrastructure without compromising compliance, control, or scale. 

 

“Avalanche is purpose-built to support the compliance, performance, and flexibility required by today’s regulated financial products,” said [nombre retirado], VP of On-Chain Finance at Ava Labs. “The deployment of ULTRA demonstrates how tokenised funds can operate on public blockchain infrastructure while meeting the operational and governance standards expected by institutional asset managers and allocators.” 

 

“The integration with Avalanche supports our broader objective of working with public blockchain networks that meet the technical and compliance expectations of institutional stakeholders,” said [nombre retirado], Founder and CEO of Libeara. “We see this as an important step in extending infrastructure optionality for tokenised strategies that require both regulatory rigour and operational scalability.” 

 

“The integration of ULTRA with Avalanche reflects our continued efforts to build institutional infrastructure for regulated digital asset strategies,” said [nombre retirado], CEO of FundBridge Capital. “As a licenced fund manager, we remain focused on aligning compliant fund structures expected by institutional allocators.” 

 

Libeara supports this deployment by providing and maintaining the infrastructure for token creation, smart contract functionality, and institutional wallet integrations. 

 

FundBridge Capital remains responsible for the fund’s governance and oversight, including its investment mandate and regulatory compliance, with Wellington Management supporting FundBridge’s overall initiative as sub-manager. 

 

 This deployment marks a pivotal advance in institutional finance, where regulatory discipline meets digital agility – accelerating the shift toward blockchain-based infrastructure purpose-built for compliant, high-efficiency financial products.

About Avalanche Blockchain Network

Avalanche is a high-performance blockchain platform designed for builders who need to scale. Engineered with a revolutionary three-part Layer 1 (L1) architecture, Avalanche is anchored by its Avalanche Consensus Mechanism, ensuring near-instant finality for transactions. The platform also features an open-source Layer 0 (L0) framework, enabling the seamless creation of interoperable Layer 1 blockchains with high throughput on both public and private networks. 

 

Supported by a global community of developers and validators, Avalanche offers a fast, low- cost environment for building the next generation of decentralised applications (dApps). With its unique blend of speed, flexibility, and scalability, Avalanche is the preferred choice for innovators pushing the boundaries of blockchain technology.
