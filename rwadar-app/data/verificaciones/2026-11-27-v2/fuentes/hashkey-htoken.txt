HashKey RWA Launches First Product Under the HashKey “hToken” Series, Advancing Traditional Financial Asset Tokenisation Through a One-Stop Issuance Solution
By: [nombre retirado]
Published: 26 October, 2026

Hong Kong, Oct. 26, 2026 — HashKey RWA, the tokenisation arm of HashKey Holdings Limited (“HashKey”, or “the Group”, Stock Code: 3887.HK), a leading digital asset financial services group in Asia with a global footprint, today announced the launch of the first tokenised securities product under its HashKey “hToken” series, leveraging its RWA One-Stop Issuance Solution.

As global financial institutions continue to explore the convergence of digital assets and traditional financial markets, the tokenisation of traditional financial assets, including funds, bonds and structured products, is attracting increasing attention. Tokenised securities are gradually moving beyond proof-of-concept initiatives and technical exploration towards real-world business applications spanning issuance, distribution and asset servicing. However, complex compliance frameworks, the high cost of adapting existing products, and the lack of standardised approaches to lifecycle management continue to constrain the large-scale adoption of tokenised issuance. In response to these needs, HashKey RWA draws on HashKey Group’s capabilities in tokenisation solution design, asset management, licensed digital asset trading, blockchain technology and institutional financial services, together with its network of banking, custody, legal and other external professional partners. Building on its One-Stop Issuance Solution, HashKey RWA has introduced the hToken series, translating tokenised issuance into a reusable, standardised product framework and moving the tokenisation of products linked to traditional financial assets beyond one-off bespoke projects towards a standardised and replicable issuance model.

As the first product launched under the “hToken” series, HashKey RWA has selected the fund units of HashKey Fintech Opportunities Limited Class A (“HFOL Class A”), managed by HashKey Capital Management (BVI) Limited, as the underlying asset. Core consideration factors of the selection process include the fund’s rigorous underlying asset quality and targeted exposure to returns in specific markets. Built on the foundations of an existing fund product, this product explores the integration of traditional financial products with digital issuance and distribution channels, offering eligible professional investors more diversified investment options while providing practical experience to support the future tokenisation of a broader range of financial assets.

Notably, the “hToken” series adopts an innovative issuance model that leverages a standardised tokenisation solution to create new digital issuance and distribution channels for traditional financial assets, without requiring extensive modifications to existing underlying fund structures. By holding tokenised securities products, investors can gain economic exposure linked to the performance of the corresponding underlying assets.

The first product is now available through the Earn Channel on HashKey Exchange, where eligible professional investors can subscribe using USDT. This further connects traditional financial assets with the digital asset ecosystem while providing financial institutions with more flexible and efficient asset tokenisation solutions.

Building on the launch of its first fund product, HashKey RWA will further develop the capabilities of the “hToken” series. Going forward, HashKey RWA will continue to enhance its one-stop issuance solution and explore its application to a broader range of financial assets, including funds, bonds, equities and structured products, offering institutional clients more flexible and efficient solutions.

About HashKey RWA

HashKey RWA is the tokenisation arm of HashKey (3887.HK). It’s an institutional grade one-stop tokenization service that transforms real world assets into programmable, accessible and globally connected financial products, driving capital markets into a new era.
It supports the full tokenization lifecycle to enable efficient end-to-end deployment from concept design to distribution. Supported by HashKey’s core infrastructure and innovative capabilities, it enables the on-chain issuance of multiple tokenized assets globally, building a compliant bridge between traditional assets and on-chain liquidity.
As a cornerstone of open finance, HashKey RWA sets a new paradigm for institutional asset issuance, management and distribution.
