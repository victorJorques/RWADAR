Overview: oTFY is a multi-blockchain token issued by a Luxembourg securitization vehicle pursuant to the Luxembourg Securitisation Law and the Swiss DLT Act. The token represents a claim on the net asset value of a revolving portfolio comprising primarily trade-finance bonds issued under Swiss law. These investment-grade bonds are issued by a separate segregated portfolio company domiciled in the Cayman Islands with physical exposure to commodities and managed by a Singapore management company. Obligate AG, Zurich, Switzerland, serves as the product sponsor.

Underlying assets: The underlying assets primarily consist of a revolving portfolio of investment-grade eNotes issued by a segregated portfolio company with physical exposure to liquid, globally traded bulk commodities that are fungible and in consistent demand across multiple jurisdictions. The portfolio is diversified across a high volume of individual transactions, each relatively modest in size. These assets are protected by all-risk marine insurance, which includes coverage for war risk and exceeds the commodity's value. any investment decisions.
