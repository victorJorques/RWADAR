Your co-ownership share corresponds to a specific weight of the selected Metal, expressed in grams. The amount of Metal you acquire in each Transaction is determined on the basis of the purchase price you pay and the applicable per-gram price as displayed on the Bitpanda Platform at the time of the Transaction, taking into account applicable fees. Your co-ownership share represents a pro rata fractional interest, by weight, in the total physical bars of that Metal type held by the Vault Operator on behalf of all co-owners. 2.2. Offer to buy Metals. When you submit an Offer to buy Metals your Offer is a request to enter into a Transaction under which you purchase Metals against payment of E-Money for a firm price as published on the Bitpanda Platform. 3.3. Offer to sell Metals. When you submit an Offer to sell Metals your Offer is a request to enter into a Transaction under which you receive your requested and firm amount of E-Money in exchange for a sale of a specified amount of Metals. 3.4. Swap. When you submit an Offer to "Swap" your Metals with another Supported Asset, your Offer is interpreted as a request to enter into a Transaction under which you (i) sell a specified amount and type of Metals for E-Money and instantaneously (ii) purchase another Supported Asset in the amount of the E-Money available from the sale described in (i). However, a Swap under this Clause effectively and economically (after taking into consideration applicable fees and spreads) corresponds to a direct exchange of a Supported Asset as the E-Money will not be credited to your Account. To complete a Swap, you are required to pay the applicable fees associated with the sale of the initial Supported Asset, followed by the fees applicable to the purchase of the subsequent Supported Asset. 3.5.
