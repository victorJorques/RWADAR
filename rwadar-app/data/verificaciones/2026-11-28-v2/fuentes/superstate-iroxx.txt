About IROXX

The Invesco Stablecoin Reserves Onchain Fund (the “Fund”) offers access to short-term U.S. government securities through a tokenized money market fund. The Fund’s investment objective is to seek current income as is consistent with liquidity and the preservation of capital, while maintaining a stable $1.00 net asset value per share. Ownership in the Fund is represented by IROXX, held as a token. Subscriptions and redemptions are facilitated through USD or USDC, with liquidity each market day, and the Fund’s income is distributed daily.

The Fund does not invest in stablecoins or in stablecoin issuers.
The Fund expects stablecoin issuers to hold a significant portion of its shares as reserve assets. Stablecoin market volatility, issuer-specific events, changes in redemption activity, or legal and regulatory developments may cause rapid or substantial Fund purchases or redemptions, which could adversely affect the Fund’s liquidity, yield, expenses, remaining shareholders, and ability to maintain a stable share price. The Fund’s limited universe of eligible reserve assets may also result in a lower yield than other money market funds, and evolving stablecoin regulation may require changes to the Fund’s investments or operations.
Large purchases or redemptions may adversely affect the Fund’s liquidity, yield, expense ratio and net asset value, increase transaction costs, and negatively impact remaining shareholders. This risk may be heightened if multiple stablecoin issuers redeem Fund shares at the same time.
The Fund’s yield will vary as the short-term securities in its portfolio mature or are sold and the proceeds are reinvested in other securities. Additionally, inflation may outpace and diminish investment returns over time.
The investment techniques and risk analysis used by the portfolio managers may not produce the desired results.
The Fund’s use of blockchain technology is subject to operational, technological and cybersecurity risks, including network outages or congestion, transaction delays or increased fees, coding or cryptographic flaws, cyberattacks, compromised digital wallets, unauthorized transfers, and network modifications or forks. These events could disrupt or incorrectly record transactions, delay or prevent access to tokenized Fund shares, and result in the loss of some or all of an investment. Because the Fund is new, there is limited operating history on which to evaluate these risks.
Decentralized blockchain networks rely on evolving governance processes that may lack consensus or clarity. Changes in network protocols or applicable laws, regulations or regulatory interpretations could impair network functionality; delay, restrict or prevent transactions in Fund shares; require changes to the Fund’s operations; or otherwise adversely affect the Fund and its shareholders.
Transactions in tokenized Fund shares, including redemptions and peer-to-peer transfers, require payment of variable blockchain transaction fees known as “gas” in the applicable network’s native digital asset. Gas prices and processing times may fluctuate significantly based on network congestion, transaction complexity, demand and the fee selected by a shareholder, and the Fund cannot control these conditions. Higher gas prices may increase shareholder costs, while an inadequate gas fee, insufficient wallet assets, network or wallet disruptions, or smart-contract errors could delay, reject, incorrectly process or prevent a transaction and may result in excessive or unintended gas usage. A redemption initiated before the Fund’s cutoff time may therefore be received in good order after the cutoff, delaying proceeds and leaving the shareholder exposed to changes in the value of Fund shares until the Fund next calculates its net asset value. Because the Fund is new, there is limited operating history regarding these transaction risks.
The Fund relies on a blockchain-integrated sub-transfer agent recordkeeping system and Investor Portal that are subject to operational, technological, recordkeeping and cybersecurity risks. System flaws, outages, integration errors, technological changes, inaccurate or incomplete investor information, or compromised credentials could impair Investor Portal access; delay, prevent or incorrectly record transactions; affect investor eligibility or regulatory determinations; or permit unauthorized activity. Participants are responsible for maintaining accurate information and restricting access to authorized persons. Unauthorized or erroneous actions may not be detected or disputed promptly, may not be reversible, and could result in inaccurate ownership records, loss of access to Fund shares, financial loss, or other adverse effects on the Fund and its shareholders.
There are a limited number of funds that maintain shares in the form of digital securities. Given the novel nature of this product, the Fund, Adviser, Transfer Agent, Sub-Transfer Agent and other service providers have relatively limited experience using blockchain technology to maintain records and facilitate transactions in the interests of digital securities of a registered fund. Accordingly, there may be an increased risk of errors or unauthorized transactions involving Fund shares. Any such errors or unauthorized transactions could adversely affect the Fund.
