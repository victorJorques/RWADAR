Technology

Axiology’s DLT TSS combines advanced blockchain technology with a focus on efficiency, compliance, and accessibility, transforming how tokenized assets are managed and traded. Permissioned infrastructure built on the XRP Ledger.

Secure Access

A private, permissioned infrastructure ensures only licensed entities can operate nodes, maintaining security and regulatory compliance.

Instant Settlement & Custody

Real-time settlement and secure custody of tokenised assets simplify asset management and eliminate settlement risks.

Integrated Secondary Market

A built-in decentralised exchange (DEX) provides liquidity and market access, allowing seamless trading of tokenized securities on a single platform.
