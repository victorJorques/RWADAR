Q&A
 How can I invest in the AFC?
To purchase shares via bank transfer, all you need is an Ethereum wallet to receive the share tokens. For optimal interaction with the Aktionariat Brokerbot (the share dispenser), we recommend using the dedicated Aktionariat Portfolio Wallet, available on iOS or Android. This wallet was specifically developed for seamless integration with the Brokerbot. Alternatively, you can connect the Brokerbot with MetaMask or any WalletConnect - compatible portfolio app.
If you prefer to pay for the share tokens using Ether (ETH) or Cryptofrancs (XCHF), make sure you have the corresponding amount available in your wallet. Through the Aktionariat Portfolio Wallet, you can conveniently purchase Ether via bank transfer.
 Is investing anonymous?
Investors' personal data, including their names, addresses, and email addresses, are not disclosed publicly. To be included in the share ledger of the respective company, investors are required to register with their names and addresses.
The Ethereum address associated with the ownership of share tokens is stored on the Ethereum blockchain and is accessible to anyone. However, it’s important to note that personal data is not stored on the blockchain and remains unlinked to the Ethereum address.
 How can I resell my tokens?
With Aktionariat AG, the issuer has the opportunity to establish a liquidity pool by setting aside a portion of the funds collected from investors for share buybacks within the brokerbot. In this scenario, investors follow the same process as when purchasing shares: they connect their Ethereum wallet to the brokerbot and select the "Sell" tab instead of the "Buy" tab. This allows investors to effortlessly sell their shares or a portion of them back to the company.
Moreover, transferring shares from one investor's wallet to another is also possible. To do this using the Shareholder Portfolio App:
The investor navigates to the "Portfolio" tab.
They select the corresponding asset.
Under "Positions," they choose the desired sender address.
Next, they click on "Transfer."
For the "To Address" field, the investor can select either their own Ethereum address or an external one. They can then initiate the transfer of the desired number of share tokens by scanning the QR code or manually entering the recipient's Ethereum address. It's important to note that payment for the transferred tokens is a separate process and can be conducted outside the blockchain ecosystem.
Important: The new holder of the share tokens must register through the Aktionariat Portfolio App to be recorded in the share register. If using a different Ethereum wallet, the investor can register in the share ledger by using the registration widget. For more information, please visit Aktionariat.com
