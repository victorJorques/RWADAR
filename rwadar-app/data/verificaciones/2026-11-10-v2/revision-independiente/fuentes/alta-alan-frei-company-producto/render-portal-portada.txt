About Alan Frei Company
New Adventures•Founded in 2022, Zürich, Switzerland
The AFC is built on three pillars: Content, Community, and Products. We create educational and entertaining content around lifehacking, optimization, and building startups. Through this, we are building a community of entrepreneurs and life hackers. For this community, we are developing smart products that make life easier and add more adventure. Members can also become shareholders and co-owners. Our goal is to build a worldwide next-generation B2C/D2C brand for urban travelers and life hackers, with customers, employees, and shareholders aligned around the same vision.
