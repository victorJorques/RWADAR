    Amundi unveils tokenised access to one of its flagship
                        index funds
Paris, November 5th 2026 - Amundi, European leading asset manager and ETF provider1,
announces the launch of a tokenised share class of one of its flagship European equity
index fund, Amundi Core STOXX Europe 600, a core European equity strategy widely used
as a building block for portfolio allocation.

With this launch, Amundi extends tokenisation beyond cash management solutions into
index investing, marking a new milestone in its digital asset strategy. By bringing a flagship
broad-market equity exposure into a tokenised format, Amundi takes a further step
towards connecting traditional investment solutions with emerging digital market
infrastructure.

The new share class has been issued on the public Ethereum blockchain. It remains fully
integrated into the fund’s existing operating model, and the fund continues to be available
also through traditional channels. Developed in collaboration with CACEIS, the solution
leverages CACEIS’s technology and infrastructure for the tokenisation of fund units.

This initiative reflects Amundi’s conviction that tokenisation can progressively support a
broader range of mainstream investment uses across asset classes. It follows the launch
over the past few months of Amundi’s first tokenised share class of a money market fund,
subscription via stablecoins as well as a series of partnerships with digital players, large
corporates and financial institutions to test concrete applications of distributed ledger
technology.

This phased approach reflects Amundi’s commitment to innovation within a robust
regulatory, operational and risk management framework. It also supports Amundi’s
ambition to progressively develop a range of tokenised funds to respond to the evolving
needs of institutional clients and digital platforms, leveraging its potential to deliver
greater operational efficiency through the automation of certain processes, improved
accessibility, fast settlement and transparency.

As market infrastructure and regulatory frameworks continue to develop, tokenised fund
shares will create new opportunities for digital distribution and applications across asset
classes. By extending tokenisation to index funds, Amundi contributes to lay the
foundations to bridge traditional finance and digital finance.
