       Jersey Taxation            Holders of the Products

                                  It is anticipated that non-Jersey holders of the Products will not
                                  be subject to any tax in Jersey in respect of the acquisition,
                                  ownership, sale, exchange of the Products. Accordingly, save
                                  as set out below, there will be no withholding or similar tax
                                  required to be deducted by the Issuer in respect of non-Jersey
                                  resident holders of the Products.
