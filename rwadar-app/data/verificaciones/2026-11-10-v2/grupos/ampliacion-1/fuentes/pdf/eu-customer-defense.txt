                                     PREAMBLE

SECURITIZE EUROPE BROKERAGE AND MARKETS, S.A. (hereinafter "Securitize") has
as one of its main objectives the establishment of a lasting and mutually satisfactory
relationship with its Clients.

The achievement of this objective requires, among other actions, achieving a high
level of quality in the provision of investment services that meets the expectations
of Clients.

Securitize's Customer Defense Regulations (hereinafter referred to as the
"Regulations") constitute a basic element to improve the quality of service, providing
a simple procedure to quickly and safely resolve cases in which the client considers
that they have not received the service they expected and constitutes a substantial
source of learning that allows correcting errors that may have been made in the
future.

The purpose of Securitize's Customer Protection Regulations is to comply with the
regulations for the protection of financial services customers and, in particular, of:

   -​ Chapter V of Law 44/2002 of 22 November 2002 on measures to reform the
      financial system.

   -​ Order ECO/734/2004 of 11 March 2004 on customer service departments
      and services and the customer ombudsman in financial institutions.


The approval of these Regulations is also part of Securitize's general customer
protection policy, establishing the means, bodies and procedures necessary to
ensure adequate protection of the right of Clients to have their Complaints
addressed and resolved.

The Regulatory Compliance and Customer Service Department offers Customer
Service (SAC), and is responsible for receiving complaints from Customers. This is an
internal service, which has sufficient autonomy so that its decisions cannot be
affected by conflicts of interest and has the material and human resources that allow
it to adopt an appropriate and rapid resolution.

Since Securitize will not appoint a Client Ombudsman, the SAC will deal with all
complaints duly filed by its Clients that fall within the scope of these Regulations.

Chapter One sets out the subject-matter, definitions and other general provisions
applicable to this Regulation.

The second chapter regulates the general aspects of the SAC, detailing its
characteristics and delimiting its respective competences in the resolution of these

The third chapter regulates the specific procedure for the submission, processing
and resolution of Clients' complaints.

All personnel involved must be aware of Securitize's internal complaints handling
procedure and have a duty to act in accordance with it.

                                      CHAPTER I

                                  General Provisions

Article 1.- Purpose.

This Regulation implements the mandate of Order ECO/734/2004, of March 11,
2004, insofar as Securitize is an investment services firm, and regulates the right of
Securitize Clients to have their complaints duly addressed and resolved.

Article 2.- Approval.

This Regulation has been approved by the Board of Directors of Securitize.

The Board of Directors will be responsible for adopting all necessary measures to
ensure its wide dissemination and will be made available to Clients by means of its
publication on the corresponding Securitize website.

Article 3.- Modification.

Any amendment to these Regulations must be submitted to the Board of Directors
or, where appropriate, the relevant Board of Directors of Securitize.

Article 4.- Definitions.

    a)​ Clients: Spanish or foreign individuals or legal entities that are users of the
        investment services provided by Securitize.

    b)​ Claim: Any type of complaint or claim that a Securitize Client may make in
        relation to its legally recognized interests and rights.

       Complaints shall relate to:




                                                                                     3
           -​ The interests and rights legally established in favour of customers
              derived from the regulations on transparency and customer
              protection.

           -​ Contracts signed with Securitize.

           -​ The requirements of good financial practices and uses.

   c)​ Customer Service "SAC": Specialized customer service established by
       Securitize, responsible for the attention and resolution of complaints from
       Securitize Clients.

Article 5.- Claims excluded due to their purpose.

5.1.​ Claims made by those who do not prove that they have received a service or
      maintain or have maintained a direct contractual relationship with Securitize
      will be excluded from these Regulations.

5.2.​ The SAC does not fall within the competence of Claims arising from:

   i.​     Securitize's employment relationships with its employees.
   ii.​    Securitize's relations with its suppliers, with the exception of those that
           refer to the entity's operations and that directly affect the rights and
           legitimate interests of customers in their relations with it.
   iii.​   Products and services marketed or contracted outside Spanish territory.
           This exclusion does not affect the possible distance marketing of financial
           services intended for consumers, and specifically the rights that they may
           claim as applicable, in accordance with Law 22/2007, of July 11, 2007, on
           distance marketing of financial services intended for consumers.
   iv.​    Complaints or claims filed through the CNMV's Complaints Service.
   v.​     Complaints or claims that are heard by administrative, arbitral or judicial
           bodies, or are pending resolution or litigation.
   vi.​    Those complaints or claims that raise controversies about facts whose
           proof can only be made in court or whose resolution necessarily requires
           the assessment of experts with specialized knowledge in a technical
           matter outside the regulations on transparency and customer protection
           or good financial practices and usages.
   vii.​   Complaints regarding data protection issues.

                                          CHAPTER II

                                       Customer Service

Article 6.- Holder of the SAC

6.1.​ The Head of Customer Service will be appointed by Securitize's Board of
      Directors and will have the functional and hierarchical dependency required for
      the position. The appointment of the Holder of the SAC will be communicated
      to the Complaints Service of the National Securities Market Commission
      (CNMV) in accordance with the requirements established by the regulations.

6.2.​ The head of the Customer Service must have the due commercial and
      professional reputation, as well as sufficient knowledge and experience to
      carry out his/her duties in accordance with the requirements established in the
      applicable regulations.

Article 7.- Grounds for Incompatibility

7.1.​     The exercise of the functions of Customer Service Holder shall be incompatible
          with the exercise of activities for or on behalf of competing financial
          institutions other than Securitize.

7.2.​ The following may not hold the position of Head of the SAC:

        a)​ Failed and insolvent not rehabilitated.

        b)​ Those who are criminally or administratively authorized or suspended to hold
            public office or to hold administrative or management positions in entities.

        c)​ Those who have a criminal record for crimes of forgery against the Public
            Treasury, punishable insolvency, infidelity in the custody of documents,
            violation of secrets, money laundering, embezzlement of public funds,
            disclosure of secrets or against property.

        d)​ Those who belong or have belonged to the management bodies of any entity
            of the Group, or who perform or have performed in the last two years
            management or executive functions in any entity of the Group.

        e)​ Those who provide professional services to companies competing with
            Securitize, as well as accept positions of employee, manager or administrator
            in them.




                                                                                       5
   f)​ Those who hold the position of member of the Board of Directors, Chief
       Executive Officer, General Manager or equivalent, or hold positions that have
       executive functions attributed to them in any credit institution, investment
       services company, management company of collective investment schemes,
       insurance company, pension fund management company or insurance
       brokerage company.

   g)​ Those who hold political office or carry out any other activities that may have
       public significance or that may affect in any way the image of Customer
       Service.


   h)​ Any other causes of incompatibility that may be established by the applicable
       regulations at any given time.

Article 8.- Duration and Termination

8.1. The Holder of the SAC shall hold the position indefinitely.

8.2. The head of the Customer Service shall cease to perform his/her duties for any
of the following reasons:

   a)​ Expiry of the term of your appointment or renewal, if applicable.

   b)​ Supervening incapacity or concurrence of any of the causes of
       incompatibility indicated in Article 7 of these Regulations.

   c)​ Have been convicted in a final judgment of intentional crime.

   d)​ Resignation.

   e)​ Termination of the contractual relationship.

   f)​ The SAC Holder's acknowledgment of the incompatibility of his/her position
       with a new responsibility or position in Securitize's organizational structure.

   g)​ Failure to comply with its obligations or gross negligence in the exercise of its
       obligations.


   h)​ Express Waiver.

       8.3. In the event of termination, a new Holder will be appointed within twenty
       days following the termination of the position.



                                                                                      6
Article 9.- Functions of the SAC

9.1.​          The Customer Service Department is set up as a specialised and autonomous
               department of Securitize that is entrusted with the function of processing, in
               accordance with these Regulations, Customer Complaints.

9.2.​ The functions of the SAC are:

        i.​         Attend to and resolve any complaints that may be submitted by Clients in
                    accordance with the procedure established in Chapter III.

        ii.​        Promote and ensure compliance with regulations on transparency and
                    customer protection and good financial practices and uses, preparing and
                    carrying out, on its own initiative or at the request of Securitize, reports,
                    recommendations and proposals in relation to these issues.

        iii.​       Ensure compliance with the information obligations imposed by the Order
                    referred to above in accordance with the provisions of Article 13 of these
                    Regulations.

        iv.​        Prepare an annual report explaining the performance of its function in
                    accordance with the provisions of Article 20 of these Regulations.




Article 10.- Structure

The SAC will be composed of its head and the auxiliary staff that, where appropriate,
the Administrative Body of the Entity deems appropriate at any given time, in view of
the needs and workload of the SAC itself.

The SAC shall have the necessary and adequate human, material, technical and
organisational resources for the fulfilment of its functions and shall adopt the
necessary training measures to ensure that its staff has adequate knowledge of the
regulations on transparency and customer protection, the obligations arising from
contracts for products and services and the requirements of good financial practices
and uses.

Article 11.- Conflict of Interest

11.1.​ The SAC will autonomously resolve the Complaints and will be separated from
       the other commercial services of Securitize, in order to avoid conflicts of
       interest.



                                                                                               7
11.2.​ Those members of the SAC who may have any type of personal interest in the
       complaints or claims issued by Securitize clients, must inform the SAC Holder
       immediately, refraining from continuing with the management of the Complaint
       in question.

11.3.​ Complaints in which it is the Owner of the SAC who has an interest in which a
       conflict of interest occurs or may occur with the Complaint, will refrain from
       participating in the management of the same, and its resolution corresponds to
       the person who occupies the most relevant position in the Customer Service.



Article 12.- Collaboration and Supervision

12.1.​ All departments of Securitize must lend their support to the SAC and
       collaborate in everything that favors the best exercise of their functions. In
       particular, they must provide any information requested by the latter in relation
       to the exercise of their functions in accordance with the principles of speed,
       safety, efficiency and coordination.

12.2.​ Any department of the entity that receives a complaint or claim and considers
       that it cannot be resolved within the framework of its usual activity in favor of
       the client, must immediately forward it to the SAC, informing the complainant
       that the competence to hear it corresponds to the SAC.

12.3.​ The SAC may also request the assistance of external experts in those matters
       submitted to its consideration that, due to their special complexity or
       significance, in its opinion, require it.




Article 13.- Duty to provide information

Securitize will provide its Clients with information, contact details of the SAC and
procedure for exercising the right to submit claims as Securitize Clients in person, or
through a representative, in the latter case providing sufficient proof of such
representation, at Securitize's address, as well as on its website, indicating at least
the following information about the service:

   a)​ The existence of the SAC, with an indication of the postal and electronic
       address where to send the claim.

   b)​ The obligation to attend to and resolve the Claims submitted by the Clients
       within two (2) months from the date of their submission, starting the

       calculation of the filing of the Claim, which will reflect the date of submission
       by any of the means indicated in article 15 of these Regulations.

   c)​ The existence of the CNMV Claims Service with specification of its postal and

   d)​ That of the need to exhaust the SAC service channel in order to be able to
       formulate complaints and operations before the CNMV's Claims Service.

   e)​ These Regulations for the Protection of the Client.

                                     CHAPTER III

                         Procedure for Handling Complaints

Article 14.- Form of notifications and requirements.

The notifications and requirements that the Customer Service must make to the
claimant will be made in the manner expressly designated by the Client and, failing
that, through the same means in which the Claim was submitted.

Article 15.- Presentation of Claims: Form, place and deadline.

15.1.​ Claims may be submitted, in person or by proxy, on paper or by computer,
       electronic or telematic means, provided that these allow the documents to be
       read, printed and preserved.

15.2.​ The use of computer, electronic or telematic means must comply with the
       requirements set out in Law 59/2003, of 19 December, on electronic
       signatures, although the SAC may adopt flexible criteria in the acceptance of
       such complaints or claims if they do not comply with all the requirements of
       the aforementioned law.

15.3.​ The procedure shall be initiated by the submission of a document stating:

   a)​ Name, surname and address of the interested party and, where applicable, of
       the person representing him/her, duly accredited; National identity card or
       passport number for natural persons and data referring to the public registry
       for legal persons.


                                                                                      9
   b)​ Reason for the Complaint, with clear specification of the issues on which a
       ruling is requested and the financial amount claimed, if any.

   c)​ That the claimant is not aware that the subject matter of the Claim is being
       substantiated through an administrative, arbitral or judicial proceeding.

   d)​ Place, date and signature.

15.4.​ The claimant must provide, together with the above document, the
       documentary evidence in his/her possession on which his/her Claim is based
       and, where applicable, the document that proves the representation.

15.5.​ Complaints may be submitted to the SAC at Securitize's postal address or by
       e-mail to the following address: support-eu@securitize.io

15.6.​ Claims must be submitted by the Clients within a maximum period of two (2)
       years from the date on which they became aware of the facts giving rise to the
       claim.

Article 16.- Admission to processing.

16.1.​ Once the Claim is received by Securitize, it will be forwarded to the SAC
       Holder. Once the Claim has been received, the file will be opened and the
       maximum period of two months for processing will begin.

16.2.​ The SAC shall acknowledge receipt in writing, shall provide the Complaining
       Client with the claim number assigned for follow-up, and shall record the date
       of submission for the purpose of calculating such period.

16.3.​ The Service will keep a record, which will be kept for five years, of all
       complaints and claims referred to it, including:

   a)​ copy of written complaints and claims;

   b)​ copy of written responses to each complaint or claim;

   c)​ details of the actions taken to investigate the reasons for the complaint; and

   d)​ Details of the actions aimed at resolving the complaint or claim, once these
       procedures have been completed, a file will be opened.

16.4.​ If the SAC does not find the identity of the claimant sufficiently accredited, or
       the facts that are the subject of the Claim cannot be clearly established, the


                                                                                     10
     signatory will be required to clarify the facts or complete the documentation
     submitted within ten (10) calendar days, with a warning that if they do not do
     so, the Claim will be filed without further proceedings. all this without
     prejudice to the right that the Client may have to file a new Claim in this regard
     under the terms established in these Regulations.

16.5.​ The period used by the claimant to clarify the facts and/or complete the
       documentation referred to in the preceding paragraph shall not be included in
       the calculation of the maximum period provided for in Article 19 of these
       Regulations for resolving the Claim.

16.6.​ The SAC may only refuse to admit a complaint in the following cases:

   a)​ When essential data for the processing that cannot be remedied is omitted,
       including in this regard, when the reason for the Complaint is not specified.

   b)​ When they are intended to be processed as claims, appeals or different
       actions whose knowledge is within the competence of the administrative,
       arbitral or judicial bodies, or when the claim is pending resolution or litigation
       or has already been resolved before an administrative, arbitral or judicial
       instance.

   c)​ When the facts, reasons and request in which the issues that are the subject
       of the complaint are specified do not refer to specific transactions or the
       submitter does not meet the condition of Client or does not affect their legally
       recognized interests and rights in accordance with the provisions of article 4
       of these Regulations.

   d)​ When the claim reiterates a previous claim, filed by the same Client in
       relation to the same facts.

   e)​ When the time limit for the submission of complaints referred to in Article
       15.6 has elapsed. of this Regulation.

16.7.​ When the SAC becomes aware of the simultaneous processing of a complaint
       or claim and an administrative, arbitral or judicial proceeding on the same
       matter, it must refrain from processing the former.

16.8.​ When it is understood that the Claim is not admissible for processing, for any
       of the reasons indicated, the interested party will be informed by means of a
       reasoned decision, giving them a period of ten (10) calendar days to present
       their allegations. When the interested party has replied and the grounds for
       inadmissibility are maintained, the final decision taken will be communicated
       to him.

                                                                                      11
16.9.​ Likewise, when the claimant completes the information of his complaint or
       claim, because his identity has not been sufficiently accredited or when the
       facts that are the subject of the complaint or claim cannot be clearly
       established, outside the period of ten calendar days referred to above, a new
       procedure will be opened for the same facts.

Article 17.- Processing

17.1.​ The SAC is responsible for dealing with the customer's query/complaint and
       ensuring that it is resolved quickly, appropriately and fairly. In this regard, the
       SAC Holder shall be responsible for ensuring that the complaint is resolved as
       quickly as possible in accordance with the regulations in force.

17.2.​ In the course of the processing of the file, the SAC may obtain from both the
       claimant and the various departments and services of Securitize, any data,
       clarifications, reports or evidence it deems relevant to make its decision.

17.3.​ In the processing of complaints, the SAC will adopt the necessary measures to
       ensure compliance with the regulations on the protection of personal data, as
       they are in force at any given time.

17.4.​ Complaints will be processed in Spanish. However, documents drawn up in
       other languages that the SAC considers relevant for the adoption of the
       decision may be included in the file without the need to translate them.

Article 18.- Search and Withdrawal.

18.1.​ If, in view of the Claim, Securitize rectifies its situation with the claimant to the
       satisfaction of the latter, it must notify the Holder of the SAC and justify it with
       documentation, unless there is express withdrawal by the interested party. In
       such cases, the Claim will be archived without further proceedings.

18.2.​ The Client may withdraw its claims at any time, which will lead to the
       immediate termination of the procedure as far as the Client is concerned.
       However, if the SAC, within the framework of its function of promoting
       compliance with the rules on transparency and customer protection and good
       financial practices and usages, deems it appropriate, it may decide to continue
       the procedure.

Article 19.- Resolution and Notification.

19.1.​ The complaint file must be completed within a maximum period of two (2)
       months, counted from the date on which the Complaint was submitted, in


                                                                                         12
     accordance with Order ECO/734/2004, of 11 March, on customer service
     departments and services and the customer ombudsman of financial
     institutions.

19.2.​ The decision will always be reasoned and will contain clear conclusions on the
       request made in each Complaint, based on the contractual clauses, the
       applicable rules of transparency and customer protection, as well as good
       financial practices and usages.

19.3.​ The decision will be notified to the claimant within ten (10) calendar days from
       its date, which will expressly mention the right to appeal to the CNMV Claims
       Service in the event of disagreement with the outcome of the decision. Such
       notification shall be made in writing or by computer, electronic or telematic
       means, provided that these allow the reading, printing and preservation of the
       documents and comply with the requirements established in Law 59/2003, of
       19 December, on electronic signature, as expressly designated by the claimant
       and, in the absence of such indication, through the same means in which the
       Claim was submitted.



Article 20.- Annual Report

In the first quarter of each year, the Holder of the SAC shall submit to the Board of
Directors of Securitize a report on the activities of the previous year, which shall
contain the following information:

   a)​ Statistical summary of Complaints dealt with with information on their
       number, admissibility for processing, reasons for inadmissibility if applicable,
       questions raised and amounts affected.

   b)​ Summary of the decisions taken, indicating whether they are favourable or
       unfavourable to the claimant.

   c)​ General criteria considered for decisions.

   d)​ Recommendations or suggestions derived from their experience, with a view
       to better achieving the purposes that inform the actions of the SAC.
