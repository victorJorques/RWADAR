1.​ Introduction

The purpose of this circular is to describe the procedures to manage the delivery
of transferable securities (tokens in all cases) and their payment (tokens in web 3
trading mode and cash in a cash account in classic mode) and the determination
of the settlement finality for the change of ownership1.

Securitize's market provides settlement services for the matching of orders traded
between Issuers and Market Members.

There are two distinct trading modes: Classic Mode and Web 3 Mode. These
modes are not interchangeable. The transfer of securities occurs through the
registration of the transfer in the registry system, which is equivalent to the
delivery of the security. Please note that in any case securities are always onchain.

2.​ Common features in both Classic Trading Mode and Web 3 Mode

2.1.​ Order matching

Orders introduced in the market shall be limited orders, where Market Members
specify the maximum execution price for the purchases and the minimum
execution price for sales2.


Matching takes place off-chain, regardless of the market mode, in Securitize’s
Platform. Then, the orderbook informs us of a match between two orders. Orders at
the same price are ranked and executed based on strict time priority.


The order is executed only if the market reaches this limit price for order execution.
If the maximum price is reached but there is an insufficient volume or
counterparties of digital securities at that price, the order will be partially executed,
and the remaining amount remains in the order book available for a potential
subsequent match.

2.2.​Trading time

Trading in both Web3 and Classic modes will follow the continuous quotation
method during market hours (08:00 to 20:00 CET, Monday to Friday). Securitize
will act as the entity responsible for the administration and registration of Digital
Securities. Orders registered in the order book will be executed according to strict
price priority4.

2.3.​Execution

Transactions take place via swaps, and if any of the required elements are not
present (pre-fund), the transaction will not be executed. In the case of the Classic
Mode, there are no risks of this occurring because the securities are held in
custody by the Securitize's omnibus account; however, in the case of the Web 3
Mode, this is addressed through the design of the platform's Smart Contracts and
automatic procedures, which have detailed rules assigned that define the

2.4.​Settlement finality

The maximum time, depending on the traffic, for initiating the transaction in the
Avalanche network is 30 seconds.

Once the transaction has been executed, the time required to consider a
transaction final (that is, every view of the blockchain shows the same transaction)
in the Avalanche DLT is less than 2 seconds. This is the moment when SF1-SF2
and SF3 (as defined by the EU Settlement Finality Directive) are simultaneously
reached.

         ●​ SF1 - the moment of the entry into the system of settlement
            instructions;
         ●​ SF2 - the moment of the irrevocability of settlement instructions
            introduced in the system and properly validated
         ●​ SF3 - the time of settlement finality

The settlement finality moments occur simultaneously, and finality are reached
almost in real time following matching once the transaction is executed, recorded
and final on the DLT.

Please note that Securitize uses Avalanche blockchain, which is network with
deterministic finality with 1 block and less than 2 seconds for finality. Once a block
has been confirmed it becomes irreversible and cannot be modified or reverted.

3.​ Classic Trading Mode

3.1.​ Execution

Delivery vs payment: The execution is atomic and immediate upon a match. Cash
settlement is simultaneous and inseparable from securities settlement, with no
risk that one leg of the trade is settled while the other fails as the assets involved
are under Securitize’s custody, thus not available to be withdrawn by Market
Members until settlement has been recorded.

3.2.​​ Settlement procedures

Once the order is admitted in the orderbook (off-chain), Market Members are
informed both via email and on the platform.​ Then, the execution of these steps is
immediate and with no settlement risk. Cash settlement is simultaneous and
inseparable from securities settlement, with no risk that one leg of the trade is
settled while the other fails as the assets involved are under Securitize’s custody,
thus not available to be withdrawn by Market Members until settlement has been
recorded on chain. the steps of the procedure, upon a match notification from the
orderbook, are:



1.​       Transfer Security Tokens: Securitize internally records the change of
          ownership of the security tokens: Security tokens change of ownership
          from the seller’s Securities Client Account to the buyers’ by updating the
          internal record.
2.​       Transfer Cash Balance: Securitize records the change of cash ownership,
          updating the personal cash balances of the Market Members involved.
          There are no cash movements resulting from settlement in the omnibus
          account opened with the credit institution, but in the individual balances
          recognized in Securitize as 'investment firm' for each Market Member.
3.​       Trade fees collection: Securitize records the change of ownership of the
          cash equivalent to the amount of the fees that had been communicated to
          the Market Members when the orders were created: The seller receives as
          cash the matched amount minus the fees. The amount of the fees is
          transferred to the Securitize's Fee Collection Account.


Finally, The DLT will be notified of the settlement via an event. In addition, the
platform records and updates transactions on the blockchain once settlement is
confirmed by creating and submitting an emitMatchEvent() transaction. And then,
this transaction emits a MatchPerformed() event to reflect the change in asset
ownership due to the trade. Investors are informed of the on-chain record via the
Securitize platform and email.

These changes of ownership will be almost automatically less, in normal
circumstances, than 2 seconds (maximum block time in Avalanche)- and
simultaneously recorded in Securitize’s records on-chain and off-chain.

These records ensure ongoing reconciliation between the ERIR registers and the
securities account balances in Classic Trading Mode.

After recording the settlement finality, if the buyer wants to withdraw his security
tokens to a personal wallet, he can request it on the platform and Securitize will
perform an ERC20 transfer to this effect (assuming the assets can be withdrawn
because they are not subject to any kind of restriction, for instance due to open
orders). Likewise, from the moment the settlement has been recorded on the
blockchain, the seller can request the withdrawal of his cash in Securitize’s
frontend and Securitize will send via transfer the cash to the buyer’s bank
account.

4.​ Web 3 Mode

4.1.​ Execution

The orderbook informs internally of a potential match between two orders. At this
point, none of the orders are considered as matched because Securitize needs to
verify that the allowance and balance of e-money/security tokens in each wallet is
enough for proceeding with the execution (This verification is done twice: one
pre-execution attempt, and the second one after the execution attempt). This stage
prevents racing conditions leading to the failure of the execution due to unilateral
reductions of e-money/security tokens, balance or allowance by the participants.
This is not a settlement failure because settlement takes place after the execution
success. None of the participants are notified yet about a match of their orders.
Next, a verification is conducted, using a Blockchain node, for checking that all
wallets participating count with enough balance and allowance for the trade. If an
inconsistency is detected, an internal alert is created, and any order from a wallet
that doesn’t meet the balance/allowance requirements is removed from the
orderbook. The timeframe for this, is the size of milliseconds. After this step, the
potential match is declared a confirmed match.
Finally, the Smart Contract is notified to process the atomic execution. When the
smart contract receives this instruction, the following actions are executed
atomically as part of a single transaction within a single “blockchain block”:
         ●​ Verify that there are sufficient tokens (both cash and securities) to
            execute
         ●​ Transfer security tokens from the seller wallet to the buyer wallet in
            the amount defined by the matched orders - by executing an ERC20

            allow the transfer of the tokens due to the pre-existing allowance
            provided by the wallet holder during order creation.
         ●​ Transfer cash tokens from the buyer wallet to the seller wallet in the

            executing an ERC20 transferFrom operation on the security token
            smart contract, which will allow the transfer of the tokens due to the
            pre-existing allowance provided by the wallet holder during order

         ●​ Transfer cash tokens from the buyer's wallet to a wallet controlled by
            the market operator in the amount defined by the fees (those
            corresponding to buyer and seller – seller receives net cash amount)
            communicated to investors - by executing an ERC20 transferFrom
            operation on the security token smart contract, which will allow the
            transfer of the tokens due to the pre-existing allowance provided by
            the wallet holder during order creation.
After the match notification, the trade may not be executed if either of the Market
Members (i) have reduced their allowance linked to the transaction, or (ii) directly
withdrawn the secutity tokens or emoney tokens required by the transaction, from
their personal wallet. So there is no risk that any of the Market Members would
lose their security tokens or emoney tokens. The timeframe for the execution is
2-32 seconds. In this extremely rare eventuality, the order that caused the failure
will be removed from the orderbook, and the consistent one will remain unaltered,
with no harm done to the rightful participant order.
4.2.​Settlement procedures

When the execution is successful, the settlement is recorded and published by the
blockchain, constituting the public record for the trade.

Once the transaction has been executed, the time required to consider a
transaction final (that is, every view of the blockchain shows the same transaction)
in the Avalanche DLT is lower than 2 seconds. This is the moment when SF1-SF2
and SF31 are simultaneously reached.

Also, fully matched orders are removed, partially matched orders remain (with their
amount reduced) and their matching preference unaltered, and market members
are informed in Securitize’s platform and via email.




     8
When the execution is not successful because any of the smart contract
transactions failed in the execution stage, the operator creates an internal alert
and a record and removes the order that caused the execution failure. This implies
that (1) No Security tokens have been transferred, (2) No e-money tokens have
been transferred, (3) Consistent orders are still on the orderbook keeping their
matching preference, (4) non-consistent orders have been removed from the
orderbook.

Finally, records of the trade and order updates are stored and kept for post trade
obligations.

    5.​ Insolvency of Market Members

Securitize settlement system has not been designated under the provisions of
Directive 98/26/EC on settlement finality in payment and securities settlement
systems (hereinafter, "Settlement Finality Directive"). Consequently, transactions
settled through this system are not subject to the protections provided by the
Directive, which safeguards against the retroaction of transactions in the event of
the insolvency of a market member.

      Should a Market Member (either acting on its own account or as an
Intermediary5) be involved in any insolvency proceedings, the following procedures
must be followed:

      ●​       Obligation of the Market Member to Notify Securitize: In the event that a
               Market Member becomes insolvent or bankrupt, or any insolvency
               proceedings are initiated, the Market Member must immediately notify
               Securitize. Failure to notify will be considered a material breach of the
               Market Member’s obligations6.

      ●​ Account and Order Management: Securitize will promptly lock the SiD
         account of the insolvent Market Member, halting all access to the platform.
         All pending purchase and sale orders, including those submitted prior to
         notification, will be cancelled. Orders placed in a Web3 mode configured
         trading asset will also be annulled.
