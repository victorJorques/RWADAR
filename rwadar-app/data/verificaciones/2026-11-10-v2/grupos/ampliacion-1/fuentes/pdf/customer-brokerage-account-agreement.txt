will maintain an account (“Account”) for the Customer(s) set forth below (all or any of which shall
be hereinafter referred to as “you” “your” or “yours”) and receive from you orders for the purchase
and sale of tokenized equity securities, including Issuer Sponsored Tokens, Custodial Entitlement
Tokens and Entitlement Tokens, each as described in Section 29, together with the stablecoins used
to settle transactions in such securities and other related property (“Securities and Other Assets”).
Your Account is a fully paid brokerage account, meaning that transactions must be fully funded at
the time of execution and settlement, and no extension of credit is provided by SM; however,
settlement may occur using stablecoins or other permitted digital assets, and Securities and Other
Assets may be held either (i) in custody by SM, or other qualified custodian, or (ii) in a self-custody
wallet linked by you to the platform and approved by SM (a “Self-Custody Wallet”), as further
described in this Agreement. Your account maintained by SM will be designated as either SM
Custody, Self Custody, or Both, depending on whether you request SM to open an SM Custody
account on the UI.
