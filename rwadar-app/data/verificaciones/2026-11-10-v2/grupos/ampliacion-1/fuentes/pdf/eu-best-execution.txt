    1.​ Introduction

The Directive 2014/65/EU of the European Parliament and of the Council of 15 May 2014
on markets in financial instruments (“MiFID II”) requires investment firms to take all
sufficient steps to obtain, when executing orders, the best possible result for their clients
taking into account price, costs, speed, likelihood of execution and settlement, size, nature
or any other consideration relevant to the execution of the order.

In addition, MiFID II also imposes the obligation for investment firms authorized to execute
orders on behalf of clients to implement procedures and arrangements which provide for
the prompt, fair and expeditious execution of client orders, relative to other client orders or
the trading interests of the investment firm.

These obligations have been developed by the Commission Delegated Regulation (EU)
2017/565 of 25 April 2016 supplementing Directive 2014/65/EU of the European
Parliament and of the Council as regards organizational requirements and operating
conditions for investment firms and defined terms for the purposes of that Directive (the
“Delegated Regulation 2017/565”) on its Chapter III, Sections 5 and 6.

In Spain, these requirements have been transposed into Law 6/2023, of 17 March, on
Securities Markets and Investment Services (“Law 6/2023”), on its Title VIII, Chapter I,
Section 8, and developed by article 141 of Royal Decree 813/2023, of 8 November, on the
legal regime governing investment firms and other entities providing investment services
(“Royal Decree 813/2023”).

    (a)​ Reception and transmission of orders: when Securitize Europe transmits client

    3.​ Execution criteria

When executing client orders, Securitize Europe will consider the following criteria for
determining the relative importance of the factors referred to in article 27(1) MiFID II:

    (a)​ The characteristics of the client, including the categorization of the client as retail
         or professional.
    (b)​ The characteristics of the client order.
    (c)​ The characteristics of financial instruments that are the subject of that order.
    (d)​ The characteristics of the execution venues to which that order can be directed.

To that effect, where Securitize Europe executes an order on behalf of a retail client, the
best possible result will be determined in terms of the total consideration, representing
the price of the financial instrument and the costs relating to execution, including all
expenses incurred by the client which are directly relating to the execution of the order,
such as execution venue fees, any other fees paid to third parties involved in the execution
of the order. Securitize Europe will not structure or charge their commissions in such a
way as to discriminate unfairly between execution venues.

For professional clients, the best possible result will be determined by the likelihood of
execution and settlement, as well as speed, owing to the currently limited availability of
tokenized financial instruments.

   4.​ Broker / execution venue selection

When Securitize Europe executes orders in over-the counter products, including bespoke
products, Securitize will check the fairness of the price proposed to the client, by
gathering market data used in the estimation of the price of such product and, where
possible, by comparing with similar or comparable products.

   5.​ Client order handling

Securitize Europe, when carrying out client orders will:

   (i)​     ensure that orders executed on behalf of clients are promptly and accurately
            recorded and allocated;
   (ii)​    carry out otherwise comparable client orders sequentially and promptly unless
            the characteristics of the order or prevailing market conditions make this
            impracticable, or the interests of the client require otherwise; and
   (iii)​   inform a retail client about any material difficulty relevant to the proper carrying
            out of orders promptly upon becoming aware of the difficulty.

Where Securitize Europe is responsible for overseeing or arranging the settlement of an
executed order, it will take all reasonable steps to ensure that any client financial
instruments or client funds or tokens received in settlement of that executed order are
promptly and correctly delivered to the account of the appropriate client.

Securitize Europe will under no circumstances aggregate client orders with own account
transactions, nor with other client orders. Given that Securitize Europe exclusively
provides investment services in relation to tokenized financial instruments, client orders

   6.​ Disclosure of the Policy and prior client consent

Securitize Europe will provide appropriate information to any clients and potential clients
on the Policy. Annex I of this Policy includes the summary of the Policy that will be
available on Securitize Europe website, explaining clearly, in sufficient detail and in a way
that can be easily understood by clients, how orders will be executed or transmitted by
Securitize Europe. Securitize Europe will also inform its clients of any material changes to
the Policy or to its execution arrangements.

Upon reasonable request, Securitize Europe will provide its clients or potential clients with
information about entities where the orders are transmitted or placed for execution.

Before delivering any investment services, Securitize Europe will obtain the client’s prior
consent to the Policy, through the acceptance of Securitize Europe’s Terms and
Conditions. Securitize Europe is also able to demonstrate to clients, upon request, that
their orders were carried out according to the Policy.

Securitize Europe informs clients that there is a possibility their orders may be executed
outside an EU trading venue, such as in a US execution venue or platform, or through a
counterparty that can be located within or outside the EU. In accordance with art. 27(5)
MiFID II, Securitize Europe will obtain the prior express consent of clients before

      7.​ Review of the Policy

Securitize Europe will review the Policy, its annexes and execution arrangements annually,
or whenever material changes2 arise that can impact its ability to achieve the best
outcome for clients. Securitize Europe will determine if a material change has occurred
and may adjust execution venues or entities to maintain best execution.

Securitize Europe will inform its clients of any material changes to the Policy or to its
execution arrangements.

Securitize Europe will also monitor on a regular basis the execution quality of the entities
and execution venues to identify and correct any potential deficiencies.
