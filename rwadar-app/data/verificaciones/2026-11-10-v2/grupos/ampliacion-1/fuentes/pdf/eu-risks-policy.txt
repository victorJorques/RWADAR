SECTION 1: IDENTIFIED RISKS

In order to comply with all current legal requirements, Securitize has
developed a non-exhaustive list of risks of various kinds, which are expected
to affect the company's normal operations at some point in time. Therefore,
the possible risks identified by Securitize are detailed as follows:

   a)​ Solvency Risk: Risk of non-compliance with the solvency requirements
       established by the applicable legal regulations.

   b)​ Market Risk: With respect to the activity of trading on own account,
       risk of loss in the face of future adverse movements in the risk factors
       of financial instruments in the financial markets in which positions are
       held.

   c)​ Operational Risk: Risk of unforeseen losses arising from significant
       deficiencies in the integrity or confidence in the systems implemented
       due to deficiencies or failures in internal processes, information
       technology, people or occurrences of adverse external events.

   d)​ Compliance Risk: Risk defined as the possibility of incurring legal or
       administrative penalties, significant financial loss or loss of reputation
       due to non-compliance with laws, regulations, internal rules and codes
       of conduct applicable to investment services.

   e)​ Interest rate risk in positions outside the trading book: Risk arising
       from possible variations in interest rates in activities other than the
       trading book.

   f)​ Business Risk: It derives from the operations related to the main
       activities of the Securitize, as well as from the competition they may
       face with the rest of the companies that participate in their market.

   g)​ Reputation Risk: Risk of losses that may result as a result of not
       realizing business opportunities attributable to the discredit that falls
       on Securitize, due to the lack of training of key personnel, fraud or
       errors in the execution of any operation.

   h)​ Strategic Risk: Risk generated by changes of great relevance in the
       economic and political environment and internal risks such as
       erroneous or incorrectly implemented business decisions, poor
       management quality, ineffective management and supervision of

       environment in which Securitize carries out its activity.
   i)​ Risk of Non-suitability: Risk that any member of the administrative
       body or senior management or responsible for control functions of
       Securitize does not comply with the suitability requirements that apply
       to him.

   j)​ Cybersecurity Risk: Risk of vulnerability to cyber-attacks, which may
       result in disruption of the operations of the SMN managed by
       Securitize, loss of data, and exposure of confidential customer
       information.

SECTION 2: GENERAL COMPLIANCE AND RISK CONTROL PRINCIPLES

Securitize shall develop and implement appropriate and reliable control
systems, procedures enabling the identification, measurement, monitoring,
evaluation and continuous communication of risks in accordance with the
following principles:

   a)​ Eliminate or reduce any risk that may endanger:

         a.​ The continuity and regularity of the services provided by the
             entity.

         b.​ Compliance with the level of own resources required and other
             financial requirements.

         c.​ Compliance with the applicable regulations on financial risks.

   b)​ Describe the methodologies, procedures and responsibilities related to
       the measurement and control of risks assumed, according to the
       following:

         a.​ Define the types of risks to which Securitize is exposed derived
             from the development of its activities and the risks derived from
             any other service provided by Securitize.

         b.​ Describe the overall risk level approved by the Board of
             Directors and the specific risk levels established.

         c.​ Determine the tools and procedures for the measurement and
             control of identified risks.

          d.​ Identify the areas involved in the detection, measurement and
              control of risks, as well as the functions they perform.

          e.​ Establish the authorization procedure to exceed the maximum
              risk limits established.

          f.​ Generate all the necessary information and reports on the work
              carried out for the corresponding areas of Securitize and the
              supervisory bodies.

   c)​ Perform the risk management function independently of the activities
       carried out by the organizational units of Securitize to ensure adequate
       information for decision- making at all levels.

   d)​ Actively manage the entire life of the risk, which implies the monitoring
       of the complete cycle of operations, from their prior analysis and
       approval to the extinction of the risk.

SECTION 3: RISK CONTROL

Depending on the type of risk faced by Securitize, Securitize has decided to
appoint the following persons or departments that will ensure, in any case, to
identify the risks that may jeopardize the normal operation of the platform or
any other process that has to do with it.

In this sense (although subject to further modifications and adjustments), the
Director of Regulatory Compliance and Customer Service together with Chief
Risk Officer will issue a Regulatory Compliance and Risk Management report
on an annual basis, aimed at covering the following aspects:

   ●​ the controls carried out for each type of risk identified, as well as the
      incidents and possible sources of risk detected, together with the
      proposals for measures considered appropriate, as well as considering
      the review of the Risk Management Policy.

This report will be received by the General Director and the Board of Directors
that will then decide further actions or measures if deemed appropriate.

Additionally, the audit firm that shall be appointed by Securitize, shall issue a
report on internal audit on an annual basis, aimed at covering the following
aspects:

   ●​ the adequacy, efficiency and effectiveness of the internal control
      system implemented by the Entity and the functions of regulatory
      compliance and management of risks, and formulation of
      recommendations that contribute to the improvement of systems and
      control procedures.

This report will be received by the General Director and the Board of Directors
that will then decide further actions or measures if deemed appropriate.

Furthermore, the internal control body (Coordination and Monitoring
Committee) will issue, together with Regulatory Compliance, Customer Care
and the Risk Department, a report on an annual basis, aimed at covering the
following aspects:

   ●​ actions and incidents in the field of prevention of money laundering
      and, where appropriate, modifications required in the Manual for the
      Prevention of Money Laundering.

This report will be received by the General Director and the Board of Directors
that will then decide further actions or measures if deemed appropriate.

Lastly, the Customer Service department, together with Regulatory
Compliance, will issue a report, on an annual basis, aimed at covering the
following aspects:

   ●​ actions and incidents in the Customer Service area.

This report will be received by the General Director and the Board of Directors
that will then decide further actions or measures if deemed appropriate.

SECTION 4: SPECIFIC MEASURES

Due to the recent establishment of the company in Spain, the exact
procedures or techniques to address the risks and safeguard the interests of
Securitize and its users have not yet been concretely detailed.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, Securitize will develop these techniques and
procedures as soon as possible and include them in this Circular in order to
comply with the requirements of the European and Spanish competent
authorities and applicable legislation.
