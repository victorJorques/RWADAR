5.9 Convertible Entitlement Tokens

A "Convertible Entitlement Token" or "CET" is a digital token that represents your security entitlement under Article 8 of the Uniform Commercial Code, on a 1:1 basis, to a corresponding share of common stock of a publicly traded company (a "Reference Security"). The Reference Security is held in book-entry form through custodial and sub-custodial arrangements maintained by or on behalf of Securitize Markets for the benefit of its customers, and Securitize Markets credits a position in the Reference Security to your account on its books and records, separate from its proprietary holdings. CETs may be offered on the Platform under the name "Securitize Stocks." The Disclosure Document describes the liquidity and secondary-market risks that apply specifically to CETs.

Each CET is, at initial launch, offered by Securitize Markets rather than by the issuer of the Reference Security, and the issuer of the Reference Security has not sponsored, endorsed, or participated in the CET. If an issuer later establishes an issuer-sponsored token program and identifies Securitize Markets as an authorized sponsor, outstanding CETs for that Reference Security may convert into issuer-sponsored tokens as described in the Disclosure Document. Before placing an order for a CET, you will be shown a disclosure at the point of order entry identifying the instrument as a CET and distinguishing it from an issuer-sponsored token, which you must acknowledge before your order can be submitted.

As a holder of a CET, you receive the economic benefits and entitlements passed through from the Reference Security, including cash dividends, net of applicable tax withholding, and other distributions, as described in the Disclosure Document. Where the Reference Security carries voting rights, you may instruct Securitize Markets how to exercise the voting rights attributable to the Reference Security underlying your CETs. Because your CET represents a security entitlement credited to your account, the Reference Security is held for you as a customer of Securitize Markets for purposes of SIPC protection, subject to the limits described in Section 3.1. SIPC protection does not cover declines in market value or losses arising from the compromise of a Whitelisted Wallet.

Access to CETs requires successful completion of Securitize's full Know Your Customer identity verification and Anti-Money Laundering screening. CETs may not be available in all jurisdictions or to all Users. The full terms governing CETs, including entitlements processing, conversion mechanics, and fees, are set out in the Disclosure Document. A commission of 2% applies to each CET purchase transaction, calculated on the gross transaction value, in lieu of the ATS trade commission described in Section 5.5. No commission applies to CET sale transactions. CETs offered to EU users are classified as financial instruments under MiFID II and are not crypto-assets under Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 on markets in crypto-assets.

5.10 Entitlement Tokens

An "Entitlement Token" is a digital token that represents a security entitlement to a Reference Security, where the Reference Security is held by Securitize Markets directly or by a third-party custodian in an account maintained for the benefit of Securitize Markets' customers. Securitize Markets, or its affiliated transfer agent acting as registrar on Securitize Markets' behalf, credits a specific position to you on its own books, separate from Securitize Markets' proprietary holdings and reconciled against the Reference Securities so held. Because a position is specifically referable to your Entitlement Token, you hold a security entitlement under Article 8 of the Uniform Commercial Code with respect to the Reference Security. Unlike a Convertible Entitlement Token, an Entitlement Token does not convert into direct registration of the Reference Security in your name.

In connection with Entitlement Token transactions, Securitize Markets effects the purchase of the Reference Security as part of executing your specific order for the corresponding Entitlement Token, at a price matched to the then-current net asset value, and credits the resulting position to you as described above. Securitize Markets does not maintain an inventory position in a Reference Security beyond what is required to fulfill pending and open Entitlement Token orders, and does not use those Reference Securities for separate proprietary trading activity.

Entitlement Tokens do not convert into an Issuer Sponsored Token or into a Convertible Entitlement Token, and are not affected by an issuer's later adoption of an issuer-sponsored token program. Before placing an order, you will be shown a disclosure identifying the instrument as an Entitlement Token and distinguishing it from a Convertible Entitlement Token and an issuer-sponsored token, which you must acknowledge before your order can be submitted.

Access to Entitlement Tokens requires successful completion of Securitize's full Know Your Customer identity verification and Anti-Money Laundering screening. Entitlement Tokens may not be available in all jurisdictions or to all Users. The full terms governing Entitlement Tokens, including entitlements processing, fees, and the application of SIPC protection, are set out in the Disclosure Document and the Entitlement Token Documents. A commission of 2% applies to each Entitlement Token purchase transaction, calculated on the gross transaction value, in lieu of the ATS trade commission described in Section 5.5. No commission applies to Entitlement Token sale transactions.
