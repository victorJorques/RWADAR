Amundi unveils tokenised access to one of its flagship index funds

Amundi, European leading asset manager and ETF provider1, announces the launch of a tokenised share class of one of its flagship European equity index fund, Amundi Core STOXX Europe 600, a core European equity strategy widely used as a building block for portfolio allocation.

With this launch, Amundi extends tokenisation beyond cash management solutions into index investing, marking a new milestone in its digital asset strategy. By bringing a flagship broad-market equity exposure into a tokenised format, Amundi takes a further step towards connecting traditional investment solutions with emerging digital market infrastructure.

The new share class has been issued on the public Ethereum blockchain. It remains fully integrated into the fund’s existing operating model, and the fund continues to be available also through traditional channels. Developed in collaboration with CACEIS, the solution leverages CACEIS’s technology and infrastructure for the tokenisation of fund units.

This initiative reflects Amundi’s conviction that tokenisation can progressively support a broader range of mainstream investment uses across asset classes. It follows the launch over the past few months of Amundi’s first tokenised share class of a money market fund, subscription via stablecoins as well as a series of partnerships with digital players, large corporates and financial institutions to test concrete applications of distributed ledger technology. 

This phased approach reflects Amundi’s commitment to innovation within a robust regulatory, operational and risk management framework. It also supports Amundi’s ambition to progressively develop a range of tokenised funds to respond to the evolving needs of institutional clients and digital platforms, leveraging its potential to deliver greater operational efficiency through the automation of certain processes, improved accessibility, fast settlement and transparency.

As market infrastructure and regulatory frameworks continue to develop, tokenised fund shares will create new opportunities for digital distribution and applications across asset classes. By extending tokenisation to index funds, Amundi contributes to lay the foundations to bridge traditional finance and digital finance.

About Amundi

Amundi, the leading European asset manager, ranking among the top 10 global players1, offers over 200 million investors a complete range of savings and investment solutions in active and passive management, in listed and private assets. Developed for a range of distributors (banks, wealth managers, financial advisors…) as well as for institutional investors and corporates, this offering is enhanced by services and technology tools covering the entire savings value chain. A subsidiary of the Crédit Agricole group and listed on the stock exchange, Amundi currently manages close to €2.6 trillion of assets2.

Its six international investment hubs3, its financial and extra-financial research capabilities and its long-standing commitment to responsible investment make Amundi a leading player in the international asset management landscape.

Thanks to its strong local presence, particularly in Europe and Asia, Amundi offers its clients the expertise and advice of 5,400 professionals across 34 countries.
