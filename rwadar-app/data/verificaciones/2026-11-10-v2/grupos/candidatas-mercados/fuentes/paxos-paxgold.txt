 Built on trust. Backed by real gold. 
 Every PAXG token is backed by one fine troy ounce of London Good Delivery gold held in custody by Paxos Trust Company, N.A.
 Secure + regulated
 PAXG is issued by Paxos under the regulatory oversight of the OCC. It’s the largest gold token with federal oversight, and has had regulatory supervision since 2019.
 No settlement risk
 PAXG moves onchain. Transfers settle near-instantly on the blockchain, while traditional gold markets and ETFs can take up to two days to settle transactions.
 Fully redeemable
 PAXG is always redeemable for physical gold, unallocated Loco London Gold or USD at prevailing market prices.
 Gold designed for the benefits of blockchain 
 PAXG offers platforms and developers a new way to offer customers access to institutional-grade gold. Builders are leveraging PAXG in index offerings, savings programs and more.
 1
 Low cost
 Paxos charges no storage fees for PAXG, giving you and your end users more predictable costs.
 2
 More accessible
 PAXG divides into small fractions up to 18 decimals. Customers don’t need to buy a full ounce, and can start with whatever they want to own.
 3
 Composable
 PAXG is a standard token. It plugs into wallets, exchanges, and DeFi protocols without custom integration work.
