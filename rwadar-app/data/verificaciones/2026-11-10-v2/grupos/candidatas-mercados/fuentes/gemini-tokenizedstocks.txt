 Tokenized Stocks are high-risk, complex financial products that may not be suitable for all investors. These derivatives can experience sharp price changes due to market sentiment, economic conditions, earnings reports, interest rate shifts, or geopolitical events, leading to significant losses. They also carry blockchain-specific risks such as loss of access, and system vulnerabilities, as well as traditional derivative risks like liquidity and counterparty exposure. You may lose some or all of your investment. Past performance is not a reliable guide to future performance. You should carefully review the product terms, including the Key Information Document, consider associated fees and charges, and seek independent advice if needed. 
 References to ‘Tokenized Stocks’ refer to dSharesTM, which are unleveraged OTC financial derivative products manufactured by Dinari, Inc that correspond 1:1 to an underlying listed stocks. Further information about Dinari, Inc. and dSharesTM, including the Key Information Documents (KID) and other relevant details, can be found . dSharesTM are issued and sold by Dinari and can generally only be redeemed or sold back to Dinari as the counterparty to the derivative products. Dinari, Inc. is a registered Transfer Agent with the US Securities & Exchange Commission (Section 17A(c)). Gemini EU distributes dSharesTM in certain EU countries.
