 Amundi adds tokenized share class to €22.8bn European equity index fund
 November 5, 2026 
 by Ledger Insights 
 Amundi has launched a tokenized share class of its Core STOXX Europe 600 index fund, the sub-fund behind one of Europe’s most widely held equity ETFs with €22.8 billion in assets under management. Issued on public Ethereum with CACEIS as transfer agent and tokenization agent, it is the first large European equity index fund to add a blockchain-based share class.
 The move extends Amundi’s tokenization program beyond cash into equity index investing. It follows three cash focused initiatives over the past year. In November 2025 Amundi added a tokenized share class to its Amundi Funds Cash EUR money market fund ( MMF ). It then developed tokenized MMF share classes with CACEIS and Ant International, built for the fintech group’s intra-group treasury to enable real time movement of liquidity between Ant entities on chain. 
 In March Amundi became delegated investment manager of the Spiko Amundi Overnight Swap Fund (SAFO), a total return swap product within Spiko’s French SICAV that has gathered roughly $2.5 billion and is the largest product within Spiko’s tokenized fund platform.
