Transparency and proof
Your gold is fully backed and securely safeguarded
Transparency in the custody of tokenised gold
Transparency at Prosegur Digital Gold means providing clear evidence
that each token is backed by real gold.
Transparency
The system is simple: each kilo is divided into 1,000 tokens of 1 g and we apply double protection (gold in a physical bunker and control in a digital bunker).
Physical custody & cybersecurity
Prosegur applies double protection: physical gold in the Prosegur Cash bunker; keys and digital control in the Prosegur Crypto cryptographic bunker with cold storage.
Audits
Independent audits verify that each 1:1 token is backed by physical gold held in custody by Prosegur and that the on-chain record reflects this correspondence. Token ↔ bullion reports and mappings are available for public download with their verification hash.
