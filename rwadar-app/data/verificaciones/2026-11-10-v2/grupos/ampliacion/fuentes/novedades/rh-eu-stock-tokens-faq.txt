How are Classic Stock Tokens different from traditional stocks? Classic Stock Tokens offer many of the same benefits as traditional stocks, but since you do not own the underlying stock, you will not have certain shareholder rights like voting. In addition, unlike with stocks, when you purchase a stock token, you enter into a derivatives contract with Robinhood Europe. How do Classic Stock Tokens work? When buying Classic Stock Tokens, you are not buying the actual stocks—you are buying tokenized contracts that follow their price, recorded on a blockchain. You can buy, sell, or hold Classic Stock Tokens—but you cannot send them to other wallets or platforms at this time. Classic Stock Tokens are displayed in USD . When you place a trade, we automatically convert your euros using the current exchange rate, plus a small 0.10% FX fee. You do not need to convert currencies yourself. What are the benefits of trading Robinhood Classic Stock Tokens? Trading Classic Stock Tokens on Robinhood offers several potential benefits: No hidden fees : Just a 0.1% FX fee on each order. No other fees from Robinhood. Start with just €1 : Access the market on your terms—and earn dividends when eligible. 24-hour market access : Buy and sell Classic Stock Tokens any time, Monday to Friday. Invest with confidence : Robinhood Classic Stock Tokens are offered under MiFID II as derivatives. The underlying assets are held securely by a US-licensed institution. How do I buy and sell Classic Stock Tokens? You can use the search bar to find available Classic Stock Tokens by typing the name or ticker symbol. Search results will show crypto, stock & ETF tokens, and perpetual futures (not available in all jurisdictions). When selling Classic Stock Tokens, you can always sell them for their value in euros. You cannot send Classic Stock Tokens to other wallets or platforms at this time. If you sell Classic Stock Tokens, the proceeds are available for trading instantly. However, you won't be able to withdraw these funds until the next business day after the sale. This withdrawal hold only applies to EUR proceeds from the sale of Classic Stock Tokens. If you have multiple sales across multiple days, the proceeds will be available to withdraw based on the next business day after each individual sale. Review Buy and sell Classic Stock Tokens for more details. How does settlement work for Classic Stock Tokens? Proceeds from selling a tokenized stock position are available for trading instantly (excluding weekends), but there is a T+1 business day hold on withdrawals until FX settlement clears. Are Classic Stock Tokens secure? Robinhood Classic Stock Tokens are offered under MiFID II as derivative contracts. The underlying assets are held securely by a US-licensed institution in a Robinhood Europe account. Robinhood Europe issues the contracts and records them on a blockchain. Review Risks below for additional information. How are Classic Stock Tokens taxed? They’re typically treated like other derivative investments but may vary by region. You can request a CSV report of your transactions directly in the app, starting from the date your account was created. Robinhood does not provide tax advice, and you should consult a tax professional for specific guidance. What stocks are available as tokens? You can view all the currently available Classic Stock Tokens in Explore (magnifying glass) and using the search bar. You can review Search for Classic Stock Tokens for more details on finding stock tokens and other available assets. How are corporate actions handled? Corporate actions on the underlying stock can affect your stock token holdings and trading activity. Types of corporate actions include stock splits, reverse stock splits, ticker changes, mergers & acquisitions (M&A), spin-offs, delistings, liquidations, and dividend payments. Trading for affected Classic Stock Tokens will typically be halted during the processing of a corporate action to ensure safe application. You will receive notifications in the app to inform you about ongoing corporate actions. For more detailed information, check out Corporate actions for Classic Stock Tokens . Risks Risks Classic Stock Tokens are derivative contracts between you and Robinhood. They are priced at the prices of the underlying securities without granting rights to them. Classic Stock Tokens carry a high level of risk and are not appropriate for all investors. Investors may lose up to the full amount of their invested capital due to market conditions or the insolvency of Robinhood.
