How Classic Stock Tokens work How Classic Stock Tokens work When buying Classic Stock Tokens, you are not buying the actual stocks—you are buying tokenized contracts that follow their price, recorded on a blockchain. You can buy, sell, or hold Classic Stock Tokens—but you cannot send them to other wallets or platforms at this time. Classic Stock Tokens are displayed, bought, and sold in USD. When you place a trade, we automatically convert your euros using the current exchange rate, plus a small 0.10% FX fee. You do not need to convert currencies yourself. Trading hours Trading hours Currently, you can trade Classic Stock Tokens from Monday at 02:00 CET/CEST until Saturday at 02:00 CET/CEST (subject to local daylight savings schedules). Outside of trading hours, you can queue buy or sell orders for when the market opens again. The order confirmation screen shows if your order is queued. Keep in mind the following holidays for stock market closures: New Year’s Day Martin Luther King, Jr. Day President’s Day Good Friday Memorial Day Juneteenth Independence Day Labor Day Thanksgiving Day Christmas Day Order types Order types We currently support buy and sell orders using either quantity amounts or EUR values, including whole tokens and fractionals. We plan to support more order types soon. Corporate actions Corporate actions Corporate actions are events that impact the underlying stock, such as splits, reverse splits, ticker changes, mergers & acquisitions, delistings, dividend payments, and spinoffs. These corporate actions will also affect your Classic Stock Tokens. We handle these corporate actions for your Classic Stock Tokens. For example, if the underlying stock undergoes a stock split, we adjust your token holdings proportional to the split ratio. Placing a new order is unavailable during corporate action processing. In most cases, trading is unavailable from the early morning on the effective date (around 2 AM CET/CEST) and resumes once processing is complete, typically by the start of the US market day (around 3:30 PM CET/CEST). You will be notified about corporate actions and trading halts. If an asset is only supporting closing orders (sell orders) due to a pending corporate action, for example during a delisting, you will see banners and/or notifications that reflect this. The same goes for when external exchanges halt trading for an asset. For more details on how specific corporate actions are handled, you can view a corporate actions tracker or review Corporate actions for Classic Stock Tokens .
