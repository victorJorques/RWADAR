Amber Hostelis — formerly known as Liepu Hostelis — is an accommodation facility at Ganību iela 36/48 in Liepāja. SIA “BALVORA Group” has professionally managed the building since 2018. The six-storey building, including the basement, has an area of approximately 4 800 m², 100 rooms and a total capacity of 330 beds. The premises are used to accommodate students, company employees, association members and other guests, as well as for space rental. The CrowdedHero campaign aims to raise funding for further renovation and reconstruction of the building and the purchase of equipment, creating the conditions for higher occupancy and revenue. 4 800 m² Total building area 100 Rooms 330 Beds 2018 Start of management operations Amber Hostelis today Existing accommodation infrastructure in Liepāja The building is already being used to provide accommodation and space rental services. Key risks Investment risks Project risk The business is concentrated in one main accommodation property. There is a risk of fluctuations in occupancy and additional reconstruction costs. Lease risk The property is leased. Changes to the lease terms or the relationship with the lessor may affect the company’s operations and cash flow. Financial and default risk The company’s equity was negative at the end of 2025. This increases the risk that the issuer may be unable to meet its obligations to bondholders in full or on time. Revenue risk The ability to pay interest and repay principal depends on hostel occupancy and accommodation and rental income. Liquidity risk There is no active secondary market for the digital bonds. Transfers are restricted and take place through the designated whitelisting mechanism. Sector and cost risk The accommodation sector is affected by competition, as well as increases in utility, labour and property maintenance costs.
