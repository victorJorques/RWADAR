Prerequisites The minimum amount to be invested € 100 (variable per project) Price List: Registration on the Platform free of charge Administration fee * (to cover administration and processing fee) 1.25 percent of the amount invested per a project Performance-based win-win price list: 1. Success fee - dividend fee chargeable starting from the second year after the date of closure of the investment campaign for a Project 5.5% calculated from the return on investment (dividends) and deducted from the payout to the investor at the time of the payout (in case it occurs). The net dividend amount is disbursed to the Investor account after deducting the win-win dividend fee. 2. Win-win exit fee (charged in the case of a successful exit) 9% calculated based on the capital gain** from the investment and deducted from the payout to the investor at the time of the payout. The net profit*** amount is distributed to the Investor account after deducting the win-win exit fee. * the processing fee will be refunded if the Non-Sophisticated Investor withdraws his/her/their offer to invest in an expression of interest in the Crowdfunding Offer during the pre-contractual reflection period. ** Capital gains are profits earned from selling an investment or financial asset. *** Net profit is the income received from selling an investment or financial asset, after deducting all expenses.
