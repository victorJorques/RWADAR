Stress-free onboarding

Simple registration

Get started in minutes with our intuitive onboarding tool. Submit standard KYC details for yourself or your investing entity — autosave ensures you can pause and return anytime without losing progress.

Team collaboration

Easily invite team members or third-party partners such as accountants and fund administrators. Manage roles and permissions to maintain control and transparency across your organization.

Wallet configuration

Add your Ethereum, Solana, or Plume wallet addresses to the Allowlist. Both custodial and non-custodial wallets are supported, enabling you to hold onchain equities and tokenized funds securely.

Asset onboarding

Once your account is active, you can seamlessly hold onchain equities. Qualified Purchasers can also apply to private funds such as USTB and USCC in just a few clicks.

Flexible ownership

Choice of format

Shares can be minted onchain to any allowlist address that you control. Not ready for tokenized shares? Shares can be held in standard book-entry form, and you can choose to mint onchain later.

Portable shares

Migrate supported public equities from your brokerage account into Superstate and mint them onchain — or bridge shares back to a brokerage account.

Robust primary liquidity

Purchase or redeem tokenized funds using USDC or bank wire. Shares are issued, or proceeds disbursed, by the next business day — with onchain liquidity available for instant redemption where available.

DeFi liquidity

Connect an allowlisted wallet to access liquidity through supported decentralized exchanges (DEXs) and lending protocols.

Powerful portfolio management

Unified portfolio

See a comprehensive view of all Superstate holdings across investing entities, allowlisted addresses, and integrated DeFi protocols — all in one place.

Take action

Initiate and manage transactions directly from the portal, including share redemptions, tokenizations, and other portfolio activities.

Track transactions

Monitor pending and completed transactions — including purchases, redemptions, transfers, mints, and bridge activity — with full transparency.

Subaccounts

Isolate balances, routing, and reporting across multiple subaccounts within a single entity, all without duplicating KYC or onboarding.

Institutional recordkeeping

Download official statements, fund materials, and key documentation whenever needed.
