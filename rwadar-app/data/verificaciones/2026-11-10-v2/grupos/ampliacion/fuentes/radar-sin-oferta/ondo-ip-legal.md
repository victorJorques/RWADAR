# Legal & Regulatory

> The legal structure of Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens.

Ondo Intelligent Portfolio tokens are structured notes issued by Ondo Global Markets (BVI) Limited (known as the "Issuer"), a bankruptcy-remote special purpose vehicle (SPV) organized in the British Virgin Islands (BVI) — the same issuer as Ondo tokenized stocks. [Learn more](https://docs.ondo.finance/ondo-stocks/legal-and-regulatory.md)
