BENJI is the World’s First U.S.-Registered Money Market Fund, Onchain.

We tokenized a traditional money market fund to leverage the efficiencies of blockchain technology for mutual fund record keeping, known as the Franklin OnChain U.S. Government Money Fund (FOBXX).

Explore Benji Contracts
1 share of FOBXX = 1 BENJI token

The Fund’s transfer agent maintains the official record of share ownership via a proprietary blockchain-integrated system, known as the Benji® platform, that currently utilizes public blockchain networks for transaction activity.

Invest using the Benji mobile app, available for Android and iOS

Shareholders can obtain or view account information and can conduct all transactions via the Benji mobile app.

Peer-to-Peer Transfers

Shareholders can transfer BENJI tokens directly to other shareholders on enabled public blockchains.

Daily Yield Accrual & Distribution

BENJI accrues yield daily through newly minted BENJI tokens that are airdropped directly into shareholders wallets.

Multi-Network Availability

BENJI is available on multiple public blockchains across the ecosystem.

Natively OnChain

All transaction records and share ownership are directly executed and stored on public blockchains.
