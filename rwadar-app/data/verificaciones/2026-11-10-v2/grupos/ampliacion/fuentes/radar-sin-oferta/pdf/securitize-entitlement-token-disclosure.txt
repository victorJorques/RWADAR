This Entitlement Token Disclosure Document (this “Disclosure Document”) is incorporated by reference

2. Defined Terms

    ● Convertible Entitlement Token (“CET”) means a digital token that represents a security
      entitlement, within the meaning of Article 8 of the Uniform Commercial Code (“UCC”), to a
      Reference Security held in book-entry form by SM on your behalf, together with a right to convert
      that entitlement into direct registration of the Reference Security if and when an issuer-sponsored
      token program becomes available for that Reference Security, as further described in Section 4
      below.

    ● Entitlement Token (“ET”) means a digital token that represents a security entitlement to a
      Reference Security, credited to you on the books of SM or its affiliated transfer agent as described
      in Section 5 below, where the Reference Security is held by SM directly or in custody by a third-
      party custodian on behalf of SM.

    ● Issuer Sponsored Token (“IST”) means a digital token issued directly by or at the direction of the
      issuer of the underlying security, representing the issuer’s own shares in a tokenized, digitally
      native form.

    ● Reference Security means the underlying security to which a CET or ET relates.

3. General Risks Applicable to CETs and ETs
The following risks apply generally to both CETs and ETs. Product-specific risks are described in Sections
4 and 5 below.

    3.1 No Guarantee of Value. The value of a CET or ET is derived from the value of the underlying
    Reference Security. An investment in a CET or ET may lose value, including total loss of principal, in
    the same manner as a direct investment in the Reference Security.

    3.2 NAV Timing Risk. Where a Reference Security is valued by reference to a net asset value (“NAV”)
    published periodically (for example, daily) rather than a continuously quoted market price, the number
    of CETs or ETs, or the redemption proceeds, you receive in connection with an order will be
    determined by the NAV published following your order, not the NAV in effect at the time you submit
    your order. Any estimate shown to you at the point of order entry is illustrative only and your actual
    position may differ once the applicable NAV is published.
    3.3 Liquidity Risk. CETs and ETs may not have a continuous public trading market. To the extent a
    secondary market exists for a CET or ET, it currently consists solely of trading conducted on SM's own
    alternative trading system or execution venue; there is no independent, unaffiliated secondary market
    for CETs or ETs, and no market maker or other party is obligated to make a market in, or otherwise
    provide liquidity for, your CET or ET position. As a result, you may not be able to sell your position at
    any particular time, in any particular size, or at a price that reflects the then-current NAV or public
    market price of the Reference Security, and the bid-ask spread or other execution cost on any available
    secondary market may be wider than what is typically available for the Reference Security itself, where
    the Reference Security trades on a national securities exchange. Where the Reference Security is itself
    subject to periodic or restricted redemption (for example, an interval fund with limited redemption
    windows), that restriction may further limit SM's ability to process your order, including a sale on any
    available secondary market, on your requested timeline. SM intends to expand the venues on which
    CETs and ETs may be traded over time, including through additional alternative trading systems,
    subject to receipt of all necessary regulatory approvals, including from the SEC. No assurance can be
    given as to whether or when any such additional venue will become available, and until it does, the
    limitations on secondary-market liquidity described above will continue to apply

    3.4 Technology and Custody Risk. CETs and ETs are recorded and transferred using blockchain-based
    technology and are subject to the risks described in the Terms, including smart contract risk, network
    risk, and the risks associated with self-custody wallets, as applicable.

    3.5 Issuer and Fund-Specific Risk. You should review the applicable issuer or fund disclosure
    documents for the Reference Security, including any prospectus or offering memorandum, before
    investing. Those documents may describe risks specific to the Reference Security that are not repeated
    in this Disclosure Document.

    3.6 System Availability Risk. SM's platform, including its order-entry, wallet-linking, and account
    interfaces, may from time to time be unavailable or degraded due to maintenance, technical failure, or

4. Convertible Entitlement Tokens (CETs)
    4.1 Structure. A CET represents a security entitlement to the Reference Security, held in book-entry
    form by SM on your behalf and credited to you specifically on SM's books, separate from SM's
    proprietary holdings. Because SM holds an underlying share specifically referable to your CET
    position, your CET position is expected to be treated as a customer securities position for purposes of
    SIPC coverage, subject to SIPC's rules, coverage limits, and eligibility requirements. As the holder of a
    security entitlement to the Reference Security, you are the beneficial owner of the Reference Security
    underlying your CET position.

    4.2 Restriction on Transfer and Conversion. The Reference Security underlying your CET will be
    restricted from trading or transfer while the CET is outstanding. If an issuer later establishes an issuer-
    sponsored token program and identifies SM as an authorized sponsor for that Reference Security, your
    outstanding CET may convert into an issuer-sponsored token, as described in the applicable issuer
    materials. Before placing an order for a CET, you will be shown a disclosure at the point of order entry
    identifying the instrument as a CET and distinguishing it from an issuer-sponsored token, which you
    must acknowledge before your order can be submitted. You may also separately request a book-entry
    conversion and transfer to another broker-dealer as described in Section 4.6 below.
    4.3 Access. Access to CETs requires successful completion of SM's full Know Your Customer identity
    verification and Anti-Money Laundering screening, which may be in addition to the account-level
    verification otherwise required to use the Platform. CETs may not be available in all jurisdictions or to
    all Users.

    4.5 No Cash Redemption. A CET is not redeemable for cash. You may realize the value of your CET
    position only by (a) selling the CET on the Platform, subject to Section 3.3 above, or (b) converting the
    CET as described in Section 4.2 or Section 4.6 below.

    4.6 Book-Entry Conversion and Transfer to Another Broker-Dealer. In addition to the conversion right
    described in Section 4.2 above, you may separately request that SM convert your CET position into
    directly registered book-entry shares of the Reference Security and transfer those shares, through the
    Direct Registration System ("DRS"), to another broker-dealer that is a member of DTCC and agrees to
    accept the position. This right is available regardless of whether an issuer-sponsored token program is
    available for the Reference Security. SM will process such a request in accordance with its then-current
    procedures, which may include additional processing time and fees as described in Section 7 below.

5. Entitlement Tokens (ETs)
    5.1 Structure. An ET represents a security entitlement to the Reference Security. SM, or its affiliated
    transfer agent acting as registrar on SM's behalf, credits a specific position to you on its own books,
    separate from SM's proprietary holdings, and reconciled against the Reference Securities held by SM
    directly or through a third-party custodian for the benefit of SM. Unlike a CET holder, you are not the
    beneficial owner of the Reference Security underlying your ET position. Your ET represents a security
    entitlement giving you the economic rights described in this Disclosure Document, but does not confer
    beneficial ownership of the Reference Security itself.

    5.2 No Conversion Right. Unlike a CET, an ET does not convert into direct registration of the
    Reference Security in your name. SM redeems an ET for cash at a price matched to the then-current
    NAV or market price of the Reference Security, subject to Section 3.2 above, and then provides you
    with an equivalent amount in USDC.

    5.3 Registration Status of the Reference Security. Because a Reference Security underlying an ET is
    registered under the Securities Act of 1933 and is not offered or sold in reliance on a private placement
    exemption, the risk disclosures applicable to unregistered securities offered on the Securitize Markets
    ATS do not describe the Reference Security underlying an ET. Liquidity and secondary-market risk
    specific to ETs are instead described in Section 3.3 above.

    5.4 No Relationship to Issuer-Sponsored Tokens. ETs do not convert into an Issuer Sponsored Token
    or into a CET and are not affected by an issuer's later adoption of an issuer-sponsored token program
    with respect to the Reference Security.

    5.5 Access. Access to ETs requires successful completion of SM's full Know Your Customer identity
    verification and Anti-Money Laundering screening, which may be in addition to the account-level
    verification otherwise required to use the Platform. ETs may not be available in all jurisdictions or to
    all Users.

    6.2 Use of Funds — CETs. Funds you submit in connection with a CET order, net of any applicable

    underlying share held for your benefit, at a price matched to the trading day’s published NAV or
    market price, as applicable, and are not available to SM for general corporate or working-capital

    benefit of CET holders to fund proprietary trading activity unrelated to those holders' positions.

    6.3 Use of Funds — ETs. Funds you submit in connection with an ET order, net of any applicable fees
    charged pursuant to Section 7 below, are used solely to fund the corresponding purchase of the
    Reference Security executed to fulfill your order, at a price matched to the trading day’s published
    NAV or market price, as applicable, and are not available to SM for general corporate or working-
    capital purposes prior to that purchase. Applicable fees are deducted from the funds you submit prior to
    that purchase and are retained by SM as compensation for its services, and SM may use such fees for its
    general corporate or working-capital purposes upon deduction. SM does not use Reference Securities
    held to fulfill ET orders to fund separate proprietary trading activity unrelated to outstanding ET
    orders.

    6.4 Minimum Order Size. The minimum order size for a CET or ET purchase is typically 1 USDC, but
    may vary by product. SM may change this minimum from time to time; the current minimum will be
    reflected at the point of order entry.

    6.5 Wallet Whitelisting and On-Chain Transfers. Your wallet must first be added to an allow-list
    associated with your Account, which requires you to complete SM's Know Your Customer identity

    6.6 Jurisdiction-Specific Restrictions. SM may impose additional or different restrictions on the

8. Corporate Actions
     8.1 General Principles.

        (a) Pass-through of economics. Subject to this Section 8, SM intends to pass through to you the
        economic Entitlements attaching to securities and Reference Securities held in your Account, net of
        any fees, taxes, or adjustments disclosed in Section 7 above.

        (b) Reliance on third parties. SM processes Corporate Actions based on information received from
        issuers, their agents, the Depository Trust Company (“DTC”), and other custodians, clearing
        agencies, and data providers. SM is not the source of this information and does not independently
        verify its accuracy or completeness before acting on it.

        (c) No guarantee of timing. The timing of SM's processing of a Corporate Action, including
        crediting of cash or securities to your Account, will generally follow the timing established by the
        issuer, its agents, or DTC, but may be delayed by SM's own operational and reconciliation
        processes, or by the timing of the underlying transfer agent's or issuer's processing.

        (d) Discretion absent instructions. For Voluntary Corporate Actions (as defined below), if SM does
        not receive your election by the applicable Election Deadline, SM may, in its sole discretion, take
        no action, allow the offer to lapse with respect to your position, or take such other action as it deems
        appropriate on your behalf. SM's typical practice absent instructions is to take no action, such that
        the offer lapses and you retain your existing position unchanged, but SM is not obligated to follow
        that practice in every case.

        (e) No investment advice. SM's notification of a Corporate Action, and any information SM
        provides about it, is for informational purposes only and does not constitute investment, tax, or legal
        advice or a recommendation as to how you should respond.

     8.2 Mandatory and Voluntary Corporate Actions.

     A “Mandatory Corporate Action” (such as a cash dividend, stock split, or mandatory merger exchange)
     is processed automatically, without requiring instructions from you. A “Voluntary Corporate Action”
     (such as a tender offer, exchange offer, or rights offering) requires an affirmative election. Because SM
     must aggregate and submit elections on behalf of multiple accountholders through DTC or other
     intermediaries, SM's internal election deadline (an “Election Deadline”) will in most cases be earlier
     than the deadline set by the issuer or DTC, and instructions received after SM's Election Deadline may
     not be processed even if received before the issuer's or DTC's own deadline. SM is under no obligation
     to exercise any right, option, or election on your behalf in the absence of timely instructions from you.

     8.3 Fractional Entitlements; Cash Distributions; Dividend Reinvestment.

     Where a Corporate Action would otherwise result in a fractional share or fractional unit being credited
     to your Account, you may receive cash in lieu of the share should the resulting share not meet SM’s
     default minimum.

     Cash dividends and other cash distributions will be paid to you in SM's designated settlement
     stablecoin, consistent with the Terms. A Corporate Action processing fee may be applied and should
the cash dividend or distribution be less than the processing fee, no payment will be made. For a
complete fee schedule, visit: https://securitize.io/fee-schedule-table.

8.5 Processing Model: Issuer Sponsored Tokens, CETs, and ETs.

(a) Issuer Sponsored Tokens. Where Securities and Other Assets are held as Issuer Sponsored Tokens,
Corporate Actions are processed directly against the token in accordance with the issuer's and its
transfer agent's procedures, because the IST itself represents the underlying share.

(b) CETs. Where Securities and Other Assets are held as CETs, Corporate Actions are processed
against the underlying book-entry shares held by SM, and the resulting adjustment is reflected in your
CET position or Account balance by SM or its affiliated transfer agent. You acknowledge that timing
differences may exist between when a Corporate Action is effected against the underlying shares and
when the corresponding adjustment is reflected in your CET position, and SM shall have no liability for
such timing differences to the extent they result from the transfer agent's or issuer's processing timeline.

(c) ETs. Where Securities and Other Assets are held as ETs, Corporate Actions are processed against
the Reference Securities held by SM directly or through a third-party custodian, and the resulting
adjustment is reflected in your ET position, credited on the books of SM or its affiliated transfer agent
as described in Section 5 above, on a pass-through basis reflecting the economic terms of the Corporate
Action. You acknowledge that timing differences may exist between when a Corporate Action is
effected against the Reference Security and when a corresponding adjustment is reflected in your ET
position, and SM shall have no liability for such timing differences to the extent they result from the
transfer agent's, custodian's, or issuer's processing timeline.

position is not affected by an issuer's adoption of an IST program with respect to the Reference
Security.
