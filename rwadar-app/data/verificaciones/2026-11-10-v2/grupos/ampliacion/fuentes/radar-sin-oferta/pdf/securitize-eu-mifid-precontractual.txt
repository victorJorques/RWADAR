1.​ About Securitize Europe

1.1.​   For any EU-based investors, investment and ancillary services are offered by
        Securitize Europe Brokerage and Markets, S.V., S.A. (“Securitize Europe”).

1.2.​   Securitize Europe is a Spanish investment firm authorized and supervised by the
        Spanish Securities Market Commission, the Comisión Nacional del Mercado de
        Valores (“CNMV”), under registration number 327. The CNMV can be contacted at
        Edison 4, 28006 Madrid or via their website www.cnmv.es or phone (+34 900 535
        015).

1.3.​   Securitize Europe is registered with the Commercial Registry of Madrid at Volume
        45141, Section 8, Sheet 81, Page M-794351, and holds the Tax Identification Number
        A13691464. Securitize Europe’s registered office is at Calle Don Ramón de la Cruz
        38, 28001 Madrid, Spain.

1.4.​   Securitize Europe is authorized to provide the following investment services as
        defined in Annex I, Section A of MiFID II:

        1.4.1.​ Reception and transmission of orders in relation to one or more financial
                instruments;
        1.4.2.​ Execution of orders on behalf of clients;
        1.4.3.​ Placing of financial instruments without a firm commitment basis; and
        1.4.4.​ Operation of an MTF.

1.5.​   Securitize Europe is authorized to provide the following ancillary services, as defined
        in Annex I, Section B of MiFID II:

        1.5.1.​ Safekeeping and administration of financial instruments for the account of
                clients;

        1.5.2.​ Granting credits or loans to an investor to allow him to carry out a transaction
                in one or more financial instruments, where the firm granting the credit or loan
                is involved in the transaction; and

        1.5.3.​ Advice to undertakings on capital structure, industrial strategy and related
                matters and advice and services relating to mergers and the purchase of
                undertakings.

2.​ Client Classification

2.1.​   Securitize Europe will notify Clients of their classification as retail clients, professional
        clients, or eligible counterparties at the time of onboarding:

        2.1.1.​ Retail clients receive the highest level of protection under MiFID II, which
                includes thorough suitability and appropriateness assessments, as well as
                comprehensive pre- and post-contractual documentation and information that
                must be made available to them.

        2.1.2.​ Professional clients, such as institutional investors, large corporations, and
                regional public entities, are afforded an intermediate level of protection. This is
                based on the assumption that they possess sufficient knowledge of financial
                markets to make their own investment decisions and to understand and
                accept the associated risks.

        2.1.3.​ Eligible   counterparties,   including   credit   institutions,   investment   firms,
                investment and pension funds, central banks, national governments, and their
                respective agencies, are granted the minimum level of protection. This reflects
                their nature as entities required to act normally in financial markets.

2.2.​   The Client has a right to request a different client classification subject to written
        confirmation from Securitize Europe to the Client. Securitize Europe will consider any
        requests received on a case-by-case basis against the criteria set out in the
        applicable regulations, but it is not obliged to agree to such a request and may
        decline to act.

3.1.​   Securitize Europe will assess whether the Financial Instruments offered under the
        RTO Service are appropriate for the Client. This involves determining whether the
        Client possesses the necessary experience and knowledge to understand the risks
        associated with the Financial Instrument being offered or requested.

3.2.​   Securitize Europe will be entitled to assume that a Client that has been classified as a

        understand the risks involved in relation to the particular types of Financial
        Instruments for which the Client is classified as a professional client.

        the Client to receive the RTO Service in respect of a Financial Instrument unless that

3.5.​   Where the Client does not provide the information requested on their knowledge and
        experience, or where they provide insufficient information regarding their knowledge
        and experience, Securitize Europe will warn the Client that Securitize Europe is not in
        a position to determine whether the Financial Instrument envisaged is appropriate for
        them. Securitize Europe will not allow the Client to receive the RTO Service in respect
        of a Financial Instrument unless that Financial Instrument has been deemed
        appropriate for the Client.

3.6.​   Securitize Europe will be entitled to rely on the information provided by the Client. The

        such information will remain complete and accurate in all material respects on an

3.7.​   Securitize Europe will provide the Client with the results of any appropriateness
        assessment carried out for the Client in connection with any Financial Instruments.

7.2.​   Securitize Europe may make payments to third-parties which enable or are necessary
        for the provision of the RTO Service to the Client including, without limitation, in

        not be considered as inducements as defined in MiFID II and will be disclosed to the
        Client in accordance with MiFID II and the applicable regulations.

7.3.​   Unless otherwise notified to the Client in writing, Securitize Europe will not charge to
        the Client any research costs in respect of any research provided by third-parties but
        will pay for any such research out of Securitize Europe’s own funds, and such
        payments shall not be treated as inducements.

8.​ Investor Compensation Scheme

8.1.​   Securitize Europe is a member of the Spanish Financial Services Compensation
        Scheme (“FOGAIN”). FOGAIN provides compensation to investors with member
        entities in certain cases of insolvency of the entity offering investment services, for the
        money and securities deposited with or entrusted to the entity.

8.2.​   FOGAIN guarantees Retail Clients of Securitize Europe the recovery of cash and
        Financial Instruments delivered to Securitize Europe and which it holds, administers,
        or manages on behalf of the Retail Client, up to a maximum of EUR 100,000 per
        Retail Client, when Securitize Europe cannot return them due to any of the following
        circumstances:

        8.2.1.​   Insolvency proceedings, such as “Concurso de acreedores”.

10.​Conflicts of Interest

10.1.​ Securitize Europe has a Conflicts of Interest Policy and specific procedures designed
        to prevent, identify, record, manage, and, where avoidance is not possible, inform its
        clients about potential conflict of interest situations that may be detrimental to them.
        The Conflicts of Interest Policy and its related procedures aim to:

        10.1.1.​ Identify relationships, services, activities or operations where conflicts of
                  interest may arise.

        10.1.2.​ Facilitate the implementation of measures to prevent conflicts of interest
                  wherever possible.

        10.1.3.​ Establish a process to identify and assess the significance of conflicts,
                  determine the steps to mitigate them, and communicate any relevant (actual
                  or potential) conflict of interest.

        10.1.4.​ Set out how to manage an unavoidable conflict of interest and how to inform
                  the affected individual or entity.

        10.1.5.​ Properly document the application of measures in place to address the
                  above objectives, so that they can be monitored internally and explained to
                  regulators.

10.2.​ To request further information about the Conflicts of Interest Policy, you may use any
        of the communication channels with Securitize Europe mentioned above.
