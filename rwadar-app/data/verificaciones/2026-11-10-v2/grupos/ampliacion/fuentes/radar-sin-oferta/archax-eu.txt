FULL MIFID II INVESTMENT FIRM

Archax Markets Europe is authorised as a Sociedad de Valores (investment firm) by the CNMV in Spain, with MiFID II passporting rights across most of the EU member states. This is a full securities licence, not a limited crypto-only authorisation.

PART OF THE ARCHAX GROUP

Archax Markets Europe benefits from the technology, expertise, and institutional relationships of the wider Archax Group, which includes FCA-regulated operations in the UK.

PAN-EUROPEAN REACH

A single authorisation in Spain provides passported access to professional and institutional clients throughout the entire European Economic Area.
