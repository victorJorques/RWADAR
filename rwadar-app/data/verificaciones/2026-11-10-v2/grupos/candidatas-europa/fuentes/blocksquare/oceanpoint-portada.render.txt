LEARN THE BASICS

What is tokenized real estate?

A real property is legally structured on-chain and divided into tokens. Each token is backed by the real-world asset, and anyone can own one.

STEP 1
One real property

A building with verified legal ownership is prepared for tokenization on Blocksquare's audited contracts.

STEP 2

The property is issued as BSPTs: on-chain tokens representing fractional ownership, each backed by the asset.

TOKENIZE

Own a property? Bring it on-chain.

Your building holds value that is hard to reach: selling takes months and loans cost interest. Tokenization turns that equity into digital property tokens investors can buy, without giving up the asset.

Unlock capital, keep the keys
Sell fractions of your property to investors instead of taking on bank debt or selling the whole building. You decide how much equity to offer.
Reach investors everywhere
Tokenized properties are open to a global pool of investors from any amount, so your asset is no longer limited to local buyers and long sales cycles.
Stay in control of your asset
You keep operating the property exactly as before. Investors hold tokens, and ownership records live transparently on-chain.
