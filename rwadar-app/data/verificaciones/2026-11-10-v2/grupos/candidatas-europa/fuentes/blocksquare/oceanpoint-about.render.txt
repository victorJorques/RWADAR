About Oceanpoint

The liquidity engine for tokenized real estate, built by the core team of Blocksquare.

Who we are

Oceanpoint is the liquidity engine for tokenized real estate: owners sell a slice of a building as property tokens and keep the rest, and a buyer funded by the buildings' rent stands ready to buy. Property tokens trade on the Oceanpoint marketplace today; the liquidity engine, built around POINT, is next.

Oceanpoint is a Luxembourg company, registered as Blocksquare S.à r.l. and being renamed Oceanpoint S.à r.l. It is built by the core team of Blocksquare, a real estate tokenization pioneer since 2018, as a new venture running on established rails.

Oceanpoint and Blocksquare

Oceanpoint: the marketplace, the liquidity engine and its treasury, and the Oceanpoint dApp. app.oceanpoint.fi

Blocksquare d.o.o. (Ljubljana, Slovenia): a separate, related company that develops the tokenization infrastructure and licenses it to Oceanpoint. blocksquare.io

Oceanpoint and Blocksquare share founders, so they are related parties. Figures for the wider Blocksquare network, such as all properties tokenized on Blocksquare-powered marketplaces, are Blocksquare's, and this site labels them that way.

BST ecosystem

Oceanpoint's dApp also hosts the staking and governance pools of the Blocksquare ecosystem, built around BST, Blocksquare's protocol token:

2018: BST (Blocksquare Token) deployed on Ethereum: 100,000,000 total supply.
February 2022: the first governance pool goes live: stake BST, receive sBST.
2022–2024: asset pools open real estate staking; liquidity pools bootstrap BST:ETH depth.
April 2025: the Oceanpoint whitepaper ([nombre recortado]) sets out POINT, a liquidity token for tokenized real estate, rolling out in phases.

sBST holders vote on proposals for these pools via Snapshot. Read the whitepaper →
