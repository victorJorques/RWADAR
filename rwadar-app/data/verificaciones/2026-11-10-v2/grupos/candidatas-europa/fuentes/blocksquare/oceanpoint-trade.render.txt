PROPERTY TOKENS

What is a property token?

BSPTs are on-chain tokens representing fractional ownership in real-world properties.

On-chain ownership

Each BSPT is tied to a specific property and backed by a legal ownership structure.

Rental income

Holding BSPTs makes you eligible for rental income distributions from the property.

Resell any time

Sell your BSPTs on the KYC-gated Oceanpoint marketplace; a standing buyer is coming with POINT.
