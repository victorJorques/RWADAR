Overview

This disclaimer contains important information about the risks associated with using the Oceanpoint protocol. By using the protocol, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this disclaimer in full.

Oceanpoint is a decentralized protocol that people can use to earn BST by performing various actions that may involve the use of various ERC-20 tokens. The Oceanpoint protocol is made up of free, public, open-source or source-available software, including a set of smart contracts that are deployed on the Ethereum Blockchain.

Your use of the Oceanpoint protocol involves various risks, including, but not limited to, losses while digital assets are being supplied to the Oceanpoint protocol and losses due to the fluctuation of prices of tokens in a pool. Before using the Oceanpoint protocol, you should review the relevant documentation to make sure you understand how the Oceanpoint protocol works.

Additionally, just as you can access email protocols such as SMTP through multiple email clients, you can access the Oceanpoint protocol through dozens of web or mobile interfaces. You are responsible for doing your own diligence on those interfaces to understand the fees and risks they present.

Governance

Although Blocksquare d.o.o. ("Blocksquare") developed much of the initial code for the Oceanpoint protocol, it does not provide, own, or control the Oceanpoint protocol, which is run by smart contracts deployed on the Ethereum blockchain.

Upgrades and modifications to the protocol are managed in a community-driven way by holders of the sBST governance token. No single party, including Blocksquare, has unilateral control over the protocol.
