Tokenization protocol
Digitize the value and revenues of any real estate property.

With our real estate tokenization protocol entrepreneurs can start digitising real estate assets at a fraction of the cost. Built on Ethereum and IPFS, any single real estate property can be converted into 100,000 tokens, either partially or in full, providing investors a transparent and standardised digitalisation process. What's more, it's applicable to any common legal ownership form.
