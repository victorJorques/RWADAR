METHODOLOGY

Where these numbers come from
On-chain figures

Tokenized value, property and country counts, BST holders, staked BST, and pool yields are read from the Blocksquare protocol contracts on Ethereum mainnet via the open-source Blocksquare indexer. Yields are trailing ten-day annualized rates. Anyone can verify every figure independently on-chain.

Marketplace figures

Rental income distributed, average projected yield, and marketplace counts come from the protocol's public marketplace API, aggregated across all Blocksquare-powered marketplaces. A JSON version of the on-chain figures is available at /api/protocol/stats.

Citation welcome: “Source: Oceanpoint statistics (https://oceanpoint.fi/stats)”. Cite Blocksquare network figures as Blocksquare's. Figures refresh every five minutes; historical values are verifiable on-chain. Educational content, not financial advice.
