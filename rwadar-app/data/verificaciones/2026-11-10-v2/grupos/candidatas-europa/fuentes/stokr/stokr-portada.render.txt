WHAT IS ASSET TOKENIZATION?
Asset tokenization refers to the process of converting the legal rights of real-world assets (equities, bonds, or revenue) into digital tokens issued on Distributed Ledger Technology (DLT). These security tokens or digital securities differ fundamentally from cryptocurrencies like Bitcoin (BTC) or Ethereum (ETH): they are backed by tangible assets and represent enforceable legal claims, making them attractive to financial professionals, family offices, and institutional investors seeking compliant and transparent exposure to digital assets.
