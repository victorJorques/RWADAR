Deposits and Withdrawals
The deposit and withdrawal process is the same across all Spiko products (T-Bills, Smart Cash, and Cash & Carry). You manage all operations from the Spiko platform.
Deposits ​
For each of our clients, each account and share class has a dedicated virtual IBAN (vIBAN), accessible directly from your interface or the API. Once your transfer is received on this vIBAN, reconciliation is done automatically and you receive a real-time notification.

Withdrawals ​
Spiko provides two withdrawal modalities: standard withdrawal and instant withdrawal .
WITHDRAWAL SECURITY
For security purposes, withdrawal bank accounts must meet strict ownership criteria:
Individual Accounts : Account holder name must match the Spiko account holder
Corporate Accounts : Account must be in the legal entity's name
Withdrawals to non-matching accounts will be rejected. Bank account ownership verification is mandatory.
Standard Withdrawals ​
Standard withdrawal is the default option. It can be used by all clients. Cutoff times and settlement timelines depend on the product and currency - refer to the relevant product section under Our products for details.
Instant Withdrawals (EUR only) ​
The instant withdrawal feature allows you to withdraw your funds from Spiko Euro and Spiko T-Bills Euro and receive them in your bank account within minutes. This feature uses SEPA instant credit transfer and is available 24/7 .
Instant transfers are supported for amounts up to €500,000 per day . Any amount above €500,000 per day is processed as a standard withdrawal, with timing depending on the product.
To make an instant withdrawal, simply make a withdrawal from an EUR-denominated product and select the "Instant Withdrawal" option. Please feel free to email us at support@spiko.io if you have any questions.
info
The instant withdrawal service is provided to you free of charge, Spiko covers all the costs related to instant withdrawals. For more details about how instant withdrawal works, please refer to Article 12 of our Terms of Use or contact us at support@spiko.io .
