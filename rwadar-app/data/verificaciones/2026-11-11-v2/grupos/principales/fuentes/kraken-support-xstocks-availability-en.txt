What stocks and ETFs are available as xStocks?
Last updated: June 18, 2026
We offer many of the most popular tokenized stocks and ETFs, including names like Tesla (TSLAx), Apple (AAPLx), and GameStop (GMEx), as well as select tokenized IPO opportunities such as SpaceX.
xStocks and ETFs available on Kraken
xStocks and ETFs available for margin trading
The following xStocks and ETFs can be traded using 3x leverage:*
AAPLx, CRCLx, GLDx, GOOGLx, HOODx, MSTRx, NVDAx, QQQx, SPYx, and TSLAx
*Geographic restrictions apply
xStocks are not available in: United States, Australia, Canada, UK and Prohibited regions.
Requirements for EEA clients
If you are based in the EEA, you must complete and pass a questionnaire before gaining access to xStocks trading.
Once approved, EEA clients can buy, sell, and convert xStocks using fiat only through kraken.com. xStocks trading via the order books is not available for EEA clients using Kraken Pro or the Kraken API and only available via the Kraken Pro “Convert” feature.
Availability for other regions
For clients outside of the EEA, you can buy, sell, and convert xStocks using both crypto and fiat (cash).
