Individuals
Our streamlined account creation process is fully automated for most applicants. We welcome clients from most jurisdictions, with a few exceptions based on regulatory requirements. Please check our geographical restrictions before proceeding.
PROCESSING TIME
Standard applications are typically processed and validated within 3 minutes.
Account Creation Steps​
1. Security Setup​
The first step involves verifying your email, choosing your password, and setting up two-factor authentication, which will be necessary for sensitive operations on the platform such as withdrawals or changing bank accounts.
2. Investment Profile​
French law requires us to assess your financial experience and savings objectives. Upon account validation, you will receive personalized investment advice based on your responses.
3. Identity Verification​
You will need to scan an ID document and complete a biometric verification step. We work with Onfido, a leading provider for online identification. We accept the following types of ID documents:
Passports
National ID cards
4. Address Verification​
Depending on your situation, we may need to ask you for proof of address. We accept the following documents:
Recent bank statements
Utility bills (electricity, water, gas)
Telecommunications bills (including mobile)
Tax documentation:
Assessment notice
Non-taxation certificate
Housing-related documents:
Tax certificate
Insurance policy/bill
CAF housing benefit statement
