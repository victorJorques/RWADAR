# Midas

> Midas brings institutional-grade investment strategies onchain. Access tokenised assets with full transparency, instant liquidity and native composability across DeFi.

Midas issues mTokens: permissionless, yield-bearing tokens that give onchain access to institutional-grade investment strategies — US Treasuries, market-neutral DeFi yield, private credit, reinsurance, and BTC/ETH/XRP strategies. Each strategy is run by an appointed Strategy Manager, with transparent onchain reporting (NAV, proof of reserves, price updates) and instant liquidity.
