November 11, 2026 xStocks Brings 200+ of the UK and EU’s Largest Companies Onchain xStocks launches 200+ tokenized UK and EU equities, including every DAX 40 company, supported by GTN and Deutsche Börse, with more regional markets to follow. The first non-US regional markets are live on xStocks, with more to follow. xStocks has launched its first regional markets, bringing tokenized equities for 200+ of the largest UK and EU-listed companies, and every constituent of the DAX 40. A few examples available to access include tokenized versions of Rolls-Royce (RR.GBx), HSBC (HSBAx), Airbus (AIR.FRx), LVMH (MC.FRx) and SAP (SAPx). Each xStock is backed 1:1 by the underlying share but represents economic exposure only, not direct ownership. How to access UK and EU-listed xStocks This new batch of xStocks are available on Kraken, Kraken Pro, participating xStocks Alliance partners and 10+ blockchains. Trades run through RFQ and the market flow, and trading follows local market hours at launch. xStocks are not available to investors in the UK or the US. Geo restrictions apply. Built with GTN and Deutsche Börse GTN provides the markets. Its brokerage and custody infrastructure handles execution and custody of the underlying UK and EU shares through the Payward Services partnership, which is how xStocks can bring non US-listed markets onchain. Deutsche Börse Group adds to that. Building on its partnership with Payward, every DAX 40 constituent is tokenized, and the DAX 40 xStocks are available on 360X, the Deutsche Börse-backed venue regulated under the EU’s DLT Pilot Regime.
