The LENDS token, demystified
LENDS are not crypto coins - they are registered shares of Switzerlend AG under Swiss law (Art. 973d CO ledger-based securities), issued as tokens on Ethereum via Aktionariat. One token, one share, full shareholder rights.

Follow the setup instructions and write down the 24 words of your recovery phrase on paper – they are your only key.
3

Log in and invest
On your computer, go to shares.lend.ch, log in to Aktionariat using the QR code, and open the direct investment page.

Know the risks.
LENDS trade on a non-supervised venue without the protections of a regulated exchange. Liquidity is limited; you may not be able to sell at the displayed price or at all. Equity can lose value, including entirely. Past platform performance does not predict share performance. This page is informational and not investment advice or a prospectus.

FAQ
Are LENDS tokens real shares?
Yes. Each LENDS token is a registered share of Switzerlend AG, issued as a ledger-based security under Art. 973d of the Swiss Code of Obligations. Token holders who register are entered in the share register with full voting and dividend rights.
Can I sell my tokens later?
You can sell back to the brokerbot when it holds sufficient liquidity, or peer-to-peer on shares.lend.ch. Liquidity is limited compared to a stock exchange - treat this as a long-term holding.
Is this regulated?
Switzerlend AG is a Swiss AG and an approved financial intermediary (SRO Polyreg). The trading venue itself is not a supervised exchange; the risk box above explains what that means.
Does LEND pay dividends?
Dividend policy is decided by the AGM. As a growth company, profits have so far been reinvested; any change will be announced in the shareholder letter.
How are the tokens taxed in Switzerland?
For Swiss private investors, shares are generally subject to wealth tax and dividends to income tax; private capital gains are generally tax-free. This is not tax advice - check with your advisor.
Do I need crypto to invest?
No. You can buy directly from the issuer by bank transfer in CHF. The token simply lives in a wallet app instead of a bank depot.
