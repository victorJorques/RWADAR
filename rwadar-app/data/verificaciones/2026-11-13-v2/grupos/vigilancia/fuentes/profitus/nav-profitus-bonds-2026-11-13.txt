PARTNERS

We partner with Axiology, a capital markets technology provider, to distribute bonds.

Axiology – A capital markets infrastructure company based in Lithuania and supervised by the Bank of Lithuania, which develops next-generation solutions for securities issuance, accounting, and settlement. Axiology offers PROFITUS, a service that opens investment accounts for investors, in which the bonds they have purchased are registered. This ensures secure, transparent, and regulated accounting of investors’ securities. Axiology is led by its founder, [nombre recortado], a former member of the Board of the Bank of Lithuania who previously worked as an economist at the central banks of England and Norway.

FUNDING PROCESS

Initial Consultation - We discuss your financing needs, the amount, the term, your business situation, and your goals.
Structuring of the Issuance - the proposed amount, term, interest rate, collateral, covenants, and key terms.
Preparation of Documents and Investor Materials - preparation of the information needed for investors to make a decision.
Distribution and Communication with Investors - The offering is presented to investors, applications are collected, and investors are kept informed of the progress and results.
Coupon Payments – Coupon payments from the issuance are made to investors.
